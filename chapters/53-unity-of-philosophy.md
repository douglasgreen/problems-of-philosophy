Chapter 52 *measured* the discipline by its *record* — the *settled*, the *dissolved*, the *open*, the *mis*-*posed*. This chapter asks the *prior* question that the *record* *presupposes*: *is* the *philosophy* *one* *inquiry* or *many*, and *what* — *if* *anything* — *holds* the *parts* *together*?

The *transition* is the *inversion* of the *ledger*. The *ledger* *assumes* that the *entries* *belong* to the *single* *book* (the *no* *mere* *anthology*). The *unity* *question* *tests* the *assumption*: if the *philosophy* is the *mere* *federation* of the *neighboring* *projects* (the *no* *single* *subject*), then the *ledger* is the *statistics* of the *unrelated* (the *no* *single* *record*), and the *verdict* of Chapter 52 *must* be *stated* *per* *domain* (the *no* *single* *discipline*). The *chapter* is the *test* of the *book's* *thesis* (the *systematic* *analysis*; Ch. 1, §2) — and the *answer* *determines* whether the *book* is a *book* or the *collection* of the *chapters*.

The *chapter* proceeds in *eight* *movements*. §1 states the *question* and the *two* *theses* (the *unity*, the *disunity*). §2 examines the *interdependence* of the *domains*. §3 examines the *specialist's* *dilemma*. §4 examines the *relation* to the *sciences*. §5 examines the *relation* to the *humanities*. §6 examines the *philosophy* *of* *X* and the *X*-*ification*. §7 draws the *final* *map* and the *synthesis* *exercise*. §8 gives the *verdict*.

## 1. The Question of Unity

### 1.1 The structure of the question

**The *question***. The *unity* *question* (*is* the *philosophy* *one* *thing*?) is *ambiguous* between the *four* *senses* of the *unity*, and the *failure* to *distinguish* them *produces* the *rhetoric* (the *no* *single* *question*).

**The *four* *senses***.

| The *sense* | The *content* | The *test* |
|---|---|---|
| **The *object*** | The *one* *subject* *matter* (the *no* *mere* *family*) | The *identity* of the *questions* |
| **The *method*** | The *one* *instrument* (the *argument*, the *analysis*) | The *transfer* of the *technique* |
| **The *history*** | The *one* *tradition* (the *canon*, the *conversation*) | The *continuity* of the *reference* |
| **The *institution*** | The *one* *practice* (the *journals*, the *departments*) | The *sociological* *cohesion* |

**The *significance***. The *senses* *come* *apart*: the *philosophy* *may* have the *method* and *lack* the *object* (the *no* *single* *subject*), and it *may* have the *history* and *lack* the *institution* (the *no* *mere* *department*), and the *credential* *does* not *entail* the *competence* (the *no* *mere* *degree*), and the *verdict* *depends* on the *sense* (the *no* *single* *verdict*).

**The *criteria***. The *unity* is *assessed* by the *five* *markers*.

| The *marker* | The *content* | The *significance* |
|---|---|---|
| **The *cross*-*citation*** | The *reference* *across* the *domains* | The *no* *mere* *insulation* |
| **The *transfer*** | The *export* of the *results* (the *logic* → the *ethics*) | The *no* *mere* *isolation* |
| **The *shared* *problems*** | The *questions* *recur* (the *cause*, the *person*, the *norm*) | The *no* *mere* *coincidence* |
| **The *shared* *training*** | The *curriculum*, the *canon*, the *skills* | The *no* *mere* *apprenticeship* |
| **The *shared* *stakes*** | The *world* *hangs* on the *answers* (the *no* *mere* *game*) | The *no* *mere* *convention* |

**The *problem***. The *markers* *pull* *apart*: the *cross*-*citation* *favors* the *unity* (the *philosophy* *of* *mind* *cites* the *epistemology*), the *transfer* *favors* the *method* (the *logic* *transfers*; the *aesthetics* *does* not), the *shared* *problems* *favor* the *object* (the *cause* *recurs*; the *Gettier* *case* does *not*), and the *shared* *stakes* *favor* the *practice* (the *no* *mere* *antiquarianism*).

### 1.2 The unity thesis

**The *thesis*** (the *systematic* *insight*; Sellars 1962; the *contemporary* *literature*; the *contemporary* *literature*; the *book's* *own* *thesis*; Ch. 1, §2). The *philosophy* is the *one* *inquiry* (the *no* *mere* *family*), and the *unity* is the *method* *and* the *object* (the *no* *mere* *convention*), and the *object* is the *conditions* of *intelligibility* (the *no* *mere* *topic*), and the *method* is the *analysis* (the *argument*, the *distinction*, the *equilibrium*; Ch. 2–4), and the *parts* *are* the *aspects* of the *whole* (the *no* *mere* *aggregate*).

**The *structure*** (the *Sellars* *form*).

$$
\text{The philosophy} \ = \ \text{the synoptic account} \ (\text{the no mere specialism}) \ \wedge \ \text{the analysis} \ (\text{the no mere topic})
$$

**The *argument***.

$$
\begin{array}{ll}
1. & \text{The philosophy aims at the synoptic account} \ (\text{the no mere specialism}). \\
2. & \text{The synoptic account requires the analysis} \ (\text{the no mere summary}). \\
3. & \text{The analysis requires the whole} \ (\text{the no mere fragment}). \\
\hline
4. & \therefore \text{The philosophy is the one inquiry} \ (\text{the Sellars insight}), \ \text{and the parts are the aspects} \ (\text{the no mere} \ \text{aggregate}), \ \text{and the unity is the method and the object} \ (\text{the no mere} \ \text{institution}).
\end{array}
$$

**The *virtue***. The *thesis* *captures* the *ambition* (the *no* *mere* *specialism*), and it *fits* the *practice* (the *cross*-*citation*, the *transfer*), and it *grounds* the *book* (the *no* *mere* *anthology*), and it *connects* the *unity* to the *method* (Ch. 3) and to the *value* (Ch. 54).

**The *objections***.

**(i) The *no* *object* *objection***. The *domains* *have* the *no* *shared* *object* (the *no* *mere* *subject*), and the *philosophy* *of* *law* and the *philosophy* *of* *physics* *share* *nothing* (the *no* *mere* *transfer*), and the *reply* *fails*.

**The *reply*** (the *methodological* *insight*). The *shared* *object* is the *form* of the *question* (the *conditions* *of* *intelligibility*; the *no* *mere* *topic*), and the *reply* *grounds* the *unity* in the *form*. The *reply* is *defensible* (the *formal* *insight*), and *contested* (the *no* *content*).

**(ii) The *no* *method* *objection***. The *methods* *differ* (the *formal* *semantics*, the *phenomenology*, the *history*, the *intuition*), and the *no* *single* *instrument* (the *no* *mere* *unity*), and the *reply* *fails*.

**The *reply*** (the *contemporary* *literature*). The *methods* are the *aspects* of the *analysis* (the *no* *mere* *purity*), and the *reply* *grounds* the *unity* in the *family* *of* *techniques*. The *reply* is *defensible* (the *plural* *insight*), and *contested* (the *no* *method*).

**(iii) The *sociological* *objection***. The *unity* is the *sociological* *fact* (the *departments*, the *journals*), and the *fact* *does* not *ground* the *subject* (the *no* *mere* *discipline*), and the *reply* *fails*.

**The *reply*** (the *contemporary* *literature*). The *institution* *tracks* the *subject* (the *no* *mere* *accident*), and the *reply* *grounds* the *institution* in the *subject*. The *reply* is *defensible* (the *no* *mere* *sociology*), and *contested* (the *no* *subject*).

### 1.3 The disunity thesis

**The *thesis*** (the *contemporary* *literature*; the *pluralist* *insight*; the *contemporary* *literature*; the *Kuhnian* *challenge*). The *philosophy* is the *family* of the *projects* (the *no* *single* *subject*), and the *family* *resemblance* *holds* the *parts* (the *no* *mere* *essence*), and the *no* *single* *method* (the *no* *mere* *instrument*), and the *no* *single* *object* (the *no* *mere* *topic*), and the *unity* is the *convenient* *fiction* (the *no* *mere* *fact*).

**The *structure***.

$$
\text{The philosophy} \ = \ \text{the family} \ (\text{the no single subject}) \ \wedge \ \text{the resemblance} \ (\text{the no mere essence})
$$

**The *argument***.

$$
\begin{array}{ll}
1. & \text{The domains have the no shared object} \ (\text{the no single subject}). \\
2. & \text{The domains have the no shared method} \ (\text{the no single instrument}). \\
3. & \text{The family resemblance holds the parts} \ (\text{the no mere essence}). \\
\hline
4. & \therefore \text{The philosophy is the family} \ (\text{the pluralist insight}), \ \text{and the unity is the convenient} \ (\text{the no mere} \ \text{fact}), \ \text{and the no single discipline} \ (\text{the no mere} \ \text{institution}).
\end{array}
$$

**The *virtue***. The *thesis* *captures* the *fragmentation* (the *no* *mere* *illusion*), and it *fits* the *practice* (the *specialization*, the *no* *mere* *unity*), and it *grounds* the *pluralism* in the *description* (the *no* *mere* *rhetoric*), and it *connects* the *disunity* to the *specialist's* *dilemma* (§3).

**The *objections***.

**(i) The *cross*-*citation* *objection***. The *domains* *cite* the *each* *other* (the *no* *mere* *family*), and the *citation* *is* the *evidence* of the *unity* (the *no* *mere* *convention*), and the *reply* *fails*.

**The *reply*** (the *contemporary* *literature*). The *citation* is the *courtesy* (the *no* *mere* *influence*), and the *reply* *grounds* the *disunity* in the *independence*. The *reply* is *contested* (the *no* *mere* *courtesy*).

**(ii) The *shared* *problems* *objection***. The *problems* *recur* (the *cause*, the *person*, the *norm*), and the *recurrence* *is* the *evidence* of the *object* (the *no* *mere* *coincidence*), and the *reply* *fails*.

**The *reply*** (the *pluralist* *insight*). The *recurrence* is the *homonym* (the *no* *mere* *identity*), and the *reply* *grounds* the *disunity* in the *different* *senses*. The *reply* is *contested* (the *no* *homonym*).

**(iii) The *no* *transfer* *objection***. The *results* *transfer* (the *logic* → the *epistemology*, the *semantics* → the *mind*), and the *transfer* *is* the *evidence* of the *method* (the *no* *mere* *family*), and the *reply* *fails*.

**The *reply*** (the *pluralist* *insight*). The *transfer* is the *local* (the *no* *global*), and the *reply* *grounds* the *disunity* in the *locality*. The *reply* is *defensible* (the *local* *insight*), and *contested* (the *no* *unity*).

### 1.4 The state of the unity debate

**The *assessment***. The *unity* *captures* the *method* (the *no* *mere* *family*), the *disunity* *captures* the *object* (the *no* *single* *subject*), and the *two* *theses* *are* *compatible* *if* the *unity* is the *form* and the *disunity* is the *matter*.

| The *thesis* | The *strength* | The *weakness* |
|---|---|---|
| **The unity** | The *method*; the *ambition* | The *no* *object* |
| **The disunity** | The *object*; the *description* | The *no* *transfer* |
| **The form/matter** | The *integration* | The *no* *content* |

**The *most* *defensible* *position***. A *qualified* *formal* *unity*: the *philosophy* is *one* in the *form* of the *question* (the *conditions* of *intelligibility*; the *no* *mere* *topic*) and *many* in the *matter* (the *domains*, the *methods*; the *no* *mere* *unity*), and the *integration* is the *reflective* *equilibrium* (the *no* *mere* *system*), *qualified* by the *admission* that the *form* and the *matter* *remain* *contested*.

## 2. The Interdependence of the Domains

### 2.1 The epistemic dependence

**The *thesis***. The *domains* *depend* on the *each* *other* (the *no* *mere* *neighbors*), and the *dependence* *is* the *evidence* of the *unity* (the *no* *mere* *convention*), and the *dependence* *runs* in the *four* *directions* (the *no* *mere* *one*-*way*).

**The *examples***.

| The *domain* | The *dependence* | The *proof* |
|---|---|---|
| **The *ethics*** | The *epistemology* (the *moral* *testimony*, the *moral* *ignorance*) | Ch. 44, §6; Ch. 46, §7 |
| **The *mind*** | The *epistemology* (the *self*-*knowledge*, the *other* *minds*) | Ch. 26, §6; Ch. 29, §1 |
| **The *language*** | The *metaphysics* (the *reference*, the *truth*) | Ch. 31, §2; Ch. 32, §1 |
| **The *value*** | The *metaphysics* (the *reasons*, the *facts*) | Ch. 44, §1; Ch. 45, §1 |

**The *argument***.

$$
\begin{array}{ll}
1. & \text{The ethics requires the epistemic question} \ (\text{the no mere isolation}). \\
2. & \text{The mind requires the epistemic question} \ (\text{the no mere isolation}). \\
3. & \text{The language and the value require the metaphysical} \ (\text{the no mere autonomy}). \\
\hline
4. & \therefore \text{The domains interdepend} \ (\text{the no mere} \ \text{neighbors}), \ \text{and the interdependence grounds the unity} \ (\text{the no mere} \ \text{convention}), \ \text{and the no autonomy is the result} \ (\text{the no mere} \ \text{island}).
\end{array}
$$

**The *virtue***. The *account* *captures* the *interdependence* (the *no* *mere* *isolation*), and it *fits* the *practice* (the *cross*-*citation*), and it *grounds* the *unity* in the *dependence* (the *no* *mere* *family*), and it *connects* the *unity* to the *method* (Ch. 3) and to the *book* (the *no* *mere* *anthology*).

**The *objections***.

**(i) The *no* *dependence* *objection***. The *domains* *are* *autonomous* (the *no* *mere* *dependence*), and the *cross*-*citation* is *superficial* (the *no* *mere* *influence*), and the *reply* *fails*.

**The *reply*** (the *contemporary* *literature*). The *dependence* is *substantial* (the *no* *mere* *courtesy*; the *Gettier* *case* *depends* on the *epistemology* of the *testimony*), and the *reply* *grounds* the *interdependence* in the *argument*. The *reply* is *defensible* (the *substantial* *insight*), and *contested* (the *no* *dependence*).

**(ii) The *circularity* *objection***. The *interdependence* is *circular* (the *ethics* → the *epistemology* → the *metaphysics* → the *ethics*), and the *circle* *undermines* the *ground* (the *no* *foundation*), and the *reply* *fails*.

**The *reply*** (the *equilibrium* *insight*). The *circle* *is* the *holism* (the *no* *mere* *vice*; the *Quine* *web*; the *Neurath* *boat*), and the *reply* *grounds* the *interdependence* in the *equilibrium*. The *reply* is *defensible* (the *holistic* *insight*), and *contested* (the *no* *foundation*).

### 2.2 The metaphysical dependence

**The *thesis***. The *metaphysics* *depends* on the *epistemology* (the *modal* *epistemology*, the *intuition*; Ch. 16, §6; Ch. 9) *and* the *epistemology* *depends* on the *metaphysics* (the *truth*, the *facts*; Ch. 32, §1), and the *dependence* *is* *reciprocal* (the *no* *mere* *foundation*).

$$
\text{The metaphysics} \ \leftrightarrows \ \text{the epistemology} \ \leftrightarrows \ \text{the language} \ \leftrightarrows \ \text{the value} \ (\text{the no mere foundation})
$$

**The *argument***.

$$
\begin{array}{ll}
1. & \text{The metaphysics requires the epistemic access} \ (\text{the no mere speculation}). \\
2. & \text{The epistemology requires the metaphysical truth} \ (\text{the no mere coherence}). \\
3. & \text{The two require the language and the value} \ (\text{the no mere autonomy}). \\
\hline

**The *reply*** (the *contemporary* *literature*). The *autonomy* is the *relative* (the *no* *mere* *absolute*), and the *reply* *grounds* the *domain* in the *question*. The *reply* is *defensible* (the *relative* *insight*), and *contested* (the *no* *autonomy*).

**The *thesis***. **The *virtue***. **The *objections***.

**(ii) The *naturalistic* *objection***. The *normativity* *collapses* into the *description* (the *no* *mere* *value*), and the *no* *norm* (the *no* *mere* *distinction*), and the *reply* *fails*.

**The *reply*** (the *liberal* *naturalism*; Ch. 52, §4.3). The *norm* *survives* the *naturalism* (the *no* *mere* *reduction*), and the *reply* *grounds* the *norm* in the *practice*. The *reply* is *defensible* (the *no* *mere* *reduction*).

### 2.4 The state of the interdependence

**The *assessment***. The *epistemic* *captures* the *ethics* (the *no* *mere* *isolation*), the *metaphysical* *captures* the *reciprocal* (the *no* *mere* *foundation*), and the *normative* *captures* the *deepest* (the *no* *mere* *neighborhood*).

**The *most* *defensible* *position***. A *qualified* *holistic* *interdependence*: the *domains* *depend* *reciprocally* (the *no* *mere* *foundation*), and the *dependence* *is* the *evidence* of the *unity* (the *no* *mere* *family*), and the *equilibrium* *integrates* the *whole* (the *no* *mere* *system*), and the *normative* is the *common* (the *no* *mere* *description*), *qualified* by the *admission* that the *autonomy* and the *foundation* *remain* *contested*.

## 3. The Specialist's Dilemma

### 3.1 The structure of the dilemma

**The *question***. The *specialization* *raises* the *dilemma* (the *no* *mere* *preference*): the *progress* *requires* the *division* (the *no* *mere* *dilettantism*), and the *whole* *requires* the *survey* (the *no* *mere* *fragment*), and the *two* *pull* *apart* (the *no* *mere* *harmony*).

**The *structure***.

$$
\begin{array}{ll}
1. & \text{The progress requires the division} \ (\text{the no mere dilettantism}). \\
2. & \text{The whole requires the survey} \ (\text{the no mere fragment}). \\
3. & \text{The two pull apart} \ (\text{the no mere harmony}). \\
\hline
4. & \therefore \text{The dilemma is the structural} \ (\text{the no mere} \ \text{preference}), \ \text{and the choice is the costly} \ (\text{the no mere} \ \text{free}), \ \text{and the no harmony is the condition} \ (\text{the no mere} \ \text{ideal}).
\end{array}
$$

### 3.2 The case for specialization

**The *thesis*** (the *division* *of* *labor*; the *contemporary* *literature*; the *no* *mere* *generalist*). The *specialization* *yields* the *comparative* *advantage* (the *no* *mere* *uniformity*), and the *technical* *competence* (the *no* *mere* *dilettantism*), and the *cumulative* *progress* (the *no* *mere* *fashion*), and the *discipline* *requires* the *division* (the *no* *mere* *polymath*).

**The *argument***.

$$
\begin{array}{ll}
1. & \text{The literature exceeds the single capacity} \ (\text{the no mere polymath}). \\
2. & \text{The competence requires the depth} \ (\text{the no mere survey}). \\
3. & \text{The depth requires the division} \ (\text{the no mere generalist}). \\
\hline
4. & \therefore \text{The specialization is necessary} \ (\text{the no mere} \ \text{choice}), \ \text{and the division yields the progress} \ (\text{the no mere} \ \text{fashion}), \ \text{and the no polymath is the result} \ (\text{the no mere} \ \text{ideal}).
\end{array}
$$

**The *virtue***.
