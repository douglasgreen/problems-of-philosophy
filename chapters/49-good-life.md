# Chapter 48 — The Good Life: Well-Being, Meaning, and Death

> “Since all knowledge and every choice revolves around some good, let us state what it is that we say the good is … and what we say happiness is, we may say it is the activity of the soul in accordance with excellence, and if there are several excellences, in accordance with the best and most complete.”
> — Aristotle, *Nicomachean Ethics* (c. 340 BCE), I.7, 1098a16–20

> “It is better to be a human being dissatisfied than a pig satisfied; better to be Socrates dissatisfied than a fool satisfied.”
> — J. S. Mill, *Utilitarianism* (1861), Ch. 2

> “When we are, death is not come, and when death is come, we are not.”
> — Epicurus, *Letter to Menoeceus*, 125

> “If you were offered the experience machine, would you plug in? … This is because we want to *do* certain things, and not just have the experience of doing them.”
> — Robert Nozick, *Anarchy, State, and Utopia* (1974), pp. 42–45

---

Chapter 45 examined the *content* of the moral judgment (the *good*, the *right*, the *virtuous*); Chapter 46 examined its *efficacy* (the *mechanism* of the *motive* and the *attribution*); Chapter 47 examined its *architecture* (the *political* *order*). This chapter examines the *point* — the *prudential* *question*: *what is good for the person*, and *why*.

The *transition* is the *inversion* of the *previous* *three*. The *moral* *judgment* is *agent*-*neutral* (the *impartial* *concern*; Ch. 45, §1.1); the *prudential* *judgment* is *agent*-*relative* (the *good for* this *person*, *not* the *good* *simpliciter*), and the *judgment* *is* *the* *subject* of the *whole* *applied* *ethics* (Ch. 51) *and* of the *political* *theory* (the *primary* *goods*, the *capabilities*, the *social* *indicators*; Ch. 47, §3.4). The *question* is *prior* to the *moral* *theories*: the *consequentialism* *aggregates* the *prudential* *goods* (the *hedonism*, the *desire*, the *objective* *list*; Ch. 45, §1.2), the *deontology* *protects* them (the *dignity*, the *autonomy*; Ch. 45, §2), and the *virtue* *ethics* *constitutes* them (the *flourishing*, the *eudaimonia*; Ch. 45, §3).

The *chapter* proceeds in *eight* *movements*. §1 examines the *theories* of *well*-*being*. §2 examines the *experience* *machine* and the *status* of the *intuitions* about the *good* *life*. §3 examines the *meaning* in *life* (the *distinctive* *question*). §4 examines the *happiness* and its *measurement* (the *empirical* *turn*). §5 examines the *eudaimonia* and the *perfectionism* (the *virtue* *return*). §6 examines the *death* and the *badness* of *death*. §7 examines the *suicide*, the *immortality*, and the *suffering*. §8 gives the *verdict*.

## 1. The Theories of Well-Being

### 1.1 The structure of the question

**The *question***. The *prudential* *question* (*what is good for* *a person*, *period*?) is *distinct* from the *moral* *question* (*what is good* *simpliciter*, the *right*, the *obligatory*), and the *distinction* *is* *the* *first* *thing* to *fix*.

**The *distinctions***.

| The *question* | The *content* | The *locution* |
|---|---|---|
| **Prudential** | The *good for* the *person* (the *welfare*, the *well*-*being*, the *interest*, the *flourishing*) | “*It* *is* *good* *for* *her*” |
| **Moral** | The *good* *simpliciter* (the *value*, the *rightness*, the *demand*) | “*It* *is* *good*” |
| **Aesthetic** | The *beauty*, the *excellence* of the *object* | “*It* *is* *beautiful*” |
| **Perfectionist** | The *excellence* of the *kind* | “*It* *is* a *good* *specimen*” |

**The *significance*.** The *prudential* *question* *is* *the* *foundation* of the *applied* *ethics* (the *medical* *decision*, the *poverty*, the *education*), and *every* *normative* *theory* *presupposes* an *answer*, and the *metaethical* *debate* (Ch. 44) *settles* *nothing* *here* — the *naturalism*, the *non*-*naturalism*, and the *expressivism* *all* *allow* *the* *prudential* *facts*; the *question* is *the* *content* *of* *those* *facts*.

**The *criteria***. The *theory* *must* *satisfy* the *criteria* of Ch. 4, §3 (the *consistency*, the *explanatory* *power*, the *simplicity*, the *conservatism*, the *adequacy* to the *intuitions*), and it *must* *respect* the *formal* *constraints*:

| The *constraint* | The *content* | The *significance* |
|---|---|---|
| **The *prudential* *prerogative*** | The *well*-*being* *is* the *values* *the* *agent* *has* a *reason* to *care* about (the *no* *alienation*) | The *agent*-*relative* *insight* |
| **The *objectivity*** | The *well*-*being* *is* *not* *the* *mere* *opinion* (the *no* *laundering*) | The *no* *adaptive* *preference* |
| **The *universality*** | The *well*-*being* *is* *the* *same* for *all* the *persons* (the *no* *arbitrary* *exception*) | The *no* *elitism* |
| **The *additivity*** | The *well*-*being* *can* be *aggregated* (the *social* *welfare*; Ch. 45, §1.4) | The *interpersonal* *comparison* |

**The *problem***. The *constraints* *pull* *apart*: the *objectivity* *conflicts* with the *prerogative* (the *paternalism*), and the *additivity* *requires* the *cardinal* *measurement* (the *comparability*), and the *universality* *conflicts* with the *pluralism* (the *different* *conceptions* of the *flourishing*).

### 1.2 Hedonism

**The *thesis*** (Bentham 1789, Ch. 1; Mill 1861, Ch. 2; Sidgwick 1874, Bk. I, Ch. 4; the *contemporary* *literature*; the *experientialism*; the *contemporary* *literature*). The *well*-*being* *consists* in the *balance* of the *pleasure* *over* the *pain* (the *hedonic* *state*), and the *only* *thing* *good* *for* the *person* *is* the *experience* (the *experiential* *insight*), and the *objects* of the *experience* *matter* *only* *in* *virtue* of the *experience* they *produce*.

**The *structure***.

$$
W(P) = \sum_t \big( \text{pleasure}(P, t) - \text{pain}(P, t) \big) \quad (\text{the *hedonic* *balance*})
$$

**The *arguments***.

**(i) The *introspective* *argument***. The *experience* *is* the *only* *thing* *that* *is* *valued* *for* its *own* *sake* (the *pleasure* *is* *self*-*intimating*; the *no* *reason* *needed*), and the *other* *goods* (the *knowledge*, the *achievement*, the *friendship*) are *valued* *because* of the *experience* they *produce* (the *experiential* *insight*).

**(ii) The *humanitarian* *argument*** (Sidgwick 1874, Bk. III, Ch. 14). The *reduction* of the *suffering* *is* the *universally* *recognized* *good* (the *no* *controversy*), and the *reduction* *requires* the *hedonic* *metric*, and the *hedonic* *metric* *avoids* the *controversy* (the *minimal* *commitment*).

**(iii) The *equivalence* *argument***. The *two* *lives* *that* *are* *introspectively* *identical* (the *same* *experience*) *cannot* *differ* *in* *well*-*being* (the *experience* *exhausts* the *welfare*), and the *equivalence* *yields* the *experientialism*, and the *experientialism* *yields* the *hedonism* (the *pleasure* *is* the *only* *intrinsic* *good* *experience*).

**The *virtue***. The *account* *is* *simple* (the *single* *metric*), and it *fits* the *practice* (the *suffering* *matters* *everywhere*), and it *grounds* the *interpersonal* *comparison* (the *hedonic* *magnitude*), and it *connects* to the *neuroscience* (the *hedonic* *tone*; the *wanting*/*liking* *distinction*; Ch. 46, §1.5).

**The *objections***.

**(i) The *experience* *machine*** (§2). The *machine* *delivers* the *maximal* *pleasure* (the *simulated* *life*) and the *intuition* *rejects* it (the *no* *plugging*), and the *rejection* *refutes* the *experientialism*, and the *reply* *fails*.

**The *reply*** (the *contemporary* *literature*). The *intuition* *tracks* the *future* *goods* (the *machine* *forecloses* the *later* *achievements*, the *relationships*), and the *reply* *reconciles* the *hedonism* with the *intuition*. The *reply* is *contested* (§2.3).

**(ii) The *hedonic* *treadmill*** (§4.3). The *adaptation* *makes* the *hedonic* *state* *unstable* (the *lottery* *winner* *returns* to the *baseline*), and the *account* *cannot* *explain* the *well*-*being* *differences* (the *disability*, the *poverty*), and the *reply* *fails*.

**The *reply*** (the *contemporary* *literature*; the *empirical* *insight*). The *adaptation* *is* *partial* (the *not* *complete*; the *done* *not* *by* the *all*), and the *reply* *grounds* the *hedonism* in the *long*-*run* *measurement*. The *reply* *is* *defensible* (the *partial* *adaptation* *still* *supports* the *hedonic* *account*).

**(iii) The *qualitative* *distinction*** (Mill 1861, Ch. 2). The *pleasures* *differ* in *kind* (the *higher* and the *lower*; the *Socrates* *vs.* the *pig*), and the *hedonism* *must* *rank* them (the *competent* *judges*), and the *ranking* *abandons* the *pure* *quantitative* *hedonism* (the *qualitative* *insight*), and the *reply* *fails*.

**The *reply***. The *quality* *is* a *dimension* of the *pleasure* (the *complexity*, the *duration*, the *certainty*; the *Mill* *qualitative* *ranking*), and the *reply* *grounds* the *quality* in the *experience* (the *higher* *pleasures* *are* *more* *pleasurable* *to* the *competent* *judge*). The *reply* is *contested* (the *quality* *is* *not* *the* *quantity*).

### 1.3 Desire-satisfaction

**The *thesis*** (the *contemporary* *literature*; the *desire* *theory*; Griffin, *Well-Being*, 1986, Ch. 1; the *informed*-*desire* *account*; Railton, “Facts and Values,” 1986, §4). The *well*-*being* *consists* in the *satisfaction* of the *desires* (the *actual*, the *ideal*, the *informed*, the *laundered*), and the *good* *for* the *person* *is* *what* the *person* *wants* (the *prerogative* *insight*), and the *desires* *can* be *objective* (the *informed* *desire* *tracks* the *facts*).

**The *varieties***.

| The *variety* | The *content* | The *problem* |
|---|---|---|
| **Actual desire** | The *present* *desire* *satisfied* | The *adaptive* *preference* |
| **Informed desire** | The *desire* *under* the *full* *information* | The *no* *coherence* (the *counterfactual* *person* is *not* *me*) |
| **Laundered desire** | The *desire* *after* the *cognitive* *therapy* | The *no* *neutrality* |
| **Ideal desire** | The *desire* *of* the *ideal* *advisor* | The *no* *prerogative* |

**The *arguments***.

**(i) The *prerogative* *argument***. The *well*-*being* *is* *the* *person*'s (the *agent*-*relative*), and the *person*'s *own* *desires* *define* the *good* (the *no* *paternalism*), and the *desire* *satisfaction* *respects* the *prerogative* (the *subjectivity*).

**(ii) The *pluralism* *argument***. The *goods* *are* *many* (the *knowledge*, the *friendship*, the *achievement*, the *pleasure*), and the *desires* *capture* the *pluralism* (the *each* *person* *values* *differently*), and the *desire* *satisfaction* *avoids* the *no* *list* (the *objective* *list* *problem*; §1.4).

**(iii) The *explanatory* *argument***. The *desire* *satisfaction* *explains* the *motivation* (the *agent* *acts* on the *desires*; Ch. 46, §1), and the *explanation* *grounds* the *well*-*being* in the *desire* (the *coherent* *link*), and the *theory* *fits* the *practice*.

**The *virtue***. The *account* *respects* the *prerogative* (the *no* *paternalism*), and it *captures* the *pluralism* (the *many* *goods*), and it *connects* the *well*-*being* to the *motivation* (Ch. 46, §1).

**The *objections***.

**(i) The *adaptive* *preference*** (the *contemporary* *literature*; Elster, *Sour Grapes*, 1983, Ch. 3; Sen, *Commodities and Capabilities*, 1985; Ch. 47, §3.4). The *desires* *adapt* to the *oppression* (the *subordinate* *accepts* the *subordination*, the *poor* *accepts* the *poverty*), and the *satisfaction* *of* the *adaptive* *desire* *is* *not* *the* *well*-*being* (the *no* *laundering*), and the *reply* *fails*.

**The *reply*** (the *informed* *desire* *account*; Griffin 1986, Ch. 1). The *informed* *desire* *removes* the *adaptation* (the *agent* *would* *desire* the *freedom* *if* *informed*), and the *reply* *grounds* the *well*-*being* in the *informed* *desire*. The *reply* is *contested* (the *counterfactual* *person* *is* *not* *me*; the *no* *prerogative*).

**(ii) The *object* *of* the *desire***. The *agent* *desires* the *state* *of* *affairs* (the *child* *cured*), and the *state* *of* *affairs* *may* *obtain* *without* the *agent*'s *knowledge* (the *unaware* *satisfaction*), and the *unaware* *satisfaction* *counts* *for* the *well*-*being* (the *no* *experience* *requirement*), and the *account* *is* *counterintuitive* (the *posthumous* *desire*; the *deceived* *agent*).

**The *reply*** (the *contemporary* *literature*). The *unaware* *satisfaction* *does* *count* (the *desire* *is* *satisfied*, and the *satisfaction* *is* *good*, *even* *unknown*), and the *reply* *grounds* the *well*-*being* in the *desire* *satisfaction* *itself* (the *no* *experience* *insight*). The *reply* is *contested* (the *counterintuitive* *consequences*).

**(iii) The *no* *hierarchy* *objection***. The *desires* *conflict* (the *short*-*term* vs. the *long*-*term*, the *informed* vs. the *actual*), and the *theory* *cannot* *rank* them (the *no* *priority*), and the *reply* *fails*.

**The *reply*** (the *contemporary* *literature*). The *informed* *desire* *ranks* the *conflict* (the *full* *information* *yields* the *ordering*), and the *reply* *grounds* the *ranking* in the *information*. The *reply* is *contested* (the *no* *aggregation* of the *desires*).

### 1.4 The objective list

**The *thesis*** (Moore 1903, §105; the *contemporary* *literature*; Finnis, *Natural Law and Natural Rights*, 1980, Ch. 4; the *contemporary* *literature*; the *perfectionism*; Hurka, *Perfectionism*, 1993). The *well*-*being* *consists* in the *objective* *goods* (the *knowledge*, the *achievement*, the *friendship*, the *aesthetic* *experience*, the *autonomy*, the *health*, the *moral* *goodness*), and the *goods* *are* *good* for the *person* *regardless* of the *desires* (the *objective* *insight*), and the *list* *is* *plural* (the *no* *single* *metric*).

**The *structure***.

$$
W(P) = \sum_{i \in \text{the *list*}} w_i \cdot G_i(P) \quad (\text{the *weighted* *sum* of the *objective* *goods*})
$$

**The *arguments***.

**(i) The *experience* *machine*** (§2). The *machine* *delivers* the *major* *goods* (the *no* *knowledge*, the *no* *achievement*, the *no* *contact*), and the *intuition* *rejects* the *machine*, and the *rejection* *supports* the *objective* *list* (the *objective* *insight*).

**(ii) The *contentment* *argument***. The *contentment* *is* *not* the *same* as the *well*-*being* (the *contented* *slave*, the *contented* *child*, the *contented* *lobotomy* *patient*), and the *difference* *requires* the *objective* *goods* (the *knowledge*, the *autonomy*), and the *reply* *grounds* the *well*-*being* in the *objective* *goods*.

**(iii) The *no* *laundering* *argument***. The *adaptive* *preferences* *launder* the *oppression* (the *no* *objective* *standard*), and the *objective* *list* *resists* the *laundering* (the *knowledge*, the *autonomy* *are* *good* *regardless*), and the *reply* *grounds* the *well*-*being* in the *objective* *goods*.

**The *virtue***. The *account* *resists* the *laundering* (the *no* *adaptive* *preference*), and it *captures* the *pluralism* (the *list*), and it *fits* the *intuition* (the *machine*, the *contentment*), and it *connects* the *well*-*being* to the *eudaimonia* (§5).

**The *objections***.

**(i) The *paternalism* *objection*** (the *contemporary* *literature*). The *objective* *list* *imposes* the *conception* of the *good* (the *agent* *must* *have* the *knowledge* *whether* *or* *not* *she* *wants* it), and the *imposition* *violates* the *prerogative* (the *no* *autonomy*), and the *reply* *fails*.

**The *reply*** (Hurka 1993, Ch. 1; the *contemporary* *literature*; the *autonomy* *as* the *list* *item*). The *autonomy* *is* *one* *of* the *goods* (the *agent* *chooses* the *form* of the *knowledge*, the *achievement*), and the *reply* *grounds* the *prerogative* in the *autonomy* (the *list* *includes* the *autonomy*). The *reply* is *defensible* (the *list* *is* *flexible*).

**(ii) The *no* *list* *objection*** (the *contemporary* *literature*). The *list* *is* *arbitrary* (the *why* *these* *goods*?), and the *different* *lists* *are* *defensible* (the *no* *determinacy*), and the *reply* *fails*.

**The *reply*** (the *contemporary* *literature*; the *nature*-*based* *account*). The *list* *is* *grounded* in the *human* *nature* (the *perfectionism*, the *function* *argument*, the *eudaimonia*; §5), and the *reply* *grounds* the *list* in the *nature*. The *reply* is *contested* (the *historicism*, the *pluralism*).

**(iii) The *no* *equivalence* *objection***. The *list* *is* *the* *disguised* *desire* *theory* (the *goods* *that* *the* *people* *happen* *to* *desire*), and the *objectivity* *is* *illusory*, and the *reply* *fails*.

**The *reply*** (the *objective* *insight*). The *goods* *are* *good* *for* the *agent* *regardless* of the *desire* (the *knowledge* *of* the *agent* *who* *does* not *care*), and the *reply* *grounds* the *objectivity* in the *reasons* (the *value* *is* *not* *the* *desire*). The *reply* is *defensible*.

### 1.5 The hybrid views

**The *thesis*** (Sumner, *Welfare, Happiness, and Ethics*, 1996, Ch. 3; the *authentic* *happiness*; the *contemporary* *literature*). The *well*-*being* *requires* *both* the *subjective* *endorsement* (the *desire*, the *satisfaction*, the *experience*) *and* the *objective* *good* (the *list*), and the *two* *are* *integrated* by the *endorsement* (the *agent* *endorses* the *objective* *good*; the *good* *is* *for* the *agent*).

**The *structure*** (the *Sumner* *form*).

$$
W(P) = \text{the *objective* *good*} \ G \ \text{that is *endorsed* by the *agent*} \ (\text{the *authentic* *happiness*})
$$

**The *arguments***.

**(i) The *prerogative* *and* the *objectivity*. The *hybrid* *satisfies* the *prerogative* (the *endorsement*) and the *objectivity* (the *goods*), and the *two* *constraints* *are* *satisfied* (the *hybrid* *insight*).

**(ii) The *no* *laundering* *and* the *no* *paternalism*. The *hybrid* *resists* the *laundering* (the *objective* *goods*) *and* the *paternalism* (the *endorsement*), and the *two* *constraints* *are* *satisfied* (the *hybrid* *insight*).

**The *virtue***. The *account* *integrates* the *prerogative* and the *objectivity*, and it *avoids* the *paternalism* and the *laundering*, and it *fits* the *intuition* (the *glad* *acceptance*, the *no* *alienation*).

**The *objections***.

**(i) The *endorsement* *question***. The *endorsement* *requires* the *informed* *agent* (the *perfect* *knowledge*, the *autonomy*), and the *counterfactual* *person* *is* *not* *the* *actual* *agent*, and the *reply* *fails*.

**The *reply*** (Sumner 1996, Ch. 3). The *endorsement* *is* the *actual* *agent*'s (the *informed* *endorsement*, the *no* *deception*; the *authentic* *happiness* *requires* the *self*-*knowledge*), and the *reply* *grounds* the *endorsement* in the *authenticity*. The *reply* is *defensible* (the *authenticity* *is* *the* *condition*).

**(ii) The *no* *list* *objection*** (the *objective* *list* *problem*; §1.4). The *hybrid* *retains* the *list*, and the *list* *is* *arbitrary*, and the *reply* *fails*.

**The *reply*** (the *contemporary* *literature*). The *list* *is* *grounded* in the *nature* (the *perfectionism*; §5), and the *reply* *grounds* the *list* in the *nature*. The *reply* is *contested*.

### 1.6 The state of the well-being debate

**The *assessment***. The *hedonism* is *defensible* (the *experiential* *insight*), and the *desire* *theory* is *defensible* (the *prerogative*), and the *objective* *list* is *defensible* (the *no* *laundering*), and the *hybrid* is the *most* *defensible* (the *integration*).

| The *theory* | The *strength* | The *weakness* |
|---|---|---|
| **Hedonism** | The *simplicity*; the *suffering* | The *machine*; the *qualitative* *distinction* |
| **Desire** | The *prerogative*; the *pluralism* | The *adaptive* *preference*; the *unaware* *satisfaction* |
| **Objective list** | The *no* *laundering*; the *machine* | The *paternalism*; the *no* *list* |
| **Hybrid** | The *integration* | The *endorsement*; the *list* |

**The *most* *defensible* *position***. A *qualified* *hybrid*: the *subjective* *endorsement* (the *prerogative* *insight*), the *objective* *goods* (the *objectivity* *insight*), the *authenticity* (the *no* *deception*), the *pluralism* (the *many* *goods*), *grounded* in the *human* *nature* (the *perfectionist* *insight*), *qualified* by the *admission* that the *list* *and* the *endorsement* *remain* *contested*.

## 2. The Experience Machine

### 2.1 The case

**The *case*** (Nozick 1974, pp. 42–45). The *machine* (the *electrodes*, the *tank*, the *superduper* *neuropsychologists*) *simulates* the *life* (the *maximal* *pleasure*, the *no* *awareness* of the *simulation*, the *program* *chosen* *in* *advance*), and the *choice* is *offered* (the *lifetime* *plug*-*in*), and the *intuition* is the *refusal* (the *no* *plugging* *in*), and the *refusal* *refutes* the *hedonism* (the *pleasure* *is* *not* *the* *only* *good*).

**The *structure***.

$$
\text{The machine intuition}: \ \text{the *refusal*} \ \Rightarrow \ \neg \ (\text{the *pleasure* *is* the *sufficient* *well-being*})
$$

**The *three* *reasons*** (Nozick 1974, pp. 43–45).

| The *reason* | The *content* | The *good* *involved* |
|---|---|---|
| **The *doing*** | We want to *do* the things (the *action*, the *achievement*) | The *agency* |
| **The *being*** | We want to *be* a *certain* *kind* of *person* (the *character*, the *courage*) | The *character* |
| **The *contact*** | We want the *contact* with the *reality* (the *truth*, the *world*) | The *reality* |

**The *argument***.

$$
\begin{array}{ll}
1. & \text{The *machine* *delivers* the *maximal* *pleasure* (the *experiential* *insight*).} \\
2. & \text{The *intuition* *rejects* the *machine* (the *no* *plugging* *in*).} \\
3. & \text{The *rejection* *requires* the *goods* *beyond* the *pleasure* (the *doing*, the *being*, the *contact*).} \\
\hline
4. & \therefore \text{The *hedonism* *is* *false* (the *machine* *insight*), and the *well*-*being* *includes* the *objective* *goods* (the *list* *insight*).}
\end{array}
$$

### 2.2 The hedonist replies

**The *replies***.

**(i) The *no* *intuition* *reply***. The *intuition* *is* *the* *cultural* *artifact* (the *Western* *individualism*, the *achievement* *ethos*), and the *agent* *who* *values* *only* the *pleasure* *would* *plug* *in*, and the *intuition* *is* *not* *universal*, and the *reply* *dissolves* the *objection*.

**The *reply*.** The *cross*-*cultural* *evidence* *is* *contested* (the *contemporary* *literature*; the *x-phi*; Ch. 3, §6), and the *intuition* *is* *robust* *enough* (the *no* *plugging* *in* *the* *majority*), and the *reply* *is* *defensible* *at* *best*.

**(ii) The *no* *simulation* *reply***. The *machine* *life* *is* *not* *the* *pleasure* *life* (the *simulation* *is* *fragile*, the *program* *is* *fixed*, the *no* *novelty*, the *no* *challenge*), and the *hedonism* *predicts* the *refusal* (the *long*-*run* *pleasure* *is* *higher* *outside* the *machine*), and the *reply* *reconciles* the *hedonism* with the *intuition*.

**The *reply*.** The *program* *can* *deliver* the *novelty* (the *simulated* *challenges*, the *simulated* *achievements*), and the *hedonist* *cannot* *explain* the *rejection* (the *simulated* *pleasure* *is* *equal*), and the *reply* *is* *contested* (§2.3).

**(iii) The *status* *quo* *bias* *reply***. The *refusal* *is* *the* *status* *quo* *bias* (the *loss* *aversion*, the *no* *imagination*), and the *intuition* *is* *not* the *evidence* (the *cognitive* *bias*), and the *reply* *defeats* the *objection*.

**The *reply*.** The *bias* *explains* some *of* the *reluctance* (the *contemporary* *literature*), and the *intuition* *persists* *after* the *reflection* (the *considered* *judgment*), and the *reply* *is* *contested* (the *no* *separate* *evidence*).

**(iv) The *two*-*level* *reply*** (the *sophisticated* *hedonism*). The *agent* *values* the *objective* *goods* *instrumentally* (the *achievement* *produces* the *pleasure*), and the *refusal* *tracks* the *instrumental* *value* (the *machine* *forgoes* the *future* *pleasure*), and the *reply* *preserves* the *hedonism*.

**The *reply*.** The *instrumental* *value* *does* *not* *explain* the *counterfactual* *case* (the *machine* *guarantees* the *pleasure*, and the *refusal* *persists*; the *Deceived* *Businessman*, the *Happy* *Addict*), and the *reply* *is* *contested*.

### 2.3 The status of the intuition

**The *question***. The *intuition* *is* *evidence* (the *moral* *perception*, the *considered* *judgment*; Ch. 44, §6.2), and the *evidence* *is* *defeasible* (the *no* *infallibility*), and the *weight* *depends* on the *robustness* (the *no* *framing* *effect*, the *no* *culture* *dependence*, the *no* *bias*), and the *reflective* *equilibrium* *integrates* the *intuition* with the *theory* (Ch. 45, §5.3).

**The *argument***.

$$
\begin{array}{ll}
1. & \text{The *intuition* *is* *evidence* (the *considered* *judgment*; the *method*).} \\
2. & \text{The *evidence* *is* *defeasible* (the *framing*, the *bias*, the *disagreement*).} \\
3. & \text{The *weight* *depends* on the *robustness* (the *replication*, the *convergence*).} \\
\hline
4. & \therefore \text{The *machine* *intuition* *supports* the *objective* *goods* (the *robust* *insight*), and it *does* not *confute* the *hedonism* *decisively* (the *defeasible* *insight*).}
\end{array}
$$

**The *significance***. The *intuition* *is* *the* *strongest* *single* *argument* against the *hedonism*, and it *is* *not* *decisive* (the *hedonist* *replies* are *contested* but *not* *absurd*), and the *balanced* *conclusion* is the *hybrid* (§1.5): the *well*-*being* *includes* the *subjective* *experience* *and* the *objective* *goods*, and the *hedonism* *is* *false* *in* the *pure* *form* *and* *defensible* *in* the *qualified* *form*.

**The *significance***. The *machine* *is* the *paradigm* *thought* *experiment* of the *well*-*being* debate (the *Nozick* *insight*), and it *shows* the *method* (the *intuition*, the *defeasibility*, the *equilibrium*), and it *grounds* the *hybrid* *theory* (§1.5), and it *connects* the *well*-*being* to the *meaning* (§3: the *machine* *life* *is* *meaningless*, and the *meaning* *requires* the *reality*).

## 3. Meaning in Life

### 3.1 The distinction

**The *thesis*** (the *contemporary* *literature*; Wolf, “Happiness and Meaning: Two Aspects of the Good Life,” 1997; Metz, “The Meaning of Life,” 2001; the *contemporary* *literature*). The *meaning* in *life* *is* *distinct* from the *well*-*being* (the *happiness*, the *desire* *satisfaction*), and the *distinction* *is* *the* *first* *thing* to *fix* (the *meaning* *is* *not* the *pleasure*), and the *meaning* *is* the *value* of the *life* *as* *a* *whole* (the *narrative* *structure*), *not* the *moment*-*by*-*moment* *aggregation*.

**The *distinction***.

| The *concept* | The *content* | The *example* |
|---|---|---|
| **Well-being** | The *good* *for* the *person* (the *moment*-*by*-*moment*) | The *pleasure*, the *health*, the *friendship* |
| **Meaning** | The *value* of the *life* *as* *a* *whole* (the *purpose*, the *significance*) | The *vocation*, the *care*, the *achievement* |
| **Happiness** | The *subjective* *satisfaction* (the *experience*, the *judgment*) | The *contentment*, the *joy* |
| **The *absurd*** | The *confrontation* *with* the *no* *cosmic* *meaning* | The *Camus* *insight* |

**The *significance***. The *distinction* *grounds* the *question* (the *meaning* *is* *not* the *happiness*), and it *fits* the *practice* (the *vocation* *sacrifices* the *happiness*; the *artist*, the *parent*, the *soldier*), and it *connects* the *well*-*being* to the *narrative* (the *life* *as* the *story*).

### 3.2 The subjective accounts

**The *thesis*** (the *subjectivism*; the *contemporary* *literature*; Frankfurt, “The Importance of What We Care About,” 1982; *The Reasons of Love*, 2004; the *contemporary* *literature*). The *meaning* *is* the *subjective* *engagement* (the *caring*, the *commitment*, the *desire*), and the *life* *is* *meaningful* *when* it is *devoted* to the *projects* *the* *agent* *cares* *about*, and the *content* of the *projects* *does* *not* *matter* (the *no* *objective* *condition*).

**The *structure***.

$$
\text{Meaning} \ = \ \text{the *caring*} \ (\text{the *final* *ends* that the agent *endorses* and *sustains*})
$$

**The *arguments***.

**(i) The *no* *cosmic* *meaning* *argument***. The *universe* *has* *no* *purpose* (the *no* *God*, the *no* *telos*; Ch. 50, §3), and the *meaning* *must* be *the* *human* *construction* (the *subjectivism*), and the *construction* *is* the *caring*, and the *caring* *suffices*.

**(ii) The *relational* *argument***. The *meaning* *is* *the* *relation* *to* the *cared*-*for* *object* (the *no* *intrinsic* *meaning*), and the *relation* *is* *subjective* (the *no* *objective* *standard*), and the *subjectivity* *grounds* the *meaning* in the *caring* (the *Frankfurt* *insight*).

**(iii) The *no* *elitism* *argument***. The *objective* *account* *elitizes* the *meaning* (the *art*, the *science*, the *discovery*), and the *ordinary* *life* (the *work*, the *family*, the *care*) *is* *meaningful*, and the *subjectivism* *captures* the *ordinary* *meaning* (the *no* *elitism*).

**The *virtue***. The *account* *grounds* the *meaning* in the *agency* (the *autonomy*, the *caring*), and it *avoids* the *elitism* (the *ordinary* *meaning*), and it *fits* the *practice* (the *commitments*).

**The *objections***.

**(i) The *no* *distinction* *objection*** (Wolf, “Happiness and Meaning,” 1997, §2; Metz 2001, §3). The *subjectivism* *cannot* *distinguish* the *meaningful* from the *trivial* (the *lives* *devoted* to the *crossword* *puzzles*, the *collection* of the *bottle* *caps*, the *Nazi* *ideology*), and the *distinction* *requires* the *objective* *condition*, and the *reply* *fails*.

**The *reply*** (the *contemporary* *literature*; the *depth* *insight*). The *caring* *admits* of *degrees* (the *depth*, the *robustness*, the *finality*), and the *deep* *caring* *distinguishes* the *meaningful* (the *vocation*, the *relationship*) from the *shallow* (the *hobby*), and the *reply* *grounds* the *distinction* in the *depth*. The *reply* is *contested* (the *depth* *is* *still* *subjective*).

**(ii) The *no* *normative* *constraint* *objection***. The *subjectivism* *validates* the *monstrous* *meaning* (the *Nazi*, the *terrorist*), and the *meaning* *requires* the *moral* *constraint*, and the *reply* *fails*.

**The *reply*** (the *contemporary* *literature*). The *meaning* *is* *not* the *morality* (the *meaningful* *life* *can* be *immoral*), and the *reply* *separates* the *two* (the *meaning* *and* the *morality*). The *reply* is *defensible* (the *no* *moral* *condition* on the *meaning*), and *contested* (the *no* *validation* of the *monstrous*).

### 3.3 The objective accounts

**The *thesis*** (the *objectivism*; the *contemporary* *literature*; Metz, *Meaning in Life*, 2013, Ch. 2; the *perfectionism*; the *no* *subjective* *condition*). The *meaning* *requires* the *objective* *value* (the *good* *of* the *object*), and the *life* *is* *meaningful* *when* it *engages* with the *objective* *goods* (the *knowledge*, the *achievement*, the *moral* *goodness*, the *aesthetic* *value*), and the *caring* *is* *not* *required* (the *no* *subjective* *condition*; the *unwitting* *contribution*).

**The *structure***.

$$
\text{Meaning} \ = \ \text{the *engagement* with the *objective* *value*} \ (\text{the *no* *subjective* *condition*})
$$

**The *arguments***.

**(i) The *depth* *argument***. The *objective* *goods* *ground* the *depth* (the *achievement* *of* the *science*, the *moral* *sacrifice*), and the *subjectivism* *cannot* *explain* the *depth* (the *trivial* *caring* *is* *equal*), and the *objectivism* *grounds* the *depth* in the *value*.

**(ii) The *no* *delusion* *argument***. The *meaningful* *life* *is* *not* *a* *delusion* (the *no* *false* *consciousness*), and the *subjectivism* *validates* the *delusion* (the *caring* *for* the *imaginary*), and the *objectivism* *grounds* the *meaning* in the *reality* (the *no* *delusion*).

**(iii) The *cross*-*personal* *argument***. The *meaning* *is* *recognized* across the *persons* (the *Einstein* *insight*, the *Gandhi* *insight*), and the *subjectivism* *cannot* *explain* the *recognition* (the *no* *shared* *standard*), and the *objectivism* *grounds* the *recognition* in the *objective* *value*.

**The *virtue***. The *account* *grounds* the *depth* (the *no* *equivalence*), and it *avoids* the *delusion* (the *no* *false* *consciousness*), and it *explains* the *recognition* (the *shared* *standard*).

**The *objections***.

**(i) The *no* *intrinsic* *meaning* *objection*** (the *subjectivism*; the *contemporary* *literature*). The *objective* *value* *is* *not* *the* *meaning* (the *cosmic* *value* *is* *not* *the* *lived* *meaning*), and the *meaning* *requires* the *caring* (the *engagement*), and the *reply* *fails*.

**The *reply*** (the *objectivism*; Metz 2013, Ch. 3). The *objective* *value* *is* the *condition* (the *meaningful* *life* *requires* the *value*), and the *caring* *is* *not* *the* *condition* (the *unwitting* *contribution* *is* *meaningful*), and the *reply* *grounds* the *meaning* in the *value*. The *reply* is *contested* (the *no* *lived* *meaning*).

**(ii) The *no* *list* *objection*** (the *objective* *list* *problem*; §1.4). The *objective* *goods* *are* *arbitrary*, and the *list* *is* *contestable*, and the *reply* *fails*.

**The *reply*** (the *contemporary* *literature*). The *list* *is* *grounded* in the *nature* (the *perfectionism*; §5), and the *reply* *grounds* the *list* in the *nature*. The *reply* is *contested*.

**(iii) The *no* *motivation* *objection***. The *objective* *meaning* *is* *not* *the* *reason* to *live* (the *no* *motivation*), and the *subjectivity* *is* *required* (the *caring* *grounds* the *motivation*), and the *reply* *fails*.

**The *reply*** (the *integration*; §3.4). The *objective* *value* *and* the *caring* *are* *integrated* (the *hybrid*), and the *reply* *grounds* the *motivation* in the *integration*. The *reply* is *defensible*.

### 3.4 The hybrid account

**The *thesis*** (Wolf, “Happiness and Meaning,” 1997; *Meaning in Life and Why It Matters*, 2010; the *contemporary* *literature*). The *meaning* *requires* *both* the *subjective* *attraction* (the *caring*, the *engagement*) *and* the *objective* *value* (the *worthwhile* *object*), and the *two* *are* *integrated* by the *fitting* *fulfillment* (the *activity* *that* *the* *agent* *loves* *and* *that* *is* *objectively* *worthwhile*).

**The *structure*** (the *Wolf* *form*).

$$
\text{Meaning} \ = \ \text{the *subjective* *attraction*} \wedge \text{the *objective* *value*} \ (\text{the *fitting* *fulfillment*})
$$

**The *argument***.

$$
\begin{array}{ll}
1. & \text{The *subjectivism* *validates* the *trivial* (the *no* *depth*).} \\
2. & \text{The *objectivism* *misses* the *lived* *meaning* (the *no* *engagement*).} \\
3. & \text{The *hybrid* *integrates* the *two* (the *subjective* *attraction* + the *objective* *value*).} \\
\hline
4. & \therefore \text{The *hybrid* *captures* the *meaning* (the *Wolf* *insight*).}
\end{array}
$$

**The *virtue***. The *account* *captures* the *depth* (the *objective* *value*) and the *engagement* (the *subjective* *attraction*), and it *avoids* the *trivial* and the *alienation*, and it *fits* the *practice* (the *vocation* *that* *matters*).

**The *objections***.

**(i) The *no* *measurement* *objection***. The *objective* *value* *is* *contestable* (the *who* *decides* the *worthwhile*), and the *hybrid* *cannot* *specify* the *condition*, and the *reply* *fails*.

**The *reply*** (Wolf 2010, Ch. 4). The *value* *is* *the* *human* *excellence* (the *perfectionism*; §5), and the *condition* *is* *specified* by the *reasons* (the *no* *arbitrary* *list*), and the *reply* *grounds* the *value* in the *nature*. The *reply* is *contested*.

**(ii) The *no* *motivation* *objection***. The *subjective* *attraction* *is* *not* *required* (the *meaningful* *life* *can* *be* *unloved*, the *duty*, the *sacrifice*), and the *hybrid* *over*-*extends* the *condition*, and the *reply* *fails*.

**The *reply*** (the *contemporary* *literature*). The *attraction* *is* *the* *condition* (the *no* *meaning* *without* the *engagement*, the *martyr* *who* *hates* the *cause* *is* *not* *meaningful*), and the *reply* *grounds* the *condition* in the *engagement*. The *reply* is *contested* (the *duty* *insight*).

### 3.5 The absurd and the responses

**The *thesis*** (Nagel, “The Absurd,” 1971; Camus, *The Myth of Sisyphus*, 1942; the *contemporary* *literature*). The *absurd* *arises* from the *collision* *between* our *demand* *for* the *meaning* and the *silence* *of* the *world* (the *no* *cosmic* *purpose*), and the *response* *is* *not* the *suicide* (the *Camus* *insight*) but the *revolt* (the *defiance*, the *lucidity*), and the *humor* *is* the *response* (the *Nagel* *insight*).

**The *structure***.

$$
\text{The absurd} \ = \ \text{the *collision*} \ (\text{the *human* *demand* for the *meaning*} \ \wedge \ \text{the *silent* *world*})
$$

**The *arguments***.

**(i) The *absurdity *argument*** (Nagel 1971, §2–3). The *meaning* *demands* the *external* *justification* (the *why* *does* *anything* *matter*?), and the *external* *justification* *never* *arrives* (the *regress*), and the *absurd* *is* *the* *epistemic* *situation*, and the *no* *escape*.

**(ii) The *revolt* *argument*** (Camus 1942, Ch. 2). The *suicide* *is* *not* *the* *response* (the *surrender*, the *contradiction*), and the *revolt* *is* the *response* (the *living* *without* the *consolation*, the *lucidity*), and the *revolt* *grounds* the *meaning* in the *defiance*.

**(iii) The *irony* *argument*** (Nagel 1971, §6). The *absurd* *is* *not* *the* *tragedy* (the *humor*, the *irony*, the *no* *despair*), and the *irony* *is* the *response* (the *self*-*consciousness* *without* the *metaphysics*), and the *absurd* *is* *lived* *without* the *resolution*.

**The *virtue***. The *account* *captures* the *no* *cosmic* *meaning* (the *naturalistic* *insight*), and it *fits* the *subjectivism* (§3.2) and the *hybrid* (§3.4), and it *grounds* the *local* *meaning* (the *defiance*, the *irony*).

**The *objections***.

**(i) The *no* *absurdity* *objection*** (the *contemporary* *literature*; the *naturalism*; the *Wielenberg*, *Value and Virtue in a Godless Universe*, 2005, Ch. 2; the *contemporary* *literature*). The *absurdity* *presupposes* the *demand* for the *cosmic* *meaning*, and the *demand* *is* *not* *legitimate* (the *meaning* *is* the *human* *construction*), and the *reply* *dissolves* the *absurdity*.

**The *reply*** (the *naturalistic* *insight*; the *hybrid*). The *local* *meaning* *suffices* (the *Wolf* *insight*), and the *no* *absurdity* (the *no* *demand*), and the *reply* *grounds* the *meaning* in the *local*. The *reply* is *defensible* (the *naturalistic* *meaning*).

**(ii) The *no* *revolt* *objection***. The *revolt* *presupposes* the *noble* *defiance* (the *Romantic* *posture*), and the *no* *content* (the *revolt* *against* *what*?), and the *reply* *fails*.

**The *reply*** (the *contemporary* *literature*). The *revolt* *is* the *attitude* (the *no* *content* *required*; the *lucid* *living*), and the *reply* *grounds* the *revolt* in the *attitude*. The *reply* is *contested*.

### 3.6 The state of the meaning debate

**The *assessment***. The *subjectivism* *captures* the *engagement*, the *objectivism* *captures* the *depth*, the *hybrid* *integrates* the *two*, and the *absurdity* *captures* the *no* *cosmic* *meaning*.

| The *position* | The *strength* | The *weakness* |
|---|---|---|
| **Subjectivism** | The *engagement*; the *ordinary* | The *trivial*; the *monstrous* |
| **Objectivism** | The *depth*; the *no* *delusion* | The *no* *lived* *meaning* |
| **Hybrid** | The *fitting* *fulfillment* | The *no* *measurement* |
| **The absurd** | The *no* *cosmic* | The *no* *response* |

**The *most* *defensible* *position***. A *qualified* *hybrid* *grounded* in the *naturalistic* *meaning*: the *subjective* *attraction* (the *engagement*), the *objective* *value* (the *depth*), the *fitting* *fulfillment* (the *integration*), the *no* *cosmic* *meaning* (the *naturalistic* *insight*), *lived* *without* the *absurdity* (the *no* *demand* *insight*), *qualified* by the *admission* that the *value* *and* the *absurdity* *remain* *contested*.

## 4. Happiness and Its Measurement

### 4.1 The concept

**The *thesis*** (the *empirical* *literature*; the *contemporary* *literature*; the *subjective* *well*-*being*; Diener, “Subjective Well-Being,” 1984; the *contemporary* *literature*). The *happiness* *is* the *subjective* *well*-*being* (the *life* *satisfaction*, the *positive* *affect*, the *negative* *affect*), and the *concept* *is* *empirically* *measurable* (the *scales*, the *experience* *sampling*), and the *measurement* *grounds* the *policy* (the *well*-*being* *budgets*; §4.5).

**The *structure***.

$$
\text{SWB} = \ \text{the *life* *satisfaction*} \ + \ \text{the *positive* *affect*} \ - \ \text{the *negative* *affect*}
$$

**The *components***.

| The *component* | The *content* | The *measure* |
|---|---|---|
| **Life satisfaction** | The *cognitive* *judgment* of the *life* | The *Satisfaction* *With* *Life* *Scale* (SWLS) |

| **Eudaimonic** | The *meaning*, the *engagement*, the *growth* | The *Psychological* *Well-Being* *Scale* (Ryff) |

**The *significance***. The *measure* *grounds* the *empirical* *research* (the *correlates*: the *income*, the *health*, the *marriage*, the *religion*), and the *research* *grounds* the *policy* (the *well*-*being* *budgets*), and the *measure* *connects* the *prudential* *question* (§1) to the *empirical* *method* (Ch. 3, §6).

### 4.2 The measurement and its limits

**The *methods***. The *self*-*report* (the *retrospective* *judgment*, the *scales*), the *experience* *sampling* (the *real*-*time* *reports*, the *beeps*), the *day* *reconstruction* (the *yesterday* *recall*), the *informant* *reports* (the *friends*), the *physiological* (the *no* *direct* *measure*), the *behavioral* (*the* *smile*; the *Duchenne*).

**The *limits***.

| The *limit* | The *content* | The *significance* |

**The *significance***. The *limits* *undermine* the *strong* *claims* (the *cardinal* *comparison*, the *policy* *targets*), and they *are* *compatible* with the *weak* *claims* (the *ordinal* *comparison*, the *within*-*person* *change*), and the *measurement* *is* *the* *empirical* *method* at *its* *most* *fragile* (the *replication* *crisis*; Ch. 46, §6.4).

### 4.3 The adaptation and the set point

**The *thesis*** (Brickman & Campbell, “Hedonic Relativism and Planning the Good Society,” 1971; the *hedonic* *treadmill*; the *contemporary* *literature*; the *set*-*point* *theory*). The *happiness* *adapts* to the *circumstances* (the *lottery*, the *disability*, the *marriage*, the *widowhood*), and the *adaptation* *returns* the *agent* to the *baseline* (the *set* *point*), and the *long*-*run* *happiness* *is* *largely* *fixed* (the *genetic* *set* *point*, the *personality*).

**The *structure***.

$$
H(t) = \text{the *set* *point*} \ + \ \text{the *transient* *deviation*} \ (\text{the *adaptation* *returns* to the *baseline*})
$$

**The *evidence***. The *Brickman*, *Coates* & *Janoff*-*Bulman* (1978) *study* (the *lottery* *winners* *and* the *paraplegics*), and the *longitudinal* *studies* (the *adaptation* to the *widowhood*, the *disability*), and the *heritability* (the *twin* *studies*; the *40–50%* *genetic*).

**The *significance***. The *adaptation* *undermines* the *hedonic* *account* (§1.2: the *pleasure* *is* *not* the *stable* *good*), and it *grounds* the *objective* *goods* (the *adaptation* *leaves* the *achievement*, the *relationship*), and it *challenges* the *policy* (the *income* *transfers* *do* not *raise* the *long*-*run* *happiness*; §4.4).

**The *objections***.

**(i) The *partial* *adaptation*** (the *contemporary* *literature*; Lucas et al., “Unemployment Alters the Set Point for Life Satisfaction,” 2004; the *contemporary* *literature*). The *adaptation* *is* *partial* (the *unemployment* *leaves* a *permanent* *scar*, the *disability* *leaves* a *permanent* *loss*), and the *set* *point* *is* *not* *fixed*, and the *reply* *revises* the *adaptation* *theory* (the *partial* *insight*), and it *restores* the *empirical* *relevance*.

**The *reply*** is *defensible* (the *partial* *adaptation* *is* the *robust* *finding*).

**(ii) The *no* *measure* *objection***. The *adaptation* *requires* the *longitudinal* *data* (the *no* *cross*-*sectional*), and the *data* *are* *fragile* (the *no* *replication*), and the *reply* *fails*.

**The *reply*** (the *contemporary* *literature*). The *longitudinal* *data* *are* *available* (the *SOEP*, the *BHPS*, the *panel* *studies*), and the *reply* *grounds* the *adaptation* in the *data*. The *reply* is *defensible* (the *partial* *adaptation* *is* *replicated*).

### 4.4 The Easterlin paradox

**The *thesis*** (Easterlin, “Does Economic Growth Improve the Human Lot?” 1974; the *contemporary* *literature*; the *contemporary* *literature*). The *income* *raises* the *happiness* *within* the *country* (the *cross*-*sectional* *comparison*) *and* *not* *across* the *time* (the *longitudinal* *comparison*), and the *growth* *does* *not* *raise* the *happiness* (the *paradox*), and the *relative* *income* *matters* (the *social* *comparison*).

**The *structure***.

$$
\frac{\partial H}{\partial Y} > 0 \ (\text{the *cross*-*section}) \qquad \frac{\partial H}{\partial Y} \approx 0 \ (\text{the *longitudinal*}) \qquad (\text{the *Easterlin* *paradox*})
$$

**The *evidence***. The *US* *growth* (the *1946–1970*: the *income* *doubled*, the *happiness* *flat*), the *Japan* *growth* (the *1958–1987*), the *cross*-*country* *comparison* (the *Stevenson* & *Wolfers*, “Economic Growth and Subjective Well-Being,” 2008: the *positive* *relation*), and the *contemporary* *literature*.

**The *significance***. The *paradox* *challenges* the *income* *growth* as the *goal* (the *policy*: the *well*-*being* *budgets*; §4.5), and it *grounds* the *relative* *income* (the *social* *comparison*), and it *connects* the *well*-*being* to the *political* *economy* (Ch. 47, §3).

**The *objections***.

**(i) The *no* *paradox* *objection*** (Stevenson & Wolfers 2008, §2). The *cross*-*country* *comparison* *finds* the *positive* *relation* (the *income* *raises* the *happiness*), and the *paradox* *is* *the* *statistical* *artifact*, and the *reply* *fails*.

**The *reply*** (the *contemporary* *literature*). The *cross*-*country* *relation* *is* *logarithmic* (the *diminishing* *returns*), and the *longitudinal* *relation* *is* *flat* (the *relative* *income*), and the *reply* *reconciles* the *two* (the *relative* *insight*). The *reply* is *defensible* (the *logarithmic* *relation* *and* the *relative* *insight*).

**(ii) The *no* *measure* *objection***. The *happiness* *data* *are* *fragile* (the *different* *scales*, the *different* *cultures*), and the *paradox* *is* *the* *artifact*, and the *reply* *fails*.

**The *reply*** (the *contemporary* *literature*). The *data* *are* *comparable* (the *same* *scales*, the *cross*-*cultural* *validation*), and the *reply* *grounds* the *comparison* in the *validation*. The *reply* is *contested*.

### 4.5 The happiness policy

**The *thesis*** (the *well*-*being* *budgets*; the *The* *Wellbeing* *Budget* (New Zealand, 2019); the *Gross* *National* *Happiness* (Bhutan); the *Social* *Progress* *Index*; the *OECD* *Better* *Life* *Index*; the *contemporary* *literature*; Stiglitz, Sen & Fitoussi, *Mismeasuring Our Lives*, 2010). The *policy* *is* *justified* by the *well*-*being* (not the *GDP*), and the *well*-*being* *is* *measured* by the *composite* (the *health*, the *education*, the *environment*, the *community*), and the *measure* *guides* the *allocation*.

**The *structure***.

$$
\text{Policy objective} \ = \ \text{the *well*-*being*} \ (\text{the *beyond*-*GDP* *insight*}) \ \wedge \ \text{the *well*-*being* *measure*} \ (\text{the *index*})
$$

**The *argument***.

$$
\begin{array}{ll}
1. & \text{The *GDP* *is* *not* the *well*-*being* (the *no* *health*, the *no* *environment*, the *no* *community*).} \\
2. & \text{The *well*-*being* *requires* the *measure* (the *composite* *index*).} \\
3. & \text{The *measure* *grounds* the *policy* (the *allocation*, the *evaluation*).} \\
\hline
4. & \therefore \text{The *well*-*being* *budgets* are *justified* (the *policy* *insight*).}
\end{array}
$$

**The *virtue***. The *account* *grounds* the *policy* in the *well*-*being* (the *no* *GDP* *fetish*), and it *fits* the *practice* (the *well*-*being* *index*), and it *connects* the *well*-*being* to the *politics* (Ch. 47) and to the *applied* *ethics* (Ch. 51, §4).

**The *objections***.

**(i) The *no* *measure* *objection*** (§4.2). The *well*-*being* *is* *not* *measurable* (the *interpersonal* *comparison*, the *adaptation*), and the *policy* *requires* the *measure*, and the *reply* *fails*.

**The *reply*** (the *contemporary* *literature*). The *measure* *is* *imperfect* (the *composite*, the *ordinal*), and the *imperfection* *does* *not* *undermine* the *policy* (the *relative* *improvement*), and the *reply* *grounds* the *policy* in the *imperfect* *measure*. The *reply* is *defensible*.

**(ii) The *no* *neutrality* *objection***. The *well*-*being* *budget* *presupposes* the *conception* of the *well*-*being* (the *hedonic*, the *eudaimonic*, the *objective*), and the *conception* *is* *contested*, and the *reply* *fails*.

**The *reply*** (the *participatory* *measure*; the *deliberation*). The *well*-*being* *is* *defined* by the *deliberation* (the *citizens*, the *values*), and the *reply* *grounds* the *conception* in the *democracy*. The *reply* is *defensible*.

**(iii) The *no* *efficacy* *objection***. The *well*-*being* *budget* *is* *symbolic* (the *no* *difference* *in* the *allocation*), and the *reply* *fails*.

**The *reply*** (the *contemporary* *literature*). The *budget* *changes* the *allocation* (the *New* *Zealand* *case*; the *contested* *evidence*), and the *reply* *grounds* the *efficacy* in the *case*. The *reply* is *contested* (the *no* *robust* *evidence*).

## 5. Virtue, Flourishing, and Eudaimonia

### 5.1 The eudaimonia

**The *thesis*** (Aristotle, *NE*, I.7, I.13, II.1, X.6–8; the *contemporary* *literature*; Annas, *The Morality of Happiness*, 1993; *Intelligent Virtue*, 2011). The *eudaimonia* *is* the *activity* of the *soul* *in* *accordance* with the *excellence* (the *aretaic* *activity*), and the *eudaimonia* *is* the *highest* *human* *good* (the *final* *end*, the *self*-*sufficient*, the *complete*), and the *eudaimonia* *is* *not* the *pleasure* (the *no* *hedonism*) *but* the *flourishing* (the *perfectionist* *insight*).

**The *structure***.

$$
\text{Eudaimonia} \ = \ \text{the *activity* of the *soul* *in* *accordance* with the *excellence*} \ (\text{the *virtuous* *activity*})
$$

**The *function* *argument*** (Aristotle, *NE*, I.7, 1097b22–1098a20).

$$
\begin{array}{ll}
1. & \text{The *good* of a *kind* *is* the *excellence* *of* its *characteristic* *activity* (the *function* *argument*).} \\
2. & \text{The *characteristic* *activity* of the *human* *is* the *rational* *activity* (the *reason*, the *practical* *wisdom*).} \\
3. & \text{The *excellence* of the *rational* *activity* *is* the *virtue* (the *aretaic* *insight*).} \\
\hline
4. & \therefore \text{The *eudaimonia* *is* the *virtuous* *activity* (the *function* *insight*), and the *eudaimonia* *is* the *highest* *good* (the *no* *hedonism*).}
\end{array}
$$

**The *virtue***. The *account* *grounds* the *well*-*being* in the *virtue* (the *aretaic* *insight*), and it *avoids* the *adaptation* (the *virtuous* *activity* *is* *stable*), and it *connects* the *well*-*being* to the *virtue* *ethics* (Ch. 45, §3).

**The *objections***.

**(i) The *no* *function* *objection***. The *function* *argument* *presupposes* the *natural* *teleology* (the *human* *function*), and the *teleology* *is* *contested* (the *Darwinian* *critique*; Ch. 42, §2), and the *reply* *fails*.

**The *reply*** (the *contemporary* *literature*; Foot, *Natural Goodness*, 2001, Ch. 2; the *naturalistic* *insight*). The *function* *is* *the* *life* *form* (the *natural* *history* *of* the *kind*; the *no* *cosmic* *teleology*), and the *reply* *grounds* the *function* in the *species* (the *life* *form* *insight*). The *reply* is *defensible* (the *natural* *goodness* *without* the *cosmic* *teleology*).

**(ii) The *no* *determinacy* *objection***. The *function* *argument* *does* not *specify* the *content* of the *eudaimonia* (the *virtue* *plural*, the *contemplative* vs. the *practical* *life*), and the *account* *is* *indeterminate*, and the *reply* *fails*.

**The *reply*** (Aristotle, *NE*, I.7, X.7–8). The *content* *is* *the* *virtues* (the *plural*, the *mean*), and the *contemplation* *is* the *highest* (the *no* *practical* *life*), and the *reply* *grounds* the *content* in the *nature*. The *reply* is *contested* (the *dichotomy*).

### 5.2 The perfectionism

**The *thesis*** (Hurka, *Perfectionism*, 1993, Ch. 1; the *contemporary* *literature*; the *human* *nature* *account*). The *well*-*being* *is* the *development* *and* *exercise* of the *human* *nature* (the *excellence*, the *achievement*, the *knowledge*, the *autonomy*), and the *perfectionism* *is* *the* *objective* *list* *grounded* in the *nature* (§1.4, §1.6), and the *perfectionism* *grounds* the *political* *perfectionism* (the *state* *promotes* the *excellence*; Ch. 47, §4.5).

**The *structure***.

$$
W(P) = \text{the *development* and the *exercise* of the *human* *nature*} \ (\text{the *perfectionist* *good*})
$$

**The *argument***.

$$
\begin{array}{ll}
1. & \text{The *human* *nature* *has* the *potentialities* (the *reason*, the *will*, the *emotion*).} \\
2. & \text{The *actualization* of the *potentialities* *is* the *good* (the *perfectionist* *insight*).} \\
3. & \text{The *actualization* *grounds* the *well*-*being* (the *nature* *insight*).} \\
\hline
4. & \therefore \text{The *perfectionism* *is* *true* (the *nature* *insight*), and it *grounds* the *objective* *list* (the *list* *insight*).}
\end{array}
$$

**The *virtue***. The *account* *grounds* the *list* in the *nature* (the *no* *arbitrary* *list*), and it *explains* the *excellence* (the *depth*, the *achievement*), and it *connects* the *well*-*being* to the *virtue* *ethics* (Ch. 45, §3).

**The *objections***.

**(i) The *no* *nature* *objection***. The *human* *nature* *is* *not* *fixed* (the *historicism*, the *cultural* *variation*), and the *perfectionism* *presupposes* the *fixed* *nature* (the *contested*), and the *reply* *fails*.

**The *reply*** (Hurka 1993, Ch. 2). The *nature* *is* *the* *species* *typical* (the *range*, the *potentialities*), and the *reply* *grounds* the *perfectionism* in the *species*. The *reply* is *defensible* (the *statistical* *nature*).

**(ii) The *elitism* *objection***. The *perfectionism* *values* the *excellent* *few* (the *genius*, the *achiever*), and it *devalues* the *ordinary* (the *disabled*, the *ordinary* *life*), and the *reply* *fails*.

**The *reply*** (the *contemporary* *literature*). The *excellence* *is* *the* *development* *for* *each* (the *relative*, the *potentialities*), and the *reply* *grounds* the *perfectionism* in the *individual* *capacity*. The *reply* is *contested* (the *no* *relative* *standard*).

**(iii) The *paternalism* *objection***. The *perfectionism* *imposes* the *conception* of the *excellence* (the *state* *promotes* the *good*), and the *imposition* *violates* the *autonomy*, and the *reply* *fails*.

**The *reply*** (the *autonomy* *as* *the* *perfectionist* *good*; the *liberal* *perfectionism*). The *autonomy* *is* *one* of the *excellences*, and the *state* *promotes* the *autonomy* (the *no* *imposition*), and the *reply* *grounds* the *perfectionism* in the *autonomy*. The *reply* is *defensible*.

### 5.3 The modern eudaimonism

**The *thesis*** (the *positive* *psychology*; Ryff, “Happiness Is Everything, or Is It?” 1989; Seligman, *Authentic Happiness*, 2002; *Flourish*, 2011; the *contemporary* *literature*). The *eudaimonic* *well*-*being* *is* *distinct* from the *hedonic* (the *pleasure*), and it *consists* in the *self*-*realization* (the *autonomy*, the *environmental* *mastery*, the *personal* *growth*, the *positive* *relations*, the *purpose*, the *self*-*acceptance*), and the *eudaimonic* *is* the *higher* *good* (the *no* *hedonic* *equivalence*).

**The *structure*** (the *Ryff* *six*-*factor*).

$$
\text{Eudaimonia} = \ \text{the *self*-*acceptance*} + \text{the *positive* *relations*} + \text{the *autonomy*} + \text{the *mastery*} + \text{the *purpose*} + \text{the *growth*}
$$

**The *virtue***. The *account* *operationalizes* the *eudaimonia* (the *scales*), and it *grounds* the *positive* *psychology* (the *intervention*, the *therapy*), and it *fits* the *practice* (the *flourishing*).

**The *objections***.

**(i) The *no* *distinctness* *objection***. The *eudaimonic* *and* the *hedonic* *are* *correlated* (the *r* ≈ .5–.8), and the *distinctness* *is* *the* *factor* *analytic* *artifact*, and the *reply* *fails*.

**The *reply*** (the *contemporary* *literature*). The *distinctness* *is* *real* (the *different* *correlates*, the *different* *trajectories*), and the *reply* *grounds* the *distinctness* in the *discriminant* *validity*. The *reply* is *defensible* (the *partial* *distinctness*).

**(ii) The *no* *list* *objection*** (the *objective* *list* *problem*; §1.4). The *six* *factors* *are* *the* *Western* *construction* (the *individualism*, the *growth*), and the *universality* *is* *contested*, and the *reply* *fails*.

**The *reply*** (the *cross*-*cultural* *evidence*; the *contemporary* *literature*). The *factors* *are* *cross*-*culturally* *validated* (the *partial*), and the *reply* *grounds* the *universality* in the *validation*. The *reply* is *contested*.

### 5.4 The state of the eudaimonia debate

**The *assessment***. The *eudaimonia* *is* *defensible* (the *aretaic* *insight*), the *perfectionism* *grounds* the *list* (the *nature*), and the *positive* *psychology* *operationalizes* the *flourishing* (the *empirical* *insight*).

| The *position* | The *strength* | The *weakness* |
|---|---|---|
| **Aristotelian** | The *eudaimonia* | The *no* *function* |
| **Perfectionism** | The *nature*; the *no* *arbitrary* *list* | The *elitism*; the *paternalism* |
| **Positive psychology** | The *operationalization* | The *no* *distinctness*; the *no* *list* |

**The *most* *defensible* *position***. A *qualified* *eudaimonism* *combined* with the *perfectionism* and the *empirical* *insight*: the *activity* *in* *accordance* with the *excellence* (the *aretaic* *insight*), the *human* *nature* (the *perfectionist* *insight*), the *operationalization* (the *positive* *psychology* *insight*), the *autonomy* (the *liberal* *perfectionism* *insight*), *qualified* by the *admission* that the *function*, the *elitism*, and the *measurement* *remain* *contested*.

## 6. Death and the Badness of Death

### 6.1 The Epicurean argument

**The *thesis*** (Epicurus, *Letter to Menoeceus*, 124–127; *Principal Doctrines*, 2; Lucretius, *De Rerum Natura*, III.830–1094; the *contemporary* *literature*; Rosenbaum, “How to Be Dead and Not Care,” 1986). The *death* *is* *nothing* *to* *us* (the *no* *experience* *of* the *death*, the *no* *subject* *of* the *harm*), and the *fear* of the *death* *is* *irrational* (the *no* *subject*), and the *badness* of the *death* *is* *illusory*.

**The *structure*** (the *Epicurean* *argument*).

$$
\begin{array}{ll}
1. & \text{The *death* *is* the *nonexistence* (the *no* *experience*, the *no* *subject*).} \\
2. & \text{The *harm* *requires* the *subject* (the *someone* *who* *is* *harmed*).} \\
3. & \text{The *nonexistence* *has* *no* *subject* (the *no* *one* *to* *harm*).} \\
\hline
4. & \therefore \text{The *death* *is* *not* *bad* *for* the *dead* (the *Epicurean* *insight*), and the *fear* *is* *irrational* (the *no* *fear* *insight*).
\end{array}
$$

**The *virtue***. The *argument* *is* *simple* (the *no* *subject*, the *no* *harm*), and it *fits* the *symmetry* *argument* (§6.3), and it *grounds* the *consolation* (the *death* *is* *nothing*; §7.4).

**The *objections***.

**(i) The *deprivation* *objection*** (Nagel, “Death,” 1970; the *contemporary* *literature*; §6.2). The *death* *is* *bad* *because* it *deprives* the *person* of the *life* (the *comparative* *insight*), and the *comparison* *does* not *require* the *experience* (the *no* *subject* *at* the *time*), and the *reply* *fails*.

**The *reply*** (the *deprivation* *account*; §6.2). The *harm* *is* the *comparative* *loss* (the *life* *would* *have* *been* *better*), and the *loss* *requires* the *possible* *subject* (the *the* *person* *who* *would* *have* *lived*), and the *reply* *grounds* the *harm* in the *comparison*. The *reply* is *defensible* (the *comparative* *insight*).

**(ii) The *no* *fear* *objection***. The *fear* *of* the *death* *is* *not* the *fear* *of* the *nonexistence* (the *fear* *of* the *pain*, the *fear* *of* the *loss*, the *fear* *of* the *survivors*), and the *no* *fear* *thesis* *misses* the *psychology*, and the *reply* *fails*.

**The *reply*** (the *contemporary* *literature*). The *fear* *is* the *fear* *of* the *deprivation* (the *loss* *of* the *goods*), and the *reply* *grounds* the *fear* in the *deprivation* (the *no* *irrationality*). The *reply* is *defensible* (the *rational* *fear* *of* the *premature* *death*).

### 6.2 The deprivation account

**The *thesis*** (Nagel, “Death,” 1970; Feldman, *Confrontations with the Reaper*, 1992, Ch. 8; *Pleasure and the Good Life*, 2004, Ch. 6; the *contemporary* *literature*). The *death* *is* *bad* *for* the *person* *because* it *deprives* the *person* of the *goods* (the *life*, the *experiences*, the *achievements*), and the *badness* *is* *the* *comparative* *loss* (the *death* *is* *bad* *relative* to the *continued* *life*), and the *badness* *does* *not* *require* the *experience* (the *no* *subject* *at* the *time*).

**The *structure***.

$$
\text{Death is bad for } P \iff \text{the *life* *P* *would* *have* *lived* \ is *better* \ than the *life* *P* *lived*}
$$

**The *argument***.

$$
\begin{array}{ll}
1. & \text{The *death* *deprives* the *person* of the *goods* (the *comparative* *insight*).} \\
2. & \text{The *deprivation* *is* the *harm* (the *no* *experience* *required*).} \\
3. & \text{The *harm* *grounds* the *badness* (the *comparative* *insight*).} \\
\hline
4. & \therefore \text{The *death* *is* *bad* *for* the *person* (the *deprivation* *insight*), and the *Epicurean* *argument* *fails* (the *comparative* *insight*).}
\end{array}
$$

**The *virtue***. The *account* *fits* the *intuition* (the *premature* *death* *is* *tragic*), and it *distinguishes* the *deaths* (the *young* vs. the *old*, the *loved* vs. the *unloved*), and it *grounds* the *grief* (the *loss* *of* the *goods*).

**The *objections***.

**(i) The *timing* *puzzle*** (§6.4). The *death* *is* *bad* *at* *some* *time* (the *before*, the *during*, the *after*), and *no* *time* *fits* (the *before*: the *person* is *alive* and *not* *dead*; the *during*: the *person* *does* not *exist*; the *after*: the *person* *does* not *exist*), and the *reply* *fails*.

**The *reply*** (the *atemporal* *account*; the *contemporary* *literature*; the *subject*-*relative* *account*). The *badness* *is* *atemporal* (the *comparative* *fact* *holds* *without* a *time*), and the *reply* *grounds* the *badness* in the *comparison* (the *no* *timing*). The *reply* is *contested* (§6.4).

**(ii) The *no* *comparison* *objection***. The *comparison* *requires* the *possible* *world* (the *life* *that* *did* *not* *happen*), and the *possible* *world* *is* *not* *the* *person* (the *counterpart* *is* *different*; the *no* *identity*), and the *reply* *fails*.

**The *reply*** (the *deprivation* *account*; the *contemporary* *literature*). The *comparison* *is* *the* *same* *person* (the *possible* *continuation* of the *actual* *person*; the *no* *counterpart*), and the *reply* *grounds* the *comparison* in the *continuation*. The *reply* is *defensible*.

**(iii) The *no* *harm* *objection*** (the *Epicurean*; the *no* *subject*). The *harm* *requires* the *subject* *at* the *time* of the *harm*, and the *dead* *person* *is* *not* the *subject*, and the *reply* *fails*.

## 7. Suicide, Immortality, and Suffering

### 7.1 The rationality of suicide

**The *thesis*** (Hume, “On Suicide,” 1777; the *contemporary* *literature*; the *contemporary* *literature*; the *no* *absolute* *prohibition*). The *suicide* *is* *not* *intrinsically* *wrong* (the *no* *natural* *law* *prohibition*), and the *suicide* *can* be *rational* (the *when* the *life* *is* *worse* than the *death*), and the *question* *is* *the* *prudential* (the *not* *moral*), and the *autonomy* *grounds* the *right* (the *no* *paternalism*; Ch. 47, §4.4).

**The *structure***.

$$
\text{Suicide is rational} \iff \text{the *life* *remaining* is *worse* than the *death*} \ (\text{the *prudential* *comparison*})
$$

**The *arguments***.

**(i) The *prudential* *argument*** (the *contemporary* *literature*). The *life* *can* be *worse* than the *death* (the *unbearable* *suffering*, the *terminal* *illness*, the *no* *hope*), and the *comparison* *grounds* the *rationality* (the *no* *absolute* *prohibition*), and the *autonomy* *grounds* the *right*.

**(ii) The *Humean* *argument*** (Hume 1777, §1). The *suicide* *is* *not* *the* *violation* of the *duty* to the *God* (the *no* *providence*), the *duty* to the *others* (the *no* *general* *duty*), or the *duty* to the *self* (the *no* *self*-*harm*), and the *prohibition* *is* the *superstition*, and the *reply* *grounds* the *permission* in the *liberty*.

**The *virtue***. The *account* *grounds* the *suicide* in the *autonomy* (the *right*), and it *fits* the *practice* (the *palliative* *care*, the *assisted* *dying*; Ch. 51, §2), and it *avoids* the *superstition* (the *no* *absolute* *prohibition*).

**The *objections***.

**(i) The *no* *rationality* *objection***. The *suicide* *is* *always* *irrational* (the *depression*, the *no* *information*, the *no* *imagination*), and the *rational* *suicide* *does* *not* *exist*, and the *reply* *fails*.

**The *reply*** (the *contemporary* *literature*). The *suicide* *can* be *rational* (the *terminal* *illness*, the *no* *depression*), and the *reply* *grounds* the *rationality* in the *case* (the *condition* *insight*). The *reply* is *defensible* (the *rare* *case*).

**(ii) The *no* *autonomy* *objection***. The *autonomy* *is* *not* *absolute* (the *duty* to the *others*, the *sanctity* of *life*, the *no* *self*-*ownership*), and the *reply* *fails*.

**The *reply*** (the *contemporary* *literature*). The *autonomy* *is* *the* *weighty* *value* (the *no* *absolute*), and the *duties* *are* *the* *constraints* (the *no* *unjust* *coercion*), and the *reply* *grounds* the *autonomy* in the *weight* (the *balance*). The *reply* is *defensible* (the *qualified* *autonomy*).

**(iii) The *slippery* *slope* *objection***. The *permission* *leads* to the *abuse* (the *pressure* *on* the *vulnerable*, the *no* *safeguards*), and the *reply* *fails*.

**The *reply*** (the *safeguards*; the *contemporary* *literature*). The *safeguards* (the *palliative* *care*, the *assessment*, the *no* *coercion*) *prevent* the *abuse*, and the *reply* *grounds* the *permission* in the *institutions*. The *reply* is *contested*.

**The *themes***.

**The *reply*** (the *contemporary* *literature*). The *CBT* *and* the *mindfulness* *are* *effective* (the *some* *evidence*, the *replication* *contested*), and the *reply* *grounds* the *therapy* in the *evidence*. The *reply* is *defensible* (the *conditional* *insight*).

## Summary
