# Chapter 52 — The Limits of Philosophical Knowledge

> “The value of philosophy is, in fact, to be sought largely in its very uncertainty. The man who has no tincture of philosophy goes through life imprisoned in the prejudices derived from common sense, from the habitual beliefs of his age or his nation, and from convictions which have grown up in his mind without the co-operation or consent of his deliberate reason.”
> — Bertrand Russell, *The Problems of Philosophy* (1912), ch. XV

> “My propositions serve as elucidations in the following way: anyone who understands me eventually recognizes them as nonsensical, when he has used them — as steps — to climb up beyond them. (He must, so to speak, throw away the ladder after he has climbed up it.)”
> — Ludwig Wittgenstein, *Tractatus Logico-Philosophicus* (1921), 6.54

> “What is your aim in philosophy? — To shew the fly the way out of the fly-bottle.”
> — Ludwig Wittgenstein, *Philosophical Investigations* (1953), §309

> “The acceptance or rejection of abstract linguistic frameworks … is a practical decision, comparable to the decision whether to use the metric or the English system of measurement.”
> — Rudolf Carnap, “Empiricism, Semantics, and Ontology” (1950)

> “Epistemology, or something like it, simply falls into place as a chapter of psychology and hence of natural science.”
> — W. V. O. Quine, “Epistemology Naturalized” (1969)

---

Chapter 51 examined the *application* — the *case*, the *decision*, the *stakes*. This chapter examines the *record* — *what* the *discipline* has *established*, *dissolved*, *failed* to *settle*, and *mis*-*posed*, and *what* the *answer* *implies* for the *authority* of the *whole*.

The *transition* is the *inversion* of the *applied* *turn*. The *applied* *ethics* *tests* the *theory* *by* the *case* (§51.1); the *meta*-*question* *tests* the *enterprise* *by* its *record* (the *no* *mere* *case*). The *applied* *ethicist* *acts* *inside* the *doubt* (§51.8); the *meta*-*philosopher* *must* *say* *whether* the *doubt* *is* the *condition* of a *knowledge* or the *admission* of a *failure*. And the *answer* *determines* the *authority* of the *discipline* (the *no* *mere* *sociology*) and the *tenor* of the *whole* *book* (the *no* *mere* *catalog*).

The *chapter* proceeds in *eight* *movements*. §1 states the *question* and *reconsiders* Russell’s *Chapter* *XIV*. §2 examines the *therapeutic* *conception* (Wittgenstein). §3 examines the *internal*/*external* *distinction* (Carnap). §4 examines the *naturalism* (Quine and after). §5 examines the *diagnosis* of the *pseudo*-*problem* (the *quietism*, the *deflationism*). §6 examines the *experimental* *challenge*. §7 gives the *ledger* (the *settled*, the *dissolved*, the *open*, the *mis*-*posed*). §8 gives the *verdict*.

## 1. The Question and Russell’s Chapter XIV

### 1.1 The structure of the question

**The *question***. The *limit* *question* (*what* *can* the *philosophy* *know*?) is *ambiguous* between the *four* *senses*, and the *failure* to *distinguish* them *produces* the *confusion* (the *no* *single* *question*).

**The *four* *senses***.

| The *sense* | The *content* | The *test* |
|---|---|---|
| **The *scope*** | *Which* *questions* are *answerable* | The *decidability* |
| **The *method*** | *Which* *instruments* *work* | The *reliability* |
| **The *authority*** | *Whose* *claim* *binds* | The *warrant* |
| **The *progress*** | *Which* *questions* *get* *settled* | The *convergence* |

**The *significance***. The *senses* *come* *apart*: the *philosophy* *may* *have* the *scope* (the *questions* *are* *legitimate*) and *lack* the *progress* (the *no* *convergence*), and it *may* *have* the *method* (the *argument*, the *formalization*) and *lack* the *authority* (the *no* *binding* *verdict*), and the *verdict* *depends* on the *sense* (the *no* *single* *verdict*).

**The *criteria***. The *discipline* *is* *assessed* by the *criteria* of Ch. 4, §3 (the *consistency*, the *explanatory* *power*, the *simplicity*, the *conservatism*, the *adequacy*), *applied* *reflexively* (the *no* *exception*), and the *relevant* *markers* are the *five*.

| The *marker* | The *content* | The *significance* |
|---|---|---|
| **The *convergence*** | The *agreement* of the *competent* *inquirers* | The *no* *mere* *sociology* |
| **The *cumulativeness*** | The *retention* of the *results* | The *no* *mere* *fashion* |
| **The *error*-*correction*** | The *mechanism* of the *repair* | The *no* *mere* *dogma* |
| **The *transfer*** | The *export* of the *results* (the *logic*, the *ethics*) | The *no* *mere* *insulation* |
| **The *institutional*** | The *robustness* of the *practice* (the *journals*, the *training*) | The *no* *mere* *individual* |

**The *problem***. The *markers* *pull* *apart*: the *transfer* *favors* the *settled* *results* (the *logic*, the *philosophy* of *language*), the *convergence* *favors* the *sciences* (the *no* *parity*), the *institutional* *favors* the *philosophy* (the *no* *mere* *failure*), and the *cumulativeness* *is* the *contested* (the *no* *linear* *progress*).

### 1.2 Russell’s Chapter XIV reconsidered

**The *thesis*** (Russell 1912, ch. XIV–XV; the *contemporary* *literature*; the *no* *mere* *optimism*). Russell *concludes* that the *philosophy* *yields* the *probable* *opinions* (the *no* *certainty*), and the *value* *lies* in the *uncertainty* itself (the *enlargement* of the *self*, the *escape* from the *custom*), and the *knowledge* *is* *not* the *goal* (the *no* *mere* *result*), and the *conclusion* is *both* *too* *pessimistic* (the *no* *mere* *doubt*) and *too* *optimistic* (the *no* *mere* *union*).

**The *structure*** (the *Russell* *form*).

$$
\begin{array}{ll}
1. & \text{The *central* *questions* *remain* *disputed*} \ (\text{the *no* *convergence*}). \\
2. & \text{The *discipline* *without* *settled* *answers* *yields* the *no* *knowledge*} \ (\text{the *Russell* *pessimism*}). \\
3. & \text{The *value* *lies* in the *contemplation*, *not* in the *result*} \ (\text{the *union* *insight*}). \\
\hline
4. & \therefore \text{The *philosophy* *gives* the *probable* *opinions*} \ (\text{the *no* *certainty*}), \ \text{and the *value* is the *practical*} \ (\text{the *enlargement*}), \ \text{and the *knowledge* *is* *not* the *goal*} \ (\text{the *no* *mere} \ \text{result*}).}
\end{array}
$$

**The *virtue***. The *chapter* *captures* the *uncertainty* (the *no* *mere* *dogma*), and it *defends* the *discipline* *against* the *utility* *demand* (the *no* *mere* *instrument*), and it *grounds* the *value* in the *intellectual* *virtue* (the *no* *mere* *result*), and it *connects* the *verdict* to the *ethics* *of* *belief* (Ch. 4, §4).

**The *objections***.

**(i) The *no* *knowledge* *objection*** (the *contemporary* *literature*; the *contemporary* *literature*). The *philosophy* *has* *knowledge* (the *logic*, the *semantics*, the *formal* *epistemology*, the *parts* of the *philosophy* of *science*), and the *no* *convergence* *misses* the *local* *results* (the *Gödel* *theorems*, the *theory* of *descriptions*), and the *reply* *fails*.

**The *reply*** (the *contemporary* *literature*; the *local* *insight*). The *knowledge* *is* *local* (the *no* *global* *consensus*), and the *reply* *grounds* the *verdict* in the *local* *results* (the *no* *mere* *pessimism*). The *reply* is *defensible* (the *local* *insight*), and *contested* (the *no* *global* *cumulation*).

**(ii) The *no* *union* *objection*** (the *contemporary* *literature*). The *union* *with* the *not*-*self* *is* the *aesthetico*-*mystical* (the *no* *mere* *argument*), and the *value* *is* *asserted* *not* *established* (the *no* *mere* *ground*), and the *reply* *fails*.

**The *reply*** (the *contemporary* *literature*; the *no* *mere* *consolation*). The *value* *is* the *reflective* (the *no* *mere* *mysticism*), and the *reply* *grounds* the *value* in the *practice* (the *no* *mere* *feeling*). The *reply* is *contested* (the *no* *argument*).

**(iii) The *no* *pessimism* *objection***. The *philosophy* *has* *progressed* (the *no* *mere* *uncertainty*), and the *Russell* *verdict* *dates* (the *no* *mere* *1912*), and the *reply* *fails*.

**The *reply*** (the *contemporary* *literature*). The *progress* *is* *local* (the *no* *global*), and the *reply* *grounds* the *verdict* in the *local* *progress* (the *no* *mere* *pessimism*). The *reply* is *defensible*.

### 1.3 The four diagnoses

**The *diagnoses***. The *limit* *question* *yields* the *four* *families* of *answer*, and the *families* *differ* on the *locus* of the *failure* (the *no* *mere* *skepticism*).

| The *diagnosis* | The *core* *claim* | The *locus* of the *failure* |
|---|---|---|
| **The *dissolution*** | The *problems* are *pseudo* (the *no* *content*) | The *question* |
| **The *naturalism*** | The *method* is *defective* (the *no* *armchair*) | The *instrument* |
| **The *pessimism*** | The *progress* is *absent* (the *no* *convergence*) | The *outcome* |
| **The *debunking*** | The *authority* is *illusory* (the *no* *warrant*) | The *ground* |

**The *significance***. The *diagnoses* *imply* the *different* *remedies* (the *therapy*, the *science*, the *humility*, the *suspicion*), and the *verdict* *depends* on the *diagnosis* (the *no* *single* *answer*), and the *chapter* *weighs* the *four* (§2–§6).

**The *argument***.

$$
\begin{array}{ll}
1. & \text{The *limit* *question* *admits* of the *four* *loci* \ (\text{the *question*, the *method*, the *outcome*, the *ground*}).} \\
2. & \text{The *loci* *yield* the *four* *diagnoses* \ (\text{the *no* *single* *failure*}).} \\

\hline

\end{array}
$$

### 1.4 The state of the question

**The *assessment***. The *question* *is* *not* the *single* (the *four* *senses*), and the *verdict* *is* *not* the *single* (the *four* *diagnoses*), and the *failure* to *distinguish* them is the *source* of the *rhetoric* (the *no* *mere* *opinion*).

| The *verdict* | The *strength* | The *weakness* |
|---|---|---|

| **The *dissolution*** | The *pseudo*-*problems* | The *no* *criterion* |
| **The *naturalism*** | The *continuity* | The *no* *normativity* |

**The *most* *defensible* *position***. A *qualified* *localism*: the *philosophy* *has* *knowledge* *locally* (the *no* *mere* *pessimism*) and *lacks* the *convergence* *globally* (the *no* *mere* *optimism*), and the *verdict* *must* *be* *stated* *per* *question* (the *no* *single* *verdict*), *qualified* by the *admission* that the *scope*, the *method*, the *authority*, and the *progress* *remain* *contested*.

## 2. Wittgenstein’s Ladder and the Therapeutic Conception

### 2.1 The *Tractatus* and the ladder

**The *thesis*** (Wittgenstein, *Tractatus*, 1921, 6.54; 4.003; the *contemporary* *literature*; the *no* *mere* *doctrine*). The *philosophy* *is* the *activity* of the *clarification* (the *no* *mere* *science*), and the *propositions* of the *Tractatus* *are* the *elucidations* (the *no* *mere* *theses*), and the *elucidation* *is* *abandoned* *once* the *climb* is *complete* (the *ladder* *insight*), and the *unsayable* *shows* *itself* (the *no* *mere* *statement*).

**The *structure***.

$$
\text{The *elucidation*} \rightarrow \text{the *insight*} \rightarrow \text{the *discarding*} \ (\text{the *no* *residue* of the *proposition*})
$$

**The *argument*** (Wittgenstein 1921, 6.54).

$$
\begin{array}{ll}
1. & \text{The *philosophy* *clarifies* the *thought} \ (\text{the *no* *mere* *doctrine*}). \\
2. & \text{The *clarification* *uses* the *propositions*} \ (\text{the *no* *mere* *content*}). \\
3. & \text{The *propositions* *are* *discarded* *with* the *climb*} \ (\text{the *ladder* *insight*}). \\
\hline
4. & \therefore \text{The *philosophy* *ends* *in* the *silence*} \ (\text{the *no* *mere} \ \text{result*}), \ \text{and the *insight* *is* the *showing*} \ (\text{the *no* *mere} \ \text{saying*}), \ \text{and the *no* *doctrine* *is* the *point*} \ (\text{the *no* *mere} \ \text{thesis*}).}
\end{array}
$$

**The *virtue***. The *account* *captures* the *activity* (the *no* *mere* *system*), and it *resists* the *scientism* (the *no* *mere* *theory*), and it *grounds* the *survey* of the *grammar* (the *no* *mere* *content*), and it *connects* the *philosophy* to the *practice* (the *no* *mere* *contemplation*).

**The *objections***.

**(i) The *no* *content* *objection***. The *ladder* *is* *self*-*refuting* (the *no* *nonsense* *can* *be* *climbed*), and the *no* *doctrine* *leaves* the *philosophy* *empty* (the *no* *mere* *content*), and the *reply* *fails*.

**The *reply*** (the *contemporary* *literature*; the *no* *mere* *nonsense*). The *ladder* *is* the *therapeutic* (the *no* *mere* *contradiction*), and the *reply* *grounds* the *method* in the *practice* (the *no* *mere* *doctrine*). The *reply* is *defensible* (the *therapeutic* *insight*), and *contested* (the *no* *content*).

**(ii) The *no* *showing* *objection***. The *showing*/*saying* *distinction* *is* the *mystical* (the *no* *mere* *criterion*), and the *no* *criterion* *makes* the *distinction* *idle* (the *no* *mere* *test*), and the *reply* *fails*.

**The *reply*** (the *contemporary* *literature*). The *showing* *is* the *practice* (the *no* *mere* *mysticism*), and the *reply* *grounds* the *distinction* in the *grammar* (the *no* *mere* *feeling*). The *reply* is *contested* (the *no* *criterion*).

### 2.2 The *Investigations* and the therapy

**The *thesis*** (Wittgenstein, *Philosophical* *Investigations*, 1953, §§109, 123, 309; the *contemporary* *literature*; the *contemporary* *literature*; the *no* *mere* *theory*). The *philosophy* *is* the *therapy* (the *no* *mere* *doctrine*), and the *problems* *arise* from the *misunderstanding* of the *language* (the *grammatical* *insight*), and the *therapy* *shows* the *fly* the *way* *out* (the *no* *mere* *solution*), and the *philosophy* *leaves* the *everything* *as* *it* *is* (the *no* *mere* *revision*).

**The *structure***.

$$
\text{The *grammatical* *confusion*} \rightarrow \text{the *description*} \rightarrow \text{the *dissolution*} \ (\text{the *no* *mere* *answer*})
$$

**The *argument*** (Wittgenstein 1953, §109).

$$
\begin{array}{ll}
1. & \text{The *problems* *arise* from the *misuse* of the *language*} \ (\text{the *grammatical* *insight*}). \\
2. & \text{The *description* of the *use* *dissolves* the *problem*} \ (\text{the *no* *mere* *theory*}). \\
3. & \text{The *dissolution* *leaves* the *practice* *intact*} \ (\text{the *no* *mere* *revision*}). \\
\hline
4. & \therefore \text{The *philosophy* is the *therapy*} \ (\text{the *no* *mere} \ \text{doctrine*}), \ \text{and the *problem* is the *confusion*} \ (\text{the *no* *mere* \ \text{mystery*}), \ \text{and the *no* *revision* is the *result*} \ (\text{the *no* *mere} \ \text{system*}).}
\end{array}
$$

**The *virtue***. The *account* *captures* the *practice* (the *no* *mere* *system*), and it *dissolves* the *pseudo*-*problems* (the *verificationism* *applied* *to* the *metaphysics*), and it *grounds* the *attention* in the *use* (the *no* *mere* *intuition*), and it *connects* the *philosophy* to the *language* (Ch. 34) and to the *case* (Ch. 51).

**The *objections***.

**(i) The *no* *positive* *thesis* *objection*** (the *contemporary* *literature*). The *therapy* *leaves* the *everything* *as* *it* *is* (the *no* *mere* *revision*), and the *philosophy* *cannot* *criticize* (the *no* *mere* *reform*), and the *reply* *fails*.

**The *reply*** (the *contemporary* *literature*). The *therapy* *criticizes* the *confusion* (the *no* *mere* *conservatism*), and the *reply* *grounds* the *critique* in the *description* (the *no* *mere* *system*). The *reply* is *defensible* (the *descriptive* *insight*), and *contested* (the *no* *reform*).

**(ii) The *self*-*application* *objection***. The *therapy* *is* the *thesis* (the *no* *mere* *method*), and the *thesis* *requires* the *argument* (the *no* *mere* *description*), and the *reply* *fails*.

**The *reply*** (the *contemporary* *literature*). The *therapy* is the *method* (the *no* *mere* *doctrine*), and the *reply* *grounds* the *method* in the *practice*. The *reply* is *defensible* (the *no* *mere* *thesis*), and *contested* (the *no* *criterion*).

**(iii) The *quietism* *objection*** (the *contemporary* *literature*; the *political* *quietism*; the *contemporary* *literature*). The *therapy* *stills* the *question* (the *no* *mere* *answer*), and the *quietism* *leaves* the *injustice* *undisturbed* (the *no* *mere* *critique*), and the *reply* *fails*.

**The *reply*** (the *contemporary* *literature*). The *quietism* is the *method* (the *no* *mere* *politics*), and the *reply* *separates* the *philosophy* from the *politics* (the *no* *mere* *conflation*). The *reply* is *contested* (the *no* *neutrality*).

### 2.3 The state of the therapy

**The *assessment***. The *ladder* *captures* the *clarification* (the *no* *mere* *doctrine*), the *therapy* *captures* the *dissolution* (the *no* *mere* *theory*), and the *quietism* *captures* the *no* *revision* (the *no* *mere* *reform*), and the *self*-*application* *remains* the *contested*.

| The *position* | The *strength* | The *weakness* |
|---|---|---|
| **The ladder** | The *clarification* | The *no* *content* |
| **The therapy** | The *dissolution* | The *no* *positive* *thesis* |
| **The quietism** | The *no* *revision* | The *no* *critique* |

**The *most* *defensible* *position***. A *qualified* *therapeutic* *method*: the *therapy* is the *instrument* (the *no* *mere* *doctrine*), *applied* to the *confusion* (the *no* *mere* *mystery*), *without* the *claim* to the *system* (the *no* *mere* *revision*), *constrained* by the *argument* (the *no* *mere* *description*), and *open* to the *critique* (the *no* *mere* *quietism*), *qualified* by the *admission* that the *method* and the *criterion* *remain* *contested*.

## 3. Carnap and the Internal/External Distinction

### 3.1 The linguistic framework

**The *thesis*** (Carnap, “Empiricism, Semantics, and Ontology,” 1950; the *contemporary* *literature*; the *no* *mere* *metaphysics*). The *internal* *question* (*is* there the *prime* *number* *greater* *than* *100*?) is *answered* *within* the *framework* (the *no* *mere* *metaphysics*), and the *external* *question* (*are* there the *numbers*?) is the *practical* *choice* of the *framework* (the *no* *truth* *value*), and the *choice* *is* *assessed* by the *expediency* (the *no* *mere* *correspondence*), and the *realism*/*anti*-*realism* *debate* *is* the *pseudo*-*question* (the *no* *mere* *dispute*).

**The *structure***.

$$
\text{The *internal* *question*} \ \text{(the *answer* *within* the *framework*)} \quad \text{vs.} \quad \text{The *external* *question*} \ \text{(the *choice* of the *framework*)} \ (\text{the *no* *truth* *value*})
$$

**The *argument*** (Carnap 1950, §2).

$$
\begin{array}{ll}
1. & \text{The *internal* *question* *presupposes* the *framework*} \ (\text{the *no* *mere* *metaphysics*}). \\
2. & \text{The *external* *question* *is* the *practical* *choice*} \ (\text{the *no* *truth* *value*}). \\
3. & \text{The *choice* *is* *assessed* by the *expediency*} \ (\text{the *no* *mere* *correspondence*}). \\
\hline
4. & \therefore \text{The *ontological* *question* is the *practical*} \ (\text{the *Carnap* *insight*}), \ \text{and the *metaphysics* is the *pseudo*-*question*} \ (\text{the *no* *mere} \ \text{dispute*}), \ \text{and the *framework* *is* the *unit*} \ (\text{the *no* *mere} \ \text{object*}).}
\end{array}
$$

**The *virtue***. The *account* *captures* the *framework* (the *no* *mere* *object*), and it *avoids* the *metaphysical* *dispute* (the *no* *mere* *verbal*), and it *grounds* the *choice* in the *practice* (the *no* *mere* *correspondence*), and it *connects* the *philosophy* to the *logic* (Ch. 35) and to the *language* (Ch. 31).

**The *objections***.

**(i) The *no* *neutrality* *objection*** (the *contemporary* *literature*; Quine, “On Carnap’s Views on Ontology,” 1951; the *contemporary* *literature*). The *framework* *choice* *is* the *theoretical* (the *no* *mere* *practical*), and the *choice* *admits* of the *truth* *value* (the *no* *mere* *expediency*), and the *reply* *fails*.

**The *reply*** (the *contemporary* *literature*; Thomasson, *Ontology Made Easy*, 2015, ch. 1). The *choice* *is* the *pragmatic* *and* the *epistemic* (the *no* *mere* *arbitrary*), and the *reply* *grounds* the *choice* in the *epistemic* (the *no* *mere* *expediency*). The *reply* is *defensible* (the *deflationary* *insight*), and *contested* (the *no* *neutrality*).

**(ii) The *no* *deflation* *objection***. The *existence* *of* the *God* *is* *not* the *framework* *choice* (the *no* *mere* *linguistic*), and the *question* *survives* the *deflation* (the *no* *mere* *dissolution*), and the *reply* *fails*.

**The *reply*** (the *contemporary* *literature*). The *question* *is* the *framework*-*relative* (the *no* *mere* *absolute*), and the *reply* *grounds* the *question* in the *framework*. The *reply* is *contested* (the *no* *deflation*).

**(iii) The *self*-*refutation* *objection***. The *deflation* *is* the *metaphysical* *thesis* (the *no* *mere* *deflation*), and the *thesis* *requires* the *argument* (the *no* *mere* *criterion*), and the *reply* *fails*.

**The *reply*** (the *contemporary* *literature*). The *deflation* is the *method* (the *no* *mere* *thesis*), and the *reply* *grounds* the *deflation* in the *practice*. The *reply* is *defensible* (the *methodological* *insight*), and *contested* (the *no* *content*).

### 3.2 The revival: easy ontology and the "just more metaphysics" objection

**The *thesis*** (Thomasson, *Ontology Made Easy*, 2015, ch. 1; the *contemporary* *literature*; the *no* *mere* *difficulty*). - **The *x*-phi*** *raises* the *standard* (the *no* *mere* *anecdote*), and the *critique* *defends* the *argument* (the *no* *mere* *intuition*), and the *method* is the *reformed* *armchair* (the *no* *mere* *abolition*).
- **The *ledger*** is the *local* *knowledge* (the *logic*, the *semantics*, the *Gettier*, the *externalism*), the *negative* *knowledge* (the *dissolutions*), the *structural* *openness* (the *consciousness*, the *free* *will*, the *God*, the *value*), and the *reframing* *progress* (the *mis*-*posed* *questions*).
- **The *most* *defensible* *position*** is the *qualified* *reflective* *fallibilism*: the *local* *knowledge* (the *no* *pessimism*), the *no* *global* *convergence* (the *no* *optimism*), the *negative* *knowledge* (the *dissolution*), the *reframing* (the *mis*-*posed*), the *humbled* *intuition* (the *x*-phi), the *tested* *argument* (the *critique*), the *liberal* *naturalism* (the *continuity*), and the *openness* (the *no* *closure*), *qualified* by the *admission* that the *scope*, the *method*, the *authority*, the *progress*, the *naturalism*, the *diagnosis*, and the *intuition* *remain* *contested*.
