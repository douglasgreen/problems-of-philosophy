# Chapter 42 — Life, Function, and Teleology

> “It is not the strongest of the species that survives, nor the most intelligent; it is the one most adaptable to change.”
> — attributed to Darwin, and almost certainly apocryphal — which is itself a lesson in the *evidential standards* of the field

> “If a thing has a function, then there is something it is *supposed* to do, and it can *fail* to do it. That ‘supposed to’ is the whole difficulty, and it is the whole interest.”
> — a formulation of the function problem, in the spirit of Millikan and Neander

> “Nothing in biology makes sense except in the light of evolution.”
> — Theodosius Dobzhansky, “Nothing in Biology Makes Sense Except in the Light of Evolution” (1973)

---

Chapter 41 examined the *physics* — the *structure* of the world at its most fundamental. This chapter examines the *biology* — the *science* of the *living*, and the *science* that *resists* the physics most stubbornly.

The *resistance* is the *subject* of the chapter, and it has *three* *faces*. The *first* is the *teleological*: biology is *saturated* with the language of *purpose*, *function*, *design*, and *adaptation*, and the *physics* has *no* *place* for it. The *second* is the *historical*: the *organisms* are *products* of a *contingent history*, and the *contingency* is *not* *derivable* from the *laws* — the *biology* is, as the *evolutionary* *tradition* says, a *historical* *science*, and its *explanations* are *narrative* as well as *nomological* (Ch. 38, §7.3). The *third* is the *normative*: the *biological* *notions* — *function*, *health*, *disease*, *species*, *life* — are *value-laden*, and the *values* are *not* *external* to the *biology* (Ch. 39, §6).

The chapter's *thesis* is that *biology* is the *strongest* *test* of the *naturalistic* *program* — the *program* of explaining the *normative* and the *teleological* in *naturalistic* *terms* — and that the *program* *succeeds* in the *function* *debate* (the *etiological* *account* is the *most* *defensible*), *partially* *succeeds* in the *species* *debate* (the *pluralism* is the *most* *defensible*), and *fails* — or at least *stalls* — in the *adaptationist* and the *value* *debates*, where the *explanatory* *and* *normative* *pluralism* is *real* but *unstable*.

The chapter proceeds in eight movements. §1 examines the *nature* of *life* and the *limits* of the *definition*. §2 examines *biological* *function* and the *teleological* *residue*. §3 examines *species*, *individuals*, and the *ontology* of *biology*. §4 examines *reduction* in *genetics*. §5 examines *evolution*, *adaptationism*, and the *evidential* *standards*. §6 examines *teleosemantics* and the *bridge* to the *mind*. §7 examines *environmental* *ethics* and the *value* of the *natural*. §8 gives an *honest* *verdict*.

## 1. What Is Life?

### 1.1 The definition problem

**The *question***. What *is* *life*?

**The *candidate* *criteria***.

| The *criterion* | The *content* | The *paradigm* | The *counterexample* |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Metabolism** | The *regulated* *exchange* of *matter* and *energy* with the *environment* | The *bacterium*, the *cell* | The *virus* (no *metabolism* of its own) |
| **Reproduction** | The *capacity* to *produce* *offspring* | The *organism* | The *sterile* *worker* *bee*, the *mule* |
| **Self-organization** | The *autopoiesis* (the *self*-*maintenance* of the *organization*) | The *cell* (the *minimal* *autopoietic* *system*) | The *crystal* (self-organizes, but *not* autopoietic) |
| **Evolutionary capacity** | The *capacity* for *evolution* by *natural* *selection* | The *lineage*, the *population* | The *individual* *organism* (the *unit* *problem*) |
| **Information** | The *genetic* *information* (the *DNA*, the *RNA*) | The *organism* | The *prion* (infectious *protein*, no *nucleic* *acid*) |

**The *significance***. The *criteria* *diverge* (the *virus* *satisfies* some and *not* others; the *mule* *satisfies* some and *not* others), and the *divergence* *raises* the *question* of *whether* *life* is a *sharp* *kind* or a *cluster* *concept* (Ch. 15, §1.4).

### 1.2 The viral limit case

**The *case***. The *virus* is a *replicator* (it *carries* the *genetic* *information*, and it *reproduces* inside the *host*), and it *does* not *metabolize* (it *has* *no* *metabolism*), and it *does* not *self-organize* (it *is* *assembled* by the *host* *machinery*), and it *evolves* (it *is* *subject* to the *selection*).

**The *argument*.

$$
\begin{array}{ll}
1. & \text{The *virus* *reproduces* and *evolves* (the *replicator* *criterion*).} \\
2. & \text{The *virus* *does* not *metabolize* or *self-organize* (the *metabolic* *criterion*).} \\
\hline
3. & \therefore \text{The *virus* *is* a *borderline* *case* (the *cluster* *concept*), and the *definition* *of* *life* is *not* *sharp*.}
\end{array}
$$

**The *significance***. The *virus* *shows* that *life* is a *cluster* *concept*, and the *cluster* *concept* *is* *the* *most* *defensible* *view* (the *prototype* *theory*; the *Rosch* *tradition*; Ch. 15, §1.4), and the *cluster* *does* *not* *provide* a *sharp* *boundary*.

### 1.3 Autopoiesis and the organizational account

**The *thesis*** (Maturana & Varela, *Autopoiesis and Cognition*, 1980; *The Tree of Knowledge*, 1987; the *contemporary* *literature*; Ruiz-Mirazo & Moreno, “Basic Autonomy as a Fundamental Step in the Synthesis of Life,” 2004). The *life* is the *self*-*production* of the *organization* (the *autopoiesis*), and the *minimal* *life* is the *self*-*maintaining* *boundary* (the *membrane*) that *produces* the *components* that *maintain* the *boundary*.

**The *structure***.

$$
\text{Life} = \text{a *self*-*producing* *network* of *processes* that *maintains* its *own* *organization*}
$$

**The *virtue***. The *account* *captures* the *difference* between the *living* and the *non-living* (the *crystal* *self-organizes*, and it *does* not *self-produce*; the *cell* *self-produces*), and it *avoids* the *reproductive* *criterion* (the *sterile* *organism* is *alive*), and it *grounds* the *life* in the *organization*, not the *molecules*.

**The *objections***.

**(i) The *‘*no* *evolution*’* *objection*. The *autopoiesis* *does* not *require* the *evolution* (the *self*-*producing* *network* *can* be *stable* *without* the *variation* and the *selection*), and the *evolutionary* *capacity* is *the* *biological* *criterion* (the *Darwinian* *insight*; §1.4).

**The *reply*** (the *contemporary* *literature*). The *autopoiesis* is the *minimal* *life* (the *metabolic* *organization*), and the *evolution* is the *further* *capacity* (the *replication* *with* the *variation*), and the *reply* *distinguishes* the *two* *levels*. The *reply* is *defensible* (the *distinction* *is* *real*; the *metabolism*-*first* vs the *replication*-*first* *debates*).

**(ii) The *‘*no* *definition*’* *objection*. The *autopoiesis* is a *complex* *notion* (the *self*-*production* *is* *hard* to *define* *precisely*), and the *definition* *is* *circular* (the *organization* *is* *defined* by the *self*-*production*, and the *self*-*production* *is* *defined* by the *organization*).

**The *reply*** (Maturana & Varela 1980). The *circularity* is *the* *point* (the *life* *is* *constituted* by the *self*-*referential* *organization*), and the *reply* *accepts* the *circularity*. The *reply* is *contested*.

### 1.4 The Darwinian definition

**The *thesis*** (the *evolutionary* *criterion*; the *contemporary* *literature*; Godfrey-Smith, “Is It a Revolution?,” 2017; the *Darwinian* *space* *account*). The *life* is a *population* *of* *replicators* *with* the *variation*, the *heredity*, and the *differential* *fitness* (the *Lewontin* *conditions*; §5.1), and the *life* is *defined* by the *evolutionary* *capacity*, not the *metabolism*.

**The *argument*.

$$
\begin{array}{ll}
1. & \text{The *Darwinian* *conditions* (the *variation*, the *heredity*, the *differential* *fitness*) *define* the *evolution* (the *Lewontin* *insight*).} \\
2. & \text{The *evolution* is the *defining* *characteristic* of the *life* (the *Dobzhansky* *insight*).} \\
\hline
3. & \therefore \text{The *life* is the *Darwinian* *population* (the *evolutionary* *criterion*).}
\end{array}
$$

**The *virtue***. The *account* *captures* the *virus* (the *replicator* *with* the *evolution*), and it *captures* the *sterile* *organism* (it *is* a *member* of the *evolving* *lineage*), and it *grounds* the *life* in the *evolution*, not the *metabolism*.

**The *objections***.

**(i) The *‘*no* *metabolism*’* *objection*. The *evolution* *requires* the *metabolism* (the *replicators* *require* the *energy* and the *materials*), and the *metabolism* *is* the *more* *fundamental* *criterion* (the *metabolism*-*first* *tradition*).

**The *reply*** (the *contemporary* *literature*). The *metabolism* *emerges* from the *evolution* (the *metabolic* *organization* *is* *selected*), and the *reply* *relates* the *two* (the *metabolism* *is* a *product* of the *evolution*). The *reply* is *contested* (the *origin* of *life* *may* *require* the *metabolism* *first*; the *RNA* *world* *vs* the *metabolism* *first*).

**(ii) The *‘*no* *sharp* *line*’* *objection*. The *Darwinian* *conditions* *come* in *degrees* (the *partial* *heredity*, the *weak* *selection*), and the *life* *is* a *matter* of *degree* (the *cluster* *concept*).

**The *reply*** (the *contemporary* *literature*). The *degree* *is* *real* (the *life* *is* a *continuum*), and the *reply* *accepts* the *continuum*. The *reply* is *defensible*.

### 1.5 The limits of definition

**The *thesis*** (the *contemporary* *literature*; the *“*new* *vitalism”* *critique*; the *“*life* *is* *not* *a* *natural* *kind”* *argument*; Machery, “Why I Think Life Is Not a Natural Kind,” 2012). The *life* is *not* a *natural* *kind* (the *phenomenon* is *too* *heterogeneous*), and the *definition* is an *instrument* for the *purpose* (the *astronomy*, the *medicine*, the *law*), and the *choice* is *pragmatic*.

**The *argument*.

$$
\begin{array}{ll}
1. & \text{The *living* *systems* *are* *too* *heterogeneous* to *have* a *single* *essence* (the *virus*, the *bacterium*, the *eukaryote*, the *multicellular* *organism*).} \\
2. & \text{The *definitions* *are* *instruments* (the *different* *purposes* *require* the *different* *criteria*).} \\
\hline
3. & \therefore \text{The *life* is *not* a *natural* *kind* (the *pluralist* *insight*), and the *definition* is *pragmatic*.}
\end{array}
$$

**The *significance***. The *argument* *connects* the *life* *debate* to the *natural* *kinds* *debate* (Ch. 39, §3), and it *supports* the *pluralism*, and it *grounds* the *life* *definition* in the *practice*.

### 1.6 The state of the life debate

**The *assessment***. The *life* is a *cluster* *concept*, and the *most* *defensible* *position* is a *pluralism* (the *metabolic*, the *autopoietic*, the *evolutionary* *criteria* *are* *all* *legitimate*).

| The *criterion* | The *strength* | The *weakness* |
|---|---|---|
| **Metabolism** | The chemical grounding | The virus |
| **Autopoiesis** | The organization | The evolution |
| **Reproduction** | The lineage | The sterile |
| **Evolution** | The Darwinian | The origin |
| **Information** | The molecular | The prion |

**The *most* *defensible* *position***. A *qualified* *pluralism*: the *life* is a *cluster* *of* *criteria* (the *pluralist* *insight*), and the *criteria* *are* *grounded* in the *physical* *organization* (the *physicalist* *insight*), and the *cluster* *is* *real* (the *realist* *insight*), *qualified* by the *admission* that the *boundary* is *vague* (Ch. 33, §3).

## 2. Biological Function and the Teleological Residue

### 2.1 The problem

**The *datum***. The *biology* *ascribes* the *functions* (the *heart* *is* *for* *pumping*; the *eye* *is* *for* *seeing*; the *DNA* *is* *for* *coding*), and the *ascription* *has* a *normative* *character* (the *heart* can *fail* to *do* what it is *supposed* to *do*, and the *failure* is a *dysfunction*).

**The *problem***. The *functions* *are* *teleological* (they *refer* to the *end*, the *purpose*), and the *physics* has *no* *place* for the *teleology*, and the *biology* *must* *either* *reduce* the *teleology* to the *non*-*teleological* *notions* or *accept* the *teleology* as *irreducible* (the *vitalist* *option*, which is *rejected*).

**The *argument*.

$$
\begin{array}{ll}
1. & \text{The *biological* *functions* are *teleological* (they *refer* to the *ends*).} \\
2. & \text{The *teleology* *requires* a *naturalistic* *reduction* (the *naturalism*; Ch. 25, §1.3).} \\
3. & \text{The *reduction* *must* *capture* the *normativity* (the *dysfunction*).} \\
\hline
4. & \therefore \text{The *function* *debate* is the *test* *case* for the *naturalization* *of* *teleology* (the *function* *problem*).}
\end{array}
$$

### 2.2 The etiological account

**The *thesis*** (Wright, “Functions,” 1973; Millikan, *Language, Thought, and Other Biological Categories*, 1984, Ch. 1; Neander, “The Teleological Notion of Function,” 1991; the *contemporary* *literature*). The *function* *of* a *trait* is the *effect* for which the *trait* was *selected* by *natural* *selection*.

**The *structure***.

$$
\text{Function}(x, F) \quad \text{iff} \quad F \text{ is the *effect* for which } x\text{’s *type* was *selected*}
$$

**The *argument*.

$$
\begin{array}{ll}
1. & \text{The *function* *ascriptions* are *teleological* (they *refer* to the *ends*).} \\
2. & \text{The *ends* are *causally* *grounded* in the *selection* (the *trait* *exists* *because* it *did* the *effect*).} \\
3. & \text{The *selection* *is* a *naturalistic* *process* (the *variation*, the *heredity*, the *fitness*).} \\
\hline
4. & \therefore \text{The *function* is the *selected* *effect* (the *etiological* *account*).}
\end{array}
$$

**The *virtue***. The *account* *naturalizes* the *teleology* (the *function* is the *selected* *effect*, and the *selection* is a *natural* *process*), and it *captures* the *normativity* (the *dysfunction* is the *failure* to *produce* the *effect* *that* the *trait* was *selected* for), and it *fits* the *adaptationist* *practice* (the *reverse* *engineering*; the *“*what* is* the* *trait* *for?”*).

**The *objections***.

**(i) The *‘*no* *selection*’* *objection*** (Cummins, “Functional Analysis,” 1975; the *causal*-*role* *account*; §2.3). The *function* *ascriptions* *apply* to the *traits* that *were* *not* *selected* (the *spandrels*, the *by*-*products*, the *neutral* *traits*), and the *etiological* *account* *cannot* *ascribe* the *functions* to them.

**The *reply*** (Gould & Lewontin 1979; the *contemporary* *literature*; Godfrey-Smith 1994). The *spandrels* *have* the *functions* in the *causal*-*role* *sense* (they *do* *something* *useful*), and the *etiological* *account* *distinguishes* the *selected* *functions* from the *use*-*functions*, and the *reply* *accepts* the *distinction*. The *reply* is *defensible*.

**(ii) The *‘*no* *specificity*’* *objection*** (the *contemporary* *literature*). The *selected* *effect* is *not* *unique* (the *heart* *was* *selected* for *pumping* *blood*, and it *also* *produces* the *sound*, the *thump*), and the *etiological* *account* *cannot* *distinguish* the *function* from the *by*-*product*.

**The *reply*** (Neander 1991; Millikan 1989). The *function* is the *effect* *in* the *explanatory* *sense* (the *effect* that *explains* the *persistence*), and the *reply* *distinguishes* the *explanatory* *effect* (the *pumping*) from the *incidental* *effects* (the *sound*). The *reply* is *defensible* (the *explanatory* *relation* *is* *the* *criterion*).

**(iii) The *‘*no* *normativity*’* *objection*** (the *contemporary* *literature*). The *etiological* *account* *naturalizes* the *normativity* (the *dysfunction* is the *failure* *to* *produce* the *selected* *effect*), and the *naturalization* *loses* the *normative* *force* (the *norm* *is* *not* *a* *value*, it is a *statistic*), and the *loss* *is *the* *cost*.

**The *reply*** (Millikan 1984; the *contemporary* *literature*). The *normativity* is *the* *actual* *norm* (the *selected* *effect*), and the *reply* *accepts* the *norm* as *natural* (the *“*proper* *function”* *is* the *norm*), and the *reply* is *contested* (the *norm* *is* *statistical*, and the *value* *is* *not* *statistical*).

### 2.3 The causal-role account

**The *thesis*** (Cummins, “Functional Analysis,” 1975; *The Nature of Psychological Explanation*, 1983). The *function* *of* a *component* is its *contribution* to the *capacity* of the *containing* *system*.

**The *structure***.

$$
\text{Function}(x, F, S) \quad \text{iff} \quad F \text{ is a *capacity* of } x \text{ that *contributes* to the *capacity* } C \text{ of the *system* } S
$$

**The *argument***.

$$
\begin{array}{ll}
1. & \text{The *function* *analysis* is a *decomposition* of a *system* into the *capacities* (the *functional* *analysis*).} \\
2. & \text{The *decomposition* *does* not *require* the *selection* (it *is* a *synchronic* *analysis*). \\
\hline
3. & \therefore \text{The *function* is the *causal* *contribution* (the *causal*-*role* *account*).}
\end{array}
$$

**The *virtue***. The *account* *applies* to the *traits* *without* the *selection* (the *spandrels*, the *artifacts*, the *learning*), and it *grounds* the *function* in the *causal* *structure*, and it *fits* the *mechanistic* *explanation* (Ch. 38, §4).

**The *objections***.

**(i) The *‘*no* *normativity*’* *objection*** (the *most* *serious*; Neander 1991; the *contemporary* *literature*). The *causal*-*role* *account* *cannot* *distinguish* the *function* from the *accidental* *contribution* (the *heart* *contributes* to the *sound*, and the *sound* *is* *not* a *function*), and it *cannot* *ascribe* the *dysfunction* (the *diseased* *heart* *does* not *contribute*, and the *function* *ascription* *remains*), and the *normativity* *is* *lost*.

**The *reply*** (Cummins 1975; the *contemporary* *literature*). The *normativity* is *supplied* by the *system* *analysis* (the *function* is *relative* to the *capacity* *C*, and the *capacity* *C* *defines* the *norm*), and the *reply* *grounds* the *norm* in the *system*. The *reply* is *contested* (the *system* *is* *chosen* by the *analyst*, and the *choice* is *pragmatic*).

**(ii) The *‘*no* *teleology*’* *objection*. The *causal*-*role* *account* *abandons* the *teleology* (the *function* is a *contribution*, not an *end*), and the *teleology* is the *point* of the *function* *ascription*.

**The *reply***. The *teleology* is *the* *causal* *role* (the *effect* *explains* the *system's* *capacity*), and the *reply* *reduces* the *teleology* to the *causation*. The *reply* is *contested*.

### 2.4 The selected-effects account

**The *thesis*** (Godfrey-Smith, “A Modern History Theory of Functions,” 1994; the *contemporary* *literature*). The *function* *ascriptions* *should* be *plural* (the *etiological* *account* *for* the *selected* *traits*, the *causal*-*role* *account* *for* the *non*-*selected* *traits*), and the *pluralism* *is* *the* *most* *defensible* *position*.

**The *argument*.

$$
\begin{array}{ll}
1. & \text{The *etiological* *account* *captures* the *selected* *traits* (the *hearts*, the *eyes*).} \\
2. & \text{The *causal*-*role* *account* *captures* the *non*-*selected* *traits* (the *spandrels*, the *artifacts*).} \\
\hline
3. & \therefore \text{The *function* *ascriptions* are *plural* (the *pluralist* *insight*).}
\end{array}
$$

**The *virtue***. The *pluralism* *fits* the *practice* (the *biologists* *use* the *both* *accounts*), and it *avoids* the *false* *choice*, and it *grounds* the *function* in the *different* *contexts* (the *evolutionary* *vs* the *mechanistic*).

**The *objections*.

**(i) The *‘*no* *univocal* *function*’* *objection*. The *pluralism* *makes* the *function* *ambiguous* (the *same* *trait* *has* the *different* *functions* *in* the *different* *accounts*), and the *ambiguity* *undermines* the *explanatory* *power* (the *function* *ascription* *should* *tell* us *what* the *trait* *is* *for*).

**The *reply*** (Godfrey-Smith 1994). The *ambiguity* is *real* (the *function* is *relative* to the *explanatory* *interest*), and the *reply* *accepts* the *relativity* (the *function* is *always* *relative* to the *system* and the *history*). The *reply* is *contested*.

**(ii) The *‘*no* *norm*’* *objection*. The *pluralism* *losses* the *normative* *force* (the *function* is *whatever* the *interest* *picks*), and the *dysfunction* *is* *not* *grounded*.

**The *reply*** (the *etiological* *account*). The *dysfunction* *is* *grounded* in the *etiological* *account* (the *selected* *effect* *is* *the* *norm*), and the *reply* *distinguishes* the *two* *kinds* of *function*. The *reply* is *defensible* for the *selected* *traits*, and it is *weaker* for the *non*-*selected*.

### 2.5 The function and the normativity

**The *thesis*** (the *contemporary* *literature*; the *“*normative*”* *function* *debate*; the *dysfunction* *literature*; the *philosophy* of *medicine*; Ch. 51, §2). The *function* *ascriptions* *encode* the *norms* (the *heart* *is* *supposed* to *pump*), and the *norms* *are* *grounded* in the *selection* (the *etiological* *account*), and the *norms* *are* *not* *mere* *statistics* (the *dysfunction* *is* *real*, *even* *when* the *dysfunction* *is* *statistically* *common*).

**The *argument*.

$$
\begin{array}{ll}
1. & \text{The *function* *ascription* *implies* the *norm* (the *dysfunction*).} \\
2. & \text{The *norm* *requires* a *normative* *ground* (the *selection*, the *statistics*, the *value*).} \\
3. & \text{The *ground* is the *selection* (the *etiological* *insight*).} \\
\hline
4. & \therefore \text{The *normativity* is *grounded* in the *selection* (the *etiological* *insight*), and it is *compatible* with the *naturalism* (the *naturalistic* *insight*), and it is *not* *reducible* to the *statistics* (the *normative* *insight*).}
\end{array}
$$

**The *significance***. The *argument* *connects* the *function* *debate* to the *philosophy* of *medicine* (the *health*, the *disease*; Ch. 51, §2), and it *grounds* the *pathology* in the *function*, and it *supports* the *etiological* *account*, and it *raises* the *question* of the *normative* *status* of the *norms* (the *epistemic* vs the *moral*; Ch. 44, §4).

### 2.6 The persistence of teleological talk

**The *thesis***. The *teleological* *talk* *persists* in the *biology* *even* *though* the *teleology* *is* *reduced*, and the *persistence* *is* *not* a *prejudice* (the *teleological* *talk* *is* *explanatorily* *indispensable*), and it *is* *not* a *vitalism* (the *teleology* *is* *grounded* in the *selection*).

**The *argument*.

$$
\begin{array}{ll}
1. & \text{The *teleological* *talk* is *indispensable* for the *adaptationist* *explanation* (the *reverse* *engineering*).} \\
2. & \text{The *indispensability* *is* *not* *eliminated* by the *reduction* (the *reduced* *notion* *is* *still* *teleological*).} \\
\hline
3. & \therefore \text{The *teleological* *talk* *persists* (the *irreducibility* *of* the *explanatory* *role*), and it is *grounded* in the *selection* (the *naturalistic* *insight*).}
\end{array}
$$

**The *significance***. The *persistence* *shows* that the *reduction* is *not* the *elimination* (the *reduced* *notion* *is* *still* *used*), and it *supports* the *naturalistic* *account*, and it *raises* the *question* of the *relation* between the *reduction* and the *explanation* (Ch. 21, §6, the *grounding*).

### 2.7 The state of the function debate

**The *assessment***. The *etiological* *account* is the *most* *defensible* for the *selected* *traits*, and the *causal*-*role* *account* is *necessary* for the *non*-*selected* traits, and the *most* *defensible* *position* is a *qualified* *pluralism*.

| The *account* | The *strength* | The *weakness* |
|---|---|---|
| **Etiological (Millikan)** | The normativity | The spandrels |
| **Causal-role (Cummins)** | The non-selected | The normativity |
| **Selected-effects (Godfrey-Smith)** | The pluralism | The ambiguity |
| **Systemic (the contemporary)** | The health | The no-selection |

**The *most* *defensible* *position***. A *qualified* *etiological* *pluralism*: the *etiological* *account* *grounds* the *normativity* (the *selected* *effect* is the *norm*), and the *causal*-*role* *account* *captures* the *non*-*selected* *traits* (the *pluralist* *insight*), and the *two* are *distinct* but *complementary* (the *coexistence* *insight*), *qualified* by the *admission* that the *normativity* *debate* is *unresolved*.

## 3. Species, Individuals, and the Ontology of Biology

### 3.1 The species problem

**The *problem***. The *species* *concept* *fragments* into the *multiple* *concepts* (the *biological* *species* *concept* (the *BSC*), the *phylogenetic* *species* *concept* (the *PSC*), the *ecological* *species* *concept* (the *ESC*), the *morphological* *species* *concept* (the *MSC*)), and the *fragmentation* *raises* the *question* of *which* *concept* *is* the *right* *one* (Ch. 39, §3.3).

**The *BSC*** (Mayr, *Systematics and the Origin of Species*, 1942, p. 120).

$$
\text{Species} = \text{a *maximal* *set* of *interbreeding* *populations* that is *reproductively* *isolated*} \ \text{from the *others*}
$$

**The *PSC*** (Cracraft, “Species Concepts and Speciation Analysis,” 1983; de Queiroz & Donoghue, “Phylogenetic Systematics and the Species Problem,” 1988). The *species* is the *smallest* *diagnosable* *cluster* of the *organisms* *sharing* the *common* *ancestry*.

**The *ESC*** (Van Valen, “Ecological Species, Multispecies, and Oaks,” 1976). The *species* is the *lineage* *occupying* the *adaptive* *zone* (the *ecological* *niche*).

**The *MSC*** (the *traditional* *morphological* *criterion*). The *species* is a *cluster* of the *organisms* *sharing* the *morphological* *characters*.

**The *argument*.

$$
\begin{array}{ll}
1. & \text{The *species* *concepts* *capture* the *different* *biological* *patterns* (the *interbreeding*, the *ancestry*, the *niche*, the *morphology*).} \\
2. & \text{The *patterns* are *real* (they *are* *not* *conventional*), and the *patterns* *cross*-*cut* *one* another.} \\
3. & \text{The *concepts* *are* *not* *reducible* to a *single* *concept* (the *irreducibility*).} \\
\hline
4. & \therefore \text{The *species* *pluralism* (the *pluralist* *insight*), and the *species* *is* *not* a *single* *natural* *kind*.}
\end{array}
$$

### 3.2 Species as individuals

**The *thesis*** (Ghiselin, “A Radical Solution to the Species Problem,” 1974; Hull, “A Matter of Individuality,” 1978; the *contemporary* *literature*). The *species* are *individuals*, not *kinds* (the *species* *are* *particular*, *spatiotemporally* *restricted*, *historically* *connected* *entities*; Ch. 15, §4.2).

**The *argument*.

$$
\begin{array}{ll}
1. & \text{The *species* *are* *spatiotemporally* *restricted* (the *Homo* *sapiens* *exists* *here* and *now*, *not* *everywhere*).} \\
2. & \text{The *species* *are* *historically* *connected* (the *members* *are* *connected* by the *ancestry*, not *only* by the *shared* *properties*).} \\
3. & \text{The *spatiotemporal* *restriction* and the *historical* *connection* are the *marks* of the *individuals* (the *Aristotelian* *criterion*; Ch. 15, §2).} \\
\hline
4. & \therefore \text{The *species* are *individuals* (the *Ghiselin*–*Hull* *thesis*).}
\end{array}
$$

**The *virtue***. The *thesis* *explains* the *species* *as* *historical* *entities* (the *species* *are* *not* *classes* *defined* by the *essential* *properties*), and it *fits* the *evolutionary* *biology* (the *species* *are* *the* *units* of the *evolution*), and it *avoids* the *essentialism* (the *species* *have* the *no* *essence*).

**The *objections***.

**(i) The *‘*no* *class*’* *objection*. The *species* *are* *used* as the *classes* (the *taxonomists* *classify* the *organisms* into the *species*), and the *classification* *requires* the *classes*, and the *thesis* *does* not *capture* the *classificatory* *practice*.

**The *reply* (the *contemporary* *literature*). The *species* *can* be *individuals* *and* *the* *members* *can* be *classified* (the *classification* is the *membership* in the *individual*, not the *instantiation* of a *kind*), and the *reply* *distinguishes* the *two* *relations*. The *reply* is *defensible* (the *part*/*whole* *relation* *replaces* the *kind*/*instance* *relation*).

**(ii) The *‘*no* *homeostatic* *cluster*’* *objection*. The *species* *are* *the* *homeostatic* *property* *clusters* (the *HPC*; the *Boyd* *account*; Ch. 39, §3.2), and the *HPC* *is* a *kind*, not an *individual*, and the *thesis* *conflicts* with the *HPC*.

**The *reply*** (the *contemporary* *literature*; Wilson, *Species*, 1999; the *“*homeostatic* *individual”*). The *HPC* *and* the *individual* *thesis* are *compatible* (the *species* *is* an *individual* *whose* *properties* *are* *homeostatically* *clustered*), and the *reply* *combines* the *two*. The *reply* is *defensible*.

### 3.3 Species pluralism

**The *thesis*** (Kitcher, “Species,” 1984; Dupré, *The Disorder of Things*, 1993; Ereshefsky, *The Poverty of the Linnaean Hierarchy*, 2001; the *contemporary* *literature*). The *species* *concepts* are *plural* (the *different* *concepts* *capture* the *different* *real* *patterns*), and *no* *single* *concept* is *privileged* (the *irreducibility*).

**The *argument*.

$$
\begin{array}{ll}
1. & \text{The *different* *species* *concepts* *capture* the *different* *real* *biological* *patterns* (the *interbreeding*, the *phylogeny*, the *niche*, the *morphology*).} \\
2. & \text{The *patterns* *cross*-*cut* (a *single* *population* *can* *be* a *species* *under* *one* *concept* and *not* under *another*).} \\
\hline
3. & \therefore \text{The *species* *pluralism* (the *pluralist* *insight*), and the *species* *is* *not* a *single* *natural* *kind*.}
\end{array}
$$

**The *virtue***. The *pluralism* *fits* the *practice* (the *different* *subfields* *use* the *different* *concepts*), and it *avoids* the *arbitrary* *choice*, and it *grounds* the *species* *in* the *multiple* *real* *patterns*.

**The *objections***.

**(i) The *‘*no* *objective* *pluralism*’* *objection*** (the *contemporary* *literature*; the *“*pluralism* *is* *relativism”* *worry*; Ch. 39, §3.5). The *pluralism* *makes* the *species* *concept* *relative* to the *interests*, and the *relativity* *undermines* the *objectivity* (the *species* *is* *whatever* *we* *say* *it* *is*).

**The *reply*** (Kitcher 1984; Dupré 1993). The *pluralism* is *constrained* by the *real* *patterns* (the *concepts* *are* *not* *arbitrary*, they *track* the *real* *biological* *structures*), and the *reply* *grounds* the *objectivity* in the *patterns* (the *realist* *insight*). The *reply* is *contested*.

**(ii) The *‘*no* *unified* *biology*’* *objection*. The *pluralism* *undermines* the *unity* of *biology* (the *different* *concepts* *make* the *different* *species*), and the *unity* is *required* for the *general* *claims*.

**The *reply*** (Ereshefsky 2001). The *unity* is *not* *required* (the *biology* *is* a *patchwork*; the *pluralism* *is* the *condition* of the *practice*), and the *reply* *accepts* the *disunity*. The *reply* is *defensible* (the *‘*disorder* *of* *things’*).

### 3.4 The biological individual

**The *question***. What *is* a *biological* *individual*? The *organism*? The *colony*? The *holobiont* (the *host* + the *microbiome*)? The *colony* *of* the *ants*? The *clone* *of* the *aspen*?

**The *criteria***.

| The *criterion* | The *definition* | The *example* | The *problem* |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Genetic** | The *shared* *genome* | The *organism* | The *chimeras*, the *holobionts* |
| **Physiological** | The *metabolic* *integration* | The *organism* | The *colonies* (the *ants*) |
| **Developmental** | The *shared* *developmental* *trajectory* | The *organism* | The *clones* (the *aspen*) |
| **Evolutionary** | The *unit* of *selection* | The *lineage* | The *group* *selection* |
| **Immunological** | The *immune* *tolerance* (the *self*/*non-self*) | The *organism* | The *symbionts* |

**The *argument*.

$$
\begin{array}{ll}
1. & \text{The *biological* *individuals* *are* *too* *heterogeneous* *to* *have* a *single* *criterion* (the *organisms*, the *colonies*, the *holobionts*).} \\
2. & \text{The *different* *criteria* *cross*-*cut* (the *colony* *satisfies* the *evolutionary* *criterion* and *not* the *immunological*).} \\
\hline
3. & \therefore \text{The *biological* *individual* *is* a *plural* *notion* (the *pluralist* *insight*), and the *individuality* *is* a *matter* of *degree* (the *continuum* *insight*).}
\end{array}
$$

**The *significance***. The *argument* *connects* the *species* *debate* to the *individuals* *debate* (the *metaphysics* of the *part*/*whole*; Ch. 21, §4), and it *supports* the *pluralism*, and it *raises* the *question* of the *value* of the *holobiont* (the *environmental* *ethics*; §7).

### 3.5 The state of the species debate

**The *assessment***. The *species* are *real*, and the *species* *concepts* are *plural*, and the *species* *individuals* *thesis* is *defensible*, and the *pluralism* is the *most* *defensible* *position*.

| The *position* | The *strength* | The *weakness* |
|---|---|---|
| **Essentialism** | The microstructure | The species |
| **HPC (Boyd)** | The clusters | The no-mechanism |
| **Individuals (Ghiselin–Hull)** | The historical | The class |
| **Pluralism** | The practice | The no-unity |
| **Nominalism** | The conventional | The projectibility |

**The *most* *defensible* *position***. A *qualified* *pluralism*: the *species* are *individuals* *with* the *homeostatic* *clusters* (the *individual* + the *HPC*), and the *concepts* are *plural* (the *pluralist* *insight*), and the *pluralism* is *constrained* by the *real* *patterns* (the *realist* *insight*), *qualified* by the *admission* that the *pluralism* *undermines* the *unity*.

## 4. Reduction in Genetics

### 4.1 The gene concept

**The *problem***. The *gene* *concept* *fragments* into the *multiple* *concepts* (the *molecular* *gene*, the *Mendelian* *gene*, the *evolutionary* *gene*, the *regulatory* *gene*), and the *fragmentation* *raises* the *question* of *which* *concept* is the *right* one (Ch. 21, §9.2; Ch. 39, §4.3).

**The *concepts***.

| The *concept* | The *definition* | The *example* | The *problem* |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Molecular** | The *DNA* *sequence* (the *transcription*, the *translation*) | The *coding* *region* | The *alternative* *splicing* |
| **Mendelian** | The *unit* of *inheritance* (the *phenotypic* *effect*) | The *allele* | The *polygeny*, the *pleiotropy* |
| **Evolutionary** | The *unit* of *selection* (the *allele* *frequency*) | The *selfish* *gene* | The *group* *selection* |
| **Regulatory** | The *regulatory* *element* (the *expression*) | The *enhancer* | The *non-coding* *DNA* |

**The *argument*.

$$
\begin{array}{ll}
1. & \text{The *gene* *concepts* *capture* the *different* *biological* *patterns* (the *coding*, the *inheritance*, the *selection*, the *regulation*).} \\
2. & \text{The *patterns* *cross*-*cut* (the *pleiotropy*, the *polygeny*, the *epigenetics*).} \\
\hline
3. & \therefore \text{The *gene* *concept* is *plural* (the *pluralist* *insight*), and the *reduction* *is* *partial* (the *Mendelian* *level* *remains* *explanatorily* *necessary*).}
\end{array}
$$

### 4.2 Gene as cause

**The *thesis***. The *genes* are *causes* of the *phenotypes* (the *genetic* *difference* *makes* the *phenotypic* *difference*), and the *causation* is the *difference*-*making* (Ch. 18, §2), and the *analysis* is *statistical* (the *heritability*, the *GWAS*, the *effect* *sizes*).

**The *argument*.

$$
\begin{array}{ll}
1. & \text{The *genes* *make* a *difference* to the *phenotypes* (the *difference*-*making* *insight*).} \\
2. & \text{The *difference* *is* *measured* by the *statistical* *analysis* (the *heritability*, the *association*).} \\
\hline
3. & \therefore \text{The *genes* are *causes* (the *causal* *insight*), and the *causation* is *context*-*dependent* (the *interactionist* *insight*).}
\end{array}
$$

**The *significance***. The *causation* is *not* *deterministic* (the *genes* *do* not *determine* the *phenotype*; the *environment* *interacts*), and the *difference*-*making* *account* *fits* the *statistics*.

### 4.3 Gene as information

**The *thesis*** (the *“*genetic* *information”* *tradition*; the *“*code”* *metaphor*; Maynard Smith, “The Concept of Information in Biology,” 2000; the *contemporary* *literature*; the *critique* in Griffiths, “Genetic Information: A Metaphor in Search of a Theory,” 2001). The *genes* *carry* the *information* (the *code* for the *proteins*), and the *information* *is* the *semantic* *content* (the *meaning*; Ch. 25, §2.3).

**The *argument*.

$$
\begin{array}{ll}
1. & \text{The *genes* *code* for the *proteins* (the *transcription*, the *translation*).} \\
2. & \text{The *coding* *is* a *form* of the *information* *transmission* (the *Shannon* *insight*).} \\
3. & \text{The *transmission* *is* *semantic* (the *code* *means* the *protein*; the *teleosemantic* *insight*).} \\
\hline
4. & \therefore \text{The *genes* *carry* the *information* (the *informational* *insight*), and the *information* *is* *context*-*dependent* (the *epigenetic* *insight*).}
\end{array}
$$

**The *objections***.

**(i) The *‘*metaphor*’* *objection* (Griffiths 2001; the *contemporary* *literature*). The *“*genetic* *information”* is a *metaphor* (the *code* *is* a *causal* *mechanism*, not a *semantic* *content*), and the *semantic* *content* *requires* the *intentionality* (the *norms*), and the *gene* *has* *no* *norms* (the *gene* *is* *not* an *agent*).

**The *reply*** (Maynard Smith 2000; the *contemporary* *literature*). The *information* *is* *teleosemantic* (the *gene* *has* a *proper* *function*; Ch. 25, §2.3; Ch. 42, §6), and the *reply* *grounds* the *content* in the *function*. The *reply* is *contested*.

**(ii) The *‘*no* *specific* *code*’* *objection*. The *genetic* *code* is *degenerate* (the *multiple* *codons* *code* for the *same* *amino* *acid*), and the *context*-*dependence* (the *alternative* *splicing*, the *epigenetics*) *undermines* the *code* *metaphor*.

**The *reply*** (the *contemporary* *literature*). The *code* is a *mechanism* (not a *rigid* *mapping*), and the *reply* *grounds* the *information* in the *mechanism*. The *reply* is *defensible*.

### 4.4 Gene as template

**The *thesis*** (the *template* *metaphor*; the *“*blueprint”*; the *critique* in the *contemporary* *literature*; the *“*causal* *parity”* *argument*; Griffiths & Knight, “What Is the Developmentalist Challenge?,” 1998). The *genes* are *templates* (the *blueprints*), and the *development* *is* the *expression* of the *blueprints*, and the *environment* *supplies* the *materials*.

**The *objections***.

**(i) The *‘*causal* *parity*’* *objection* (Griffiths & Knight 1998; Ch. 42, §4.5). The *genes* and the *environment* are *causally* *on* *a* *par*, and the *genes* *do* not *have* a *special* *causal* *role*, and the *template* *metaphor* *privileges* the *genes* *without* the *justification*.

**The *reply*** (the *contemporary* *literature*). The *genes* *have* the *special* *role* *in* the *inheritance* (the *replication*, the *transmission*) and not in the *causation* (the *developmental* *insight*), and the *reply* *distinguishes* the *two* *roles*. The *reply* is *defensible*.

**(ii) The *‘*no* *blueprint*’* *objection*. The *DNA* is *not* a *blueprint* (the *blueprint* *specifies* the *structure*, and the *DNA* *specifies* the *protein* *sequences*, and the *structure* *emerges* from the *process*), and the *metaphor* *misleads*.

**The *reply***. The *metaphor* is *loose* (it is a *heuristic*, not a *theory*), and the *reply* *accepts* the *limitation*. The *reply* is *defensible*.

### 4.5 The causal parity and the developmental systems

**The *thesis*** (Oyama, *The Ontogeny of Information*, 1985; Griffiths & Gray, “Developmental Systems and Evolutionary Explanation,” 1994; the *contemporary* *literature*). The *development* *is* the *product* of the *developmental* *system* (the *genes*, the *environment*, the *epigenetic* *processes*), and *no* *component* *has* the *privileged* *causal* *role*, and the *causal* *parity* *undermines* the *genetic* *determinism*.

**The *argument*.

$$
\begin{array}{ll}
1. & \text{The *development* *is* *caused* by the *multiple* *factors* (the *genes*, the *environment*, the *epigenetics*).} \\
2. & \text{The *factors* *are* *causally* *on* a *par* (the *interactionism*).} \\
3. & \text{The *parity* *undermines* the *privileged* *role* of the *genes* (the *genetic* *determinism*).} \\
\hline
4. & \therefore \text{The *developmental* *systems* *account* (the *parity* *insight*), and the *genetic* *determinism* is *false* (the *interactionist* *insight*).}
\end{array}
$$

**The *virtue***. The *account* *fits* the *empirical* *literature* (the *epigenetics*, the *plasticity*), and it *undermines* the *genetic* *determinism* (the *ideological* *stakes*; Ch. 44, §7).

**The *objections***.

**(i) The *‘*no* *parity*’* *objection* (the *contemporary* *literature*; the *“*replicator*”* *view*; Dawkins, *The Selfish Gene*, 1976). The *genes* *are* the *replicators* (the *units* of the *inheritance*), and the *environment* is *not* a *replicator*, and the *asymmetry* *grounds* the *privileged* *role* (the *evolutionary* *insight*).

**The *reply*** (Griffiths & Gray 1994; Oyama 1985). The *replication* is *not* *required* for the *heredity* (the *epigenetic* *inheritance*, the *cultural* *inheritance*), and the *reply* *extends* the *heredity* *beyond* the *genes*. The *reply* is *contested* (the *replicator* *concept* *is* *contested*; the *“*replicator”* *vs* the *“*reproducer”*).

**(ii) The *‘*no* *explanation*’* *objection*. The *parity* *dissolves* the *explanation* (the *difference*-*making* *account* *requires* the *causal* *specificity*, and the *parity* *denies* the *specificity*), and the *explanation* *is* *lost*.

**The *reply*** (the *contemporary* *literature*). The *explanation* is *context*-*dependent* (the *causal* *role* *depends* on the *question*; the *difference*-*making* is *question*-*relative*), and the *reply* *grounds* the *explanation* in the *context*. The *reply* is *defensible*.

### 4.6 The state of the genetics debate

**The *assessment***. The *gene* *concept* is *plural*, and the *reduction* is *partial*, and the *genetic* *determinism* is *false*.

| The *account* | The *strength* | The *weakness* |
|---|---|---|
| **Genetic reduction** | The molecular | The pleiotropy |
| **Informational** | The code | The metaphor |
| **Causal parity** | The interaction | The replicator |
| **Pluralism** | The practice | The no-unity |

**The *most* *defensible* *position***. A *qualified* *interactionism*: the *genes* *are* *causes* (the *difference*-*making* *insight*), and the *environment* *is* *causally* *on* a *par* (the *parity* *insight*), and the *genes* *have* the *special* *role* *in* the *inheritance* (the *replicator* *insight*), and the *gene* *concept* is *plural* (the *pluralist* *insight*), *qualified* by the *admission* that the *information* *metaphor* is *contested*.

## 5. Evolution, Adaptationism, and the Evidential Standards

### 5.1 Natural selection

**The *thesis*** (Darwin, *On the Origin of Species*, 1859; Lewontin, “The Units of Selection,” 1970; the *contemporary* *literature*). The *natural* *selection* *requires* the *three* *conditions*.

**The *Lewontin* *conditions***.

$$
\begin{array}{ll}
(1) & \text{The *variation* (the *individuals* *differ* in the *traits*).} \\
(2) & \text{The *heritability* (the *traits* are *inherited*).} \\
(3) & \text{The *differential* *fitness* (the *traits* *affect* the *reproductive* *success*).}
\end{array}
$$

**The *argument*.

$$
\begin{array}{ll}
1. & \text{If (1), (2), and (3), then the *trait* *frequencies* *change* (the *selection* *follows*).} \\
2. & \text{The *selection* is the *mechanism* of the *adaptation* (the *Darwinian* *insight*).} \\
\hline
3. & \therefore \text{The *selection* *explains* the *adaptation* (the *selectionist* *insight*), and the *explanation* is *mechanistic* (the *mechanistic* *insight*; Ch. 38, §4).}
\end{array}
$$

**The *significance***. The *selection* is *blind* (no *foresight*, no *goal*), and it *produces* the *appearance* of *design* (the *“*design* *without* a *designer”*), and the *appearance* is the *source* of the *teleology* (§2).

### 5.2 Adaptationism

**The *thesis*** (the *adaptationist* *program*; the *“*Panglossian”* *critique*; Gould & Lewontin, “The Spandrels of San Marco,” 1979; the *contemporary* *literature*). The *adaptationism* is the *research* *strategy* of *explaining* the *traits* as the *products* of the *selection*, and the *strategy* *has* the *evidential* *standards* (the *optimality* *models*, the *comparative* *method*, the *experimental* *method*).

**The *argument* *for* the *adaptationism***.

$$
\begin{array}{ll}
1. & \text{The *selection* is the *only* *natural* *mechanism* that *produces* the *complex* *adaptive* *design* (the *Darwinian* *insight*).} \\
2. & \text{The *complex* *design* is *pervasive* (the *organisms* *are* *complex*).} \\
\hline
3. & \therefore \text{The *adaptationism* is the *best* *explanation* of the *complex* *design* (the *adaptationist* *insight*).}
\end{array}
$$

**The *critique*** (Gould & Lewontin 1979; the *“*spandrels”*; the *“*just*-*so* *stories”*; Ch. 38, §9.3).

**The *spandrels***. The *spandrels* are the *by*-*products* (the *architectural* *necessities* of the *vaults*), and the *biology* *has* the *analogues* (the *traits* that are *by*-*products* of the *developmental* *constraints*, the *drift*, the *selection* *on* the *other* *traits*), and the *adaptationism* *ignores* the *alternatives*.

**The *argument*.

$$
\begin{array}{ll}
1. & \text{The *traits* *can* be the *products* of the *selection*, the *drift*, the *developmental* *constraints*, the *by*-*products* (the *alternatives*).} \\
2. & \text{The *adaptationism* *explains* the *traits* by the *selection* (the *strategy*), and the *strategy* *must* *exclude* the *alternatives* (the *evidence*).} \\
3. & \text{The *exclusion* *requires* the *evidence* (the *optimality* *models*, the *comparative* *method*), and the *evidence* *is* *contested* (the *“*just*-*so* *stories”*).} \\
\hline
4. & \therefore \text{The *adaptationism* is a *research* *strategy* (the *heuristic*), and it *must* be *constrained* by the *evidence* (the *evidential* *standard*), and the *standard* *is* *contested* (the *critique*).}
\end{array}
$$

**The *significance***. The *critique* *connects* the *biology* to the *philosophy* of *explanation* (Ch. 38, §9), and it *shows* that the *adaptationism* *requires* the *testable* *predictions* (the *novel* *prediction*, the *independent* *evidence*), and the *standard* is the *constraint*.

### 5.3 The levels of selection

**The *question***. What is the *unit* of the *selection*? The *gene* (the *gene*-*centric* *view*; Williams, *Adaptation and Natural Selection*, 1966; Dawkins, *The Selfish Gene*, 1976)? The *organism* (the *individual* *view*)? The *group* (the *group* *selection*; Wynne-Edwards, 1962; the *contemporary* *revival*; Sober & Wilson, *Unto Others*, 1998)? The *species* (the *species* *selection*; Stanley, 1975)?

**The *positions***.

| The *unit* | The *claim* | The *proponent* |
|---|---|---|
| **Gene** | The *gene* is the *replicator*; the *organism* is the *vehicle* | Williams; Dawkins |
| **Organism** | The *organism* is the *unit* of the *selection* | the *classical* *view* |
| **Group** | The *group* can be the *unit* (the *altruism*) | Wynne-Edwards; Sober & Wilson |
| **Species** | The *species* is the *unit* (the *macroevolution*) | Stanley; Gould |

**The *argument*** (the *gene* *centric* *view*).

$$
\begin{array}{ll}
1. & \text{The *replicators* are the *genes* (the *copied* *entities*).} \\
2. & \text{The *selection* *requires* the *replication* (the *inheritance*).} \\
\hline
3. & \therefore \text{The *gene* is the *unit* of the *selection* (the *gene*-*centric* *insight*).}
\end{array}
$$

**The *virtue***. The *view* *explains* the *altruism* (the *kin* *selection*, the *Hamilton* *rule*; the *selfish* *gene*), and it *fits* the *kin* *selection* *literature*.

**The *objections***.

**(i) The *‘*no* *replication*’* *objection* (the *contemporary* *literature*). The *heritability* is *not* *only* the *genetic* (the *epigenetic* *inheritance*, the *cultural* *inheritance*), and the *replication* *is* *not* *the* *criterion* (the *reproducer* *view*; Godfrey-Smith, “Darwinian Populations and Natural Selection,” 2009), and the *gene*-*centric* *view* *is* *too* *narrow*.

**The *reply*** (the *reproducer* *view*; Godfrey-Smith 2009). The *unit* is the *reproducer* (the *entity* that *produces* the *similar* *offspring*), and the *reproducer* *can* be the *organism* (the *bacteria*, the *clones*), and the *reply* *extends* the *units*. The *reply* is *defensible*.

**(ii) The *group* *selection* *objection*. The *altruism* (the *worker* *bee*, the *sterile* *caste*) is *not* *explained* by the *gene*-*centric* *view* (the *sterile* *worker* *has* *no* *offspring*), and the *group* *selection* is *required*.

**The *reply*** (Hamilton, “The Genetical Evolution of Social Behavior,” 1964). The *kin* *selection* *explains* the *altruism* (the *worker* *shares* the *genes* *with* the *queen*, and the *kin* *selection* *is* *the* *gene*-*centric* *account*), and the *reply* *is* *defensible* (the *Hamilton* *rule*). The *group* *selection* *is* *required* for the *groups* *with* the *low* *relatedness*, and the *debate* *is* *unresolved*.

### 5.4 Evolutionary psychology

**The *thesis*** (the *Santa* *Barbara* *school*; Tooby & Cosmides, “The Psychological Foundations of Culture,” 1992; the *contemporary* *literature*). The *human* *mind* is a *set* of the *evolved* *modules* (the *psychological* *adaptations*), and the *modules* were *selected* in the *environment* of the *evolutionary* *adaptedness* (the *EEA*), and the *modules* *explain* the *human* *behavior* (the *cheater* *detection*, the *mate* *preferences*).

**The *argument*.

$$
\begin{array}{ll}
1. & \text{The *mind* is a *set* of the *adaptations* (the *evolutionary* *insight*).} \\
2. & \text{The *adaptations* were *selected* in the *EEA* (the *environmental* *insight*).} \\
3. & \text{The *EEA* is *different* from the *current* *environment* (the *mismatch* *insight*).} \\
\hline
4. & \therefore \text{The *mind* *has* the *modules* that *were* *adaptive* in the *EEA* (the *evolutionary* *psychology* *insight*), and the *modules* *explain* the *behavior*.}
\end{array}
$$

**The *virtue***. The *program* *connects* the *psychology* to the *evolution* (the *biological* *insight*), and it *generates* the *testable* *hypotheses* (the *cheater* *detection*; the *contemporary* *literature*), and it *fits* the *cognitive* *science* (the *modularity*).

**The *critiques***.

**(i) The *‘*just*-*so* *stories*’* *critique* (Gould & Lewontin 1979; Buller, *Adapting Minds*, 2005; Ch. 38, §9.3). The *adaptationist* *explanations* *are* *speculative* (the *EEA* is *not* *known*, and the *stories* *are* *not* *tested*), and the *stories* *fail* the *evidential* *standard*.

**The *reply*** (Tooby & Cosmides 1992; the *contemporary* *literature*). The *EEA* is *constrained* by the *evidence* (the *comparative* *method*, the *hunter*-*gatherer* *data*, the *developmental* *data*), and the *reply* *grounds* the *hypotheses* in the *evidence*. The *reply* is *contested* (the *evidence* *is* *contested*, and the *replication* *crisis* *applies*; Ch. 3, §6.3).

**(ii) The *‘*no* *modularity*’* *critique*. The *massive* *modularity* is *contested* (the *plasticity*, the *domain*-*general* *mechanisms*, the *cultural* *shaping*), and the *modularity* *is* *not* *established*.

**The *reply*** (the *contemporary* *literature*). The *modularity* is *partial* (the *modules* *are* *nested*, and the *plasticity* *is* *real*), and the *reply* *weakens* the *claim*. The *reply* is *contested*.

**(iii) The *‘*no* *heritability*’* *critique*. The *heritability* of the *psychological* *traits* is *contested* (the *environmental* *shaping*, the *cultural* *transmission*), and the *adaptationist* *inference* *requires* the *heritability*.

**The *reply***. The *heritability* is *partial* (the *evolutionary* *psychology* *concerns* the *species*-*typical* *adaptations*, not the *individual* *differences*), and the *reply* *distinguishes* the *two* *questions*. The *reply* is *defensible*.

### 5.5 The evidential standards

**The *thesis*** (the *contemporary* *literature*; the *“*strong* *inference”* *criterion*; the *testing* *of the *adaptationist* *hypotheses*). The *adaptationist* *explanation* *is* *justified* *only* *if* it *survives* the *independent* *tests* (the *novel* *predictions*, the *comparative* *evidence*, the *experimental* *manipulations*), and the *standards* are *the* *same* as the *general* *scientific* *standards* (Ch. 40, §1).

**The *argument*.

$$
\begin{array}{ll}
1. & \text{The *adaptationist* *explanation* is an *inference* to the *best* *explanation* (Ch. 10, §6).} \\
2. & \text{The *inference* *requires* the *comparison* with the *alternatives* (the *drift*, the *constraint*, the *by*-*product*).} \\
3. & \text{The *comparison* *requires* the *evidence* (the *independent* *tests*).} \\
\hline
4. & \therefore \text{The *adaptationism* *is* *justified* by the *evidence* (the *standard*), and the *evidence* *is* *the* *criterion* (the *evidential* *standard*).}
\end{array}
$$

**The *criteria***.

| The *criterion* | The *demand* | The *example* |
|---|---|---|
| **Novel prediction** | The *hypothesis* *predicts* the *new* *observations* | The *cheater* *detection* (the *Cosmides* *experiment*) |
| **Independent evidence** | The *evidence* is *not* the *observation* *that* *motivated* the *hypothesis* | The *comparative* *method* |
| **Falsifiability** | The *hypothesis* *could* be *refuted* (Ch. 40, §6) | The *optimality* *models* |
| **Replication** | The *results* *replicate* (Ch. 3, §6.3) | The *replication* *crisis* |

**The *significance***. The *standards* *ground* the *adaptationism* in the *general* *epistemology*, and the *critiques* *are* the *applications* of the *standards*, and the *debate* *is* *about* the *evidence*, not the *principle*.

### 5.6 The state of the evolution debate

**The *assessment***. The *selection* is the *mechanism* of the *adaptation*, and the *adaptationism* is a *research* *strategy*, and the *evidence* is the *criterion*, and the *evolutionary* *psychology* is *promising* and *contested*.

| The *position* | The *strength* | The *weakness* |
|---|---|---|
| **Adaptationism** | The design | The just-so stories |
| **Spandrels (Gould)** | The by-products | The no-test |
| **Gene-centric** | The replication | The group selection |
| **Reproducer** | The units | The no-replication |
| **Evolutionary psychology** | The testable hypotheses | The EEA |

**The *most* *defensible* *position***. A *qualified* *adaptationism*: the *selection* is the *primary* *mechanism* of the *complex* *design* (the *adaptationist* *insight*), and the *adaptationist* *hypothesis* *must* *survive* the *independent* *tests* (the *evidential* *standard*), and the *alternatives* (the *drift*, the *constraint*, the *by*-*product*) *must* be *excluded* (the *pluralist* *insight*), *qualified* by the *admission* that the *evidence* is *contested* and the *EEA* is *speculative*.

## 6. Teleosemantics and the Normativity of Mind

### 6.1 The bridge

**The *question***. Can the *biological* *function* *ground* the *mental* *content* (the *intentionality*; Ch. 25, §2.3)?

**The *thesis*** (Millikan, *Language, Thought, and Other Biological Categories*, 1984; *White Queen Psychology*, 1993; the *contemporary* *literature*). The *mental* *content* is *grounded* in the *biological* *function*: a *state* *represents* $p$ *iff* the *state* *has* the *proper* *function* of *indicating* $p$ (the *teleosemantic* *insight*).

**The *argument*.

$$
\begin{array}{ll}
1. & \text{The *biological* *function* is *normative* (the *dysfunction*; §2.5).} \\
2. & \text{The *mental* *content* is *normative* (the *misrepresentation*, the *correctness*; Ch. 25, §1.4).} \\
3. & \text{The *function* *grounds* the *content* (the *teleosemantic* *insight*).} \\
\hline
4. & \therefore \text{The *content* is *grounded* in the *biological* *function* (the *teleosemantics*), and the *normativity* of the *content* is *naturalized* (the *naturalistic* *insight*).}
\end{array}
$$

### 6.2 Millikan's proper functions

**The *thesis*** (Millikan 1984, Ch. 1). The *proper* *function* *of* a *device* is the *effect* that *explains* the *device's* *selection* *history*, and the *representation* is the *device* whose *proper* *function* is to *produce* the *isomorphic* *state* in the *consumer*.

**The *structure***.

$$
\text{Represents}(r, p) \quad \text{iff} \quad \text{the *proper* *function* of } r \text{ is to *indicate* } p \text{ (the *producer*) and to *guide* the *consumer*}
$$

**The *virtue***. The *theory* *grounds* the *content* in the *selection* *history* (the *producer* *semantics*), and it *captures* the *normativity* (the *misrepresentation* is the *failure* of the *proper* *function*), and it *connects* the *content* to the *consumer* (the *consumer* *semantics*).

**The *objections***.

**(i) The *‘*swampman*’* *objection* (Davidson, “Knowing One’s Own Mind,” 1987; Ch. 25, §2.3). The *molecule*-for-*molecule* *duplicate* *has* *no* *selection* *history*, and the *theory* *assigns* it *no* *content*, and the *duplicate* *seems* to *have* the *beliefs*, and the *theory* *is* *too* *demanding*.

**The *reply*** (Millikan 1993, Ch. 4; the *contemporary* *literature*). The *content* *requires* the *function*, and the *function* *requires* the *history*, and the *duplicate* *has* the *derived* *functions* (the *functions* *of* the *original*), and the *reply* *grounds* the *content* in the *inherited* *function*. The *reply* is *contested*.

**(ii) The *‘*no* *indeterminacy*’* *objection* (Ch. 25, §2.3; Fodor 1987, Ch. 4; Pietroski, “Intentionality and Teleology,” 1992). The *proper* *function* *underdetermines* the *content* (the *frog*'*s* *detector* *has* the *function* *to* *indicate* the *flies*, and *also* the *small* *black* *objects*), and the *indeterminacy* *is* *not* *resolved*.

**The *reply*** (Millikan 1993, Ch. 2). The *function* is *determined* by the *normal* *conditions* (the *“*normal*”* *explanation* *is* the *most* *specific* *one*), and the *reply* *grounds* the *content* in the *normal* *conditions*. The *reply* is *contested*.

### 6.3 The consumer semantics and the producer semantics

**The *distinction*** (Godfrey-Smith, “Misinformation,” 1989; the *contemporary* *literature*).

| The *kind* | The *content* is *determined* by | The *proponent* |
|---|---|---|
| **Producer** | The *function* of the *signal* to *indicate* (the *sender*) | Millikan; Dretske |
| **Consumer** | The *function* of the *signal* to *guide* the *consumer*'*s* *behavior* | Godfrey-Smith; the *contemporary* *literature* |

**The *argument* (the *consumer* *semantics*).

$$
\begin{array}{ll}
1. & \text{The *content* *guides* the *behavior* (the *consumer* *uses* the *signal*).} \\
2. & \text{The *guidance* *is* the *function* (the *consumer* *semantics*).} \\
\hline
3. & \therefore \text{The *content* is *determined* by the *consumer* *semantics* (the *consumer* *insight*), and the *producer* *semantics* is *necessary* (the *producer* *insight*).}
\end{array}
$$

**The *virtue***. The *consumer* *semantics* *avoids* the *producer* *indeterminacy* (the *content* is *fixed* by the *consumer's* *use*, and the *use* *is* the *specific* *behavior*), and it *fits* the *teleosemantic* *framework*.

**The *objections***.

**(i) The *‘*no* *consumer*’* *objection* (the *contemporary* *literature*). The *consumer* *is* *not* *always* *present* (the *representation* *can* *exist* *without* the *consumer*), and the *consumer* *semantics* *fails* for the *simple* *organisms*.

**The *reply*** (Godfrey-Smith 1989). The *consumer* is the *system* *that* *uses* the *signal* (the *whole* *organism*), and the *reply* *extends* the *consumer* *to* the *whole* *organism*. The *reply* is *defensible*.

**(ii) The *‘*no* *producer*’* *objection*. The *consumer* *semantics* *loses* the *indicator* *relation* (the *signal* *need* *not* *indicate*), and the *content* *is* *not* *grounded* in the *world*.

**The *reply*** (Godfrey-Smith 1989; the *contemporary* *literature*). The *indicator* *relation* is *the* *producer* *function* (the *coexistence* of the *two* *semantics*), and the *reply* *combines* the *two*. The *reply* is *defensible*.

### 6.4 The biosemantics

**The *thesis*** (Godfrey-Smith, “On the Theoretical Role of ‘Genetic Coding’,” 2000; *Complexity and the Function of Mind in Nature*, 1996; the *contemporary* *literature*). The *biosemantics* *combines* the *teleosemantic* *insight* (the *content* *is* *grounded* in the *function*) with the *informational* *insight* (the *content* *is* *carried* by the *information*; Ch. 25, §2.1), and the *combination* *avoids* the *objections* to *each*.

**The *argument*.

$$
\begin{array}{ll}
1. & \text{The *teleosemantics* *grounds* the *normativity* (the *proper* *function*).} \\
2. & \text{The *informational* *account* *grounds* the *indicator* *relation* (the *correlation*).} \\
3. & \text{The *combination* *avoids* the *objections* to *each* (the *swampman*, the *disjunction*; Ch. 25, §2.2–2.3).} \\
\hline
4. & \therefore \text{The *biosemantics* is the *most* *defensible* *account* (the *biosemantic* *insight*).}
\end{array}
$$

**The *virtue***. The *account* *combines* the *two* *insights*, and it *grounds* the *content* in the *function* and the *information*, and it *fits* the *biological* *practice*.

**The *objections***.

**(i) The *‘*no* *norm*’* *objection*. The *combination* *inherits* the *normativity* *problem* (the *proper* *function* *is* *statistical*, and the *norm* *is* *not* *the* *value*; §2.5).

**The *reply*** (the *contemporary* *literature*). The *norm* is *the* *proper* *function* (the *selected* *effect*), and the *reply* *grounds* the *norm* in the *selection*. The *reply* is *contested*.

**(ii) The *‘*no* *content*’* *objection*. The *biosemantics* *grounds* the *indicator* *relation* (the *correlation*) but not the *semantic* *content* (the *meaning*; the *intentionality*; Ch. 25, §1), and the *gap* *remains*.

**The *reply*** (Godfrey-Smith 1996; the *contemporary* *literature*). The *content* is *the* *functional* *role* (the *teleosemantic* *insight*), and the *reply* *grounds* the *content* in the *function*. The *reply* is *contested* (the *“*explanatory* *gap”*; Ch. 24, §2).

### 6.5 The normativity of mind

**The *thesis***. The *mental* *content* is *normative* (the *belief* can be *true* or *false*, the *desire* *satisfied* or *frustrated*, the *perception* *veridical* or *illusory*), and the *normativity* *requires* the *ground* (the *function*, the *representation*, the *agency*).

**The *argument*.

$$
\begin{array}{ll}
1. & \text{The *mental* *content* is *normative* (the *correctness* *conditions*; Ch. 25, §1.4).} \\
2. & \text{The *normativity* *requires* a *naturalistic* *ground* (the *function*, the *information*).} \\
3. & \text{The *ground* is the *biological* *function* (the *teleosemantic* *insight*).} \\
\hline
4. & \therefore \text{The *normativity* *is* *grounded* in the *function* (the *teleosemantic* *insight*), and the *ground* *is* *naturalistic* (the *naturalistic* *insight*), and the *ground* *is* *not* *sufficient* (the *explanatory* *gap*; the *reply* is *contested*).}
\end{array}
$$

**The *significance***. The *argument* *connects* the *biology* to the *mind* (Ch. 25), and it *grounds* the *normativity* in the *function*, and it *leaves* the *gap* (the *“*hard* *problem”*; Ch. 24, §2) *unresolved*.

### 6.6 The state of the teleosemantics

**The *assessment***. The *teleosemantics* is the *most* *developed* *naturalistic* *account* of the *content*, and it *faces* the *swampman*, the *indeterminacy*, and the *explanatory* *gap*.

| The *position* | The *strength* | The *weakness* |
|---|---|---|
| **Producer (Millikan)** | The normativity | The swampman |
| **Consumer (Godfrey-Smith)** | The guidance | The no-consumer |
| **Biosemantics** | The combination | The normativity |
| **Informational (Dretske)** | The indicator | The disjunction |

**The *most* *defensible* *position***. A *qualified* *biosemantics*: the *content* is *grounded* in the *biological* *function* (the *teleosemantic* *insight*), and the *function* *includes* the *informational* *relation* (the *informational* *insight*), and the *account* *leaves* the *explanatory* *gap* (the *phenomenal* *content*; Ch. 24) *unresolved*, *qualified* by the *admission* that the *normativity* *debate* is *contested*.

## 7. Environmental Ethics and the Value of the Natural

### 7.1 The question

**The *question***. Does *nature* have *intrinsic* *value*? And is the *value* *grounded* in the *biology* (§3) or the *ethics* (Ch. 45)?

**The *positions***.

| The *position* | The *claim* | The *proponent* |
|---|---|---|
| **Anthropocentrism** | The *nature* has the *instrumental* *value* (for the *humans*) | the *classical* *tradition* |
| **Biocentrism** | The *individual* *organisms* have the *intrinsic* *value* (the *interest*, the *sentience*; Ch. 51, §5) | Taylor, *Respect for Nature*, 1986 |
| **Ecocentrism** | The *ecosystems* (the *holistic* *entities*) have the *intrinsic* *value* | Leopold, *A Sand County Almanac*, 1949; Rolston, *Environmental Ethics*, 1988 |
| **Value pluralism** | The *values* are *plural* (the *instrumental*, the *intrinsic*, the *relational*) | the *contemporary* *literature* |

### 7.2 Biocentrism and the interests

**The *thesis*** (Taylor 1986; the *contemporary* *literature*). Every *living* *organism* is a *teleological* *center* (it *has* a *good* *of* *its* *own*), and the *good* *grounds* the *intrinsic* *value* (the *respect*).

**The *argument*.

$$
\begin{array}{ll}
1. & \text{Every *organism* *has* a *good* *of* *its* *own* (the *teleological* *center*; §2).} \\
2. & \text{The *good* *grounds* the *intrinsic* *value* (the *biocentric* *premise*).} \\
\hline
3. & \therefore \text{Every *organism* has the *intrinsic* *value* (the *biocentrism*).}
\end{array}
$$

**The *virtue***. The *account* *grounds* the *value* in the *biology* (the *function*, the *good*), and it *extends* the *moral* *status* *beyond* the *sentient* *animals* (the *plants*, the *microbes*).

**The *objections***.

**(i) The *‘*no* *interest*’* *objection*. The *plants* and the *microbes* *have* *no* *interests* (the *interests* *require* the *sentience*, the *experience*; Ch. 29, §5), and the *biocentrism* *extends* the *value* *beyond* the *experience*, and the *extension* is *contested*.

**The *reply*** (Taylor 1986). The *good* *of* *the* *organism* is *the* *teleological* *good* (the *flourishing*), and the *good* *does* *not* *require* the *sentience* (the *plant* *has* a *good*, and it *does* *not* *experience* it), and the *reply* *grounds* the *value* in the *teleology*. The *reply* is *contested* (the *value* *requires* the *valuer*; the *subjectivist* *objection*).

**(ii) The *‘*no* *hierarchy*’* *objection*. The *biocentrism* *makes* all the *organisms* *equal* (the *bacteria* and the *humans*), and the *equal* *value* *is* *counterintuitive* (the *humans* *have* the *interests* *that* *the* *bacteria* *lack*), and the *account* *needs* the *hierarchy*.

**The *reply*** (Taylor 1986). The *equal* *value* *does* *not* *entail* the *equal* *treatment* (the *interests* *determine* the *treatment*), and the *reply* *distinguishes* the *value* from the *treatment*. The *reply* is *defensible*.

### 7.3 Ecocentrism and the ecosystems

**The *thesis*** (Leopold, *A Sand County Almanac*, 1949; the *land* *ethic*; Rolston 1988; the *contemporary* *literature*). The *ecosystems* (the *holistic* *entities*) *have* the *intrinsic* *value* (the *integrity*, the *stability*, the *beauty*), and the *value* is *not* *reducible* to the *value* of the *individual* *organisms*.

**The *argument*.

$$
\begin{array}{ll}
1. & \text{The *ecosystems* *have* the *properties* (the *integrity*, the *stability*, the *diversity*) that *ground* the *value*.} \\
2. & \text{The *properties* are *not* *reducible* to the *individual* *organisms* (the *holistic* *insight*).} \\
\hline
3. & \therefore \text{The *ecosystems* *have* the *intrinsic* *value* (the *ecocentrism*), and the *value* is *irreducible* (the *holistic* *insight*).}
\end{array}
$$

**The *virtue***. The *account* *grounds* the *value* in the *ecological* *properties* (the *diversity*, the *integrity*), and it *fits* the *conservation* *practice* (the *biodiversity* *hotspots*, the *ecosystem* *services*).

**The *objections***.

**(i) The *‘*no* *subject*’* *objection*. The *ecosystems* *are* *not* *subjects* (they *have* *no* *experience*, no *interests*), and the *value* *requires* a *subject* (the *subjectivist* *insight*), and the *ecocentrism* *posits* the *value* *without* a *valuer*.

**The *reply*** (Leopold 1949; Rolston 1988). The *value* is *objective* (the *diversity* *is* *valuable* *regardless* of the *valuer*), and the *reply* *grounds* the *value* in the *objective* *properties*. The *reply* is *contested* (the *objectivity* of *value*; Ch. 44, §2).

**(ii) The *‘*no* *individual*’* *objection*. The *ecocentrism* *sacrifices* the *individual* *organisms* *for* the *ecosystem* (the *culling*, the *invasive* *species* *removal*), and the *sacrifice* *conflicts* with the *biocentrism* (§7.2).

**The *reply*** (the *contemporary* *literature*). The *values* are *plural* (the *individual* and the *holistic*), and the *reply* *balances* the *two* (the *value* *pluralism*). The *reply* is *defensible*.

### 7.4 The naturalistic fallacy and the value of nature

**The *problem***. The *is*/*ought* *gap* (Ch. 44, §1): the *biology* *describes* the *nature*, and the *ethics* *prescribes* the *value*, and the *derivation* *of the *ought* from the *is* is *impossible*.

**The *argument*.

$$
\begin{array}{ll}
1. & \text{The *biology* *describes* the *natural* *facts* (the *functions*, the *ecosystems*, the *species*).} \\
2. & \text{The *ethics* *requires* the *normative* *premises* (the *is*/*ought* *gap*; Ch. 44, §1).} \\
\hline
3. & \therefore \text{The *value* of *nature* *cannot* be *derived* from the *biology* *alone* (the *naturalistic* *fallacy*), and the *value* *requires* the *independent* *normative* *argument*.}
\end{array}
$$

**The *significance***. The *argument* *connects* the *biology* to the *ethics*, and it *shows* that the *value* of *nature* *requires* the *normative* *theory*, and the *biology* *constrains* the *values* (the *facts* *matter* for the *values*) but *does* *not* *determine* them.

### 7.5 The value of natural kinds

**The *question***. Does the *naturalness* *ground* the *value*? (The *natural* *kinds* *are* *valuable* *because* they are *natural*? The *species* *are* *valuable* *because* they are *real*? The *biodiversity* *is* *valuable* *because* the *kinds* are *real*?)

**The *positions***.

| The *position* | The *claim* | The *proponent* |
|---|---|---|
| **Naturalism** | The *naturalness* *grounds* the *value* (the *natural* is *good*) | the *deep* *ecology* *tradition* |
| **Anti-naturalism** | The *naturalness* does *not* *ground* the *value* (the *value* is *independent*) | the *contemporary* *literature* |
| **Relational** | The *value* is *relational* (the *nature* *and* the *valuer*) | the *contemporary* *literature* |

**The *argument* *for* the *anti-naturalism***.

$$
\begin{array}{ll}
1. & \text{The *naturalness* is a *descriptive* *property* (the *causal* *history*, the *selection*).} \\
2. & \text{The *value* is a *normative* *property* (the *good*, the *ought*).} \\
3. & \text{The *normative* *property* is *not* *reducible* to the *descriptive* (the *is*/*ought* *gap*; Ch. 44, §1).} \\
\hline
4. & \therefore \text{The *naturalness* does* *not* *ground* the *value* (the *anti*-*naturalism*), and the *value* *requires* the *independent* *normative* *argument* (the *normative* *insight*).}
\end{array}
$$

**The *significance***. The *argument* *connects* the *natural* *kinds* *debate* (Ch. 39, §3) to the *ethics* (Ch. 44, §1), and it *shows* that the *value* of the *species* is *not* *automatic*, and the *conservation* *biology* *requires* the *normative* *argument* (the *“*biodiversity*”* *value*; the *contemporary* *literature*).

### 7.6 The state of the environmental ethics

**The *assessment***. The *environmental* *ethics* is *unresolved*, and the *most* *defensible* *position* is the *value* *pluralism* (the *instrumental*, the *intrinsic*, the *relational* *values*).

| The *position* | The *strength* | The *weakness* |
|---|---|---|
| **Anthropocentrism** | The practice | The intrinsic value |
| **Biocentrism** | The organisms | The no-interest |
| **Ecocentrism** | The ecosystems | The no-subject |
| **Value pluralism** | The complexity | The no-unity |

**The *most* *defensible* *position***. A *qualified* *value* *pluralism*: the *nature* *has* the *multiple* *values* (the *instrumental*, the *intrinsic*, the *relational*), and the *values* *are* *grounded* in the *biological* *facts* (the *functions*, the *ecosystems*) and the *normative* *theory* (the *ethics*), and the *two* *are* *distinct* (the *is*/*ought* *gap*), *qualified* by the *admission* that the *balance* is *contested*.

## 8. The State of the Debate

### 8.1 What is settled

- **The *life* is a *cluster* *concept*.** The *metabolic*, the *autopoietic*, the *evolutionary* *criteria* are *all* *legitimate*, and the *boundary* is *vague*.
- **The *function* is *grounded* in the *selection* (the *etiological* *account*).** The *etiological* *account* *naturalizes* the *teleology* and *captures* the *normativity* (the *dysfunction*), and it *faces* the *spandrels* and the *synchronic* *traits*.
- **The *causal*-*role* *account* *captures* the *non*-*selected* *traits*.** The *two* *accounts* are *complementary* (the *pluralist* *insight*).
- **The *species* are *individuals* with the *homeostatic* *clusters*.** The *individual* *thesis* and the *HPC* are *compatible*, and the *species* *concepts* are *plural*.
- **The *gene* *concept* is *plural*.** The *genes* are *causes* and *information* and *templates*, and the *causal* *parity* *undermines* the *genetic* *determinism*.
- **The *selection* is the *mechanism* of the *adaptation*.** The *Lewontin* *conditions* are *necessary* and *sufficient*.
- **The *adaptationism* is a *research* *strategy*.** The *evidence* is the *criterion*, and the *just*-*so* *stories* *fail* the *standard*.
- **The *teleosemantics* *grounds* the *content* in the *function*.** The *account* is the *most* *developed* *naturalistic* *account*, and it *faces* the *swampman* and the *explanatory* *gap*.
- **The *value* of *nature* is *plural*.** The *biology* *constrains* but does *not* *determine* the *value* (the *is*/*ought* *gap*).

### 8.2 What is open

- **The *life* definition.** The *cluster* and the *criteria*.
- **The *function* *debate.** The *normativity* and the *spandrels*.
- **The *species* *debate.** The *individuals*, the *HPC*, the *pluralism*.
- **The *gene* *concept.** The *information* and the *parity*.
- **The *adaptationism*.** The *evidence* and the *EEA*.
- **The *teleosemantics*.** The *normativity* and the *explanatory* *gap*.
- **The *value* of *nature*.** The *intrinsic* and the *plural*.

### 8.3 The methodological lesson

The chapter is a case study in the method of Part I.

- **Conceptual analysis** (§1 of Ch. 3) generated the *distinctions* between the *life* *criteria*, the *function* *accounts*, the *species* *concepts*.
- **Thought experiments** (§2 of Ch. 3) — the *swampman*, the *grue* *emerald*, the *spandrels*, the *twin* *earth* — are the *engine* of the debate.
- **Formalization** (§3 of Ch. 3) — the *Lewontin* *conditions*, the *Hamilton* *rule*, the *price* *equation* — is *essential* for the *field*.
- **Reflective equilibrium** (§4 of Ch. 3) is the *method* by which the *intuitions* and the *theories* are *adjusted*.
- **Negative capability** (§7 of Ch. 3) is the *response* to the *residue*: the *humility* to *hold* the *question* *open*.

The honest verdict: the philosophy of biology is a field in transition. The classical positions are untenable in their pure forms; the contemporary positions are promising but incomplete; the debate is unresolved. The unresolved character is instructive: it shows what philosophical progress looks like — not a settled answer, but a sharper understanding of what the question is.

## Summary

- **The *life* is a *cluster* *concept**, and the *metabolic*, the *autopoietic*, the *evolutionary* *criteria* are *all* *legitimate*, and the *boundary* is *vague* (the *virus* is a *borderline* *case*).
- **The *function* is *grounded* in the *selection* (the *etiological* *account*), and it *captures* the *normativity* (the *dysfunction*), and it *faces* the *spandrels* (the *non*-*selected* *traits*), and the *causal*-*role* *account* *captures* the *non*-*selected* *traits*, and the *two* are *complementary* (the *pluralist* *insight*).
- **The *species* are *individuals* with the *homeostatic* *clusters* (the *Ghiselin*–*Hull* + the *Boyd*), and the *species* *concepts* are *plural* (the *Kitcher*–*Dupré*), and the *pluralism* is the *most* *defensible*.
- **The *gene* *concept* is *plural* (the *molecular*, the *Mendelian*, the *evolutionary*, the *regulatory*), and the *causal* *parity* *undermines* the *genetic* *determinism*, and the *information* *metaphor* is *contested*.
- **The *selection* is the *mechanism* of the *adaptation* (the *Lewontin* *conditions*), and the *adaptationism* is a *research* *strategy*, and the *evidence* is the *criterion*, and the *just*-*so* *stories* *fail* the *standard*, and the *evolutionary* *psychology* is *promising* and *contested*.
- **The *teleosemantics* *grounds* the *content* in the *biological* *function*, and the *account* is the *most* *developed* *naturalistic* *account*, and it *faces* the *swampman*, the *indeterminacy*, and the *explanatory* *gap*.
- **The *value* of *nature* is *plural* (the *instrumental*, the *intrinsic*, the *relational*), and the *biology* *constrains* the *value* but does *not* *determine* it (the *is*/*ought* *gap*), and the *naturalness* does *not* *ground* the *value*.
- **The *most* *defensible* *position*** is a *qualified* *naturalism*: the *biological* *functions* are *grounded* in the *selection* (the *etiological* *insight*), the *species* are *individuals* with the *homeostatic* *clusters* (the *pluralist* *insight*), the *adaptationism* is *constrained* by the *evidence* (the *evidential* *insight*), the *teleosemantics* *grounds* the *content* (the *teleosemantic* *insight*), and the *value* of *nature* is *plural* (the *normative* *insight*), *qualified* by the *admission* that the *normativity*, the *evidence*, and the *explanatory* *gap* *remain* *unresolved*.
