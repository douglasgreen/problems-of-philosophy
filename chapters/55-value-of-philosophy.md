# Chapter 54 — The Value of Philosophy

> “The value of philosophy is, in fact, to be sought largely in its very uncertainty. … The mind which has become accustomed to the freedom and impartiality of philosophic contemplation will preserve something of the same freedom and impartiality in the world of action and emotion. It will be the same with the good which philosophy can do.”
> — Bertrand Russell, *The Problems of Philosophy* (1912), ch. XV

> “The unexamined life is not worth living for a human being.”
> — Socrates, *Apology* (399 BCE), 38a

> “It is owing to their wonder that men both now begin and at first began to philosophize.”
> — Aristotle, *Metaphysics* (c. 350 BCE), I.2, 982b12

> “Philosophy is a battle against the bewitchment of our intelligence by means of language.”
> — Ludwig Wittgenstein, *Philosophical Investigations* (1953), §109

> “What is the use of studying philosophy if all that it does for you is to enable you to talk with some plausibility about some abstruse questions of logic, etc., and if it does not improve your thinking about the important questions of everyday life?”
> — Ludwig Wittgenstein, *Culture and Value* (1980), 1937 entry

---

Chapter 53 established the *form* of the *unity* — the philosophy as *one* in the *form* (the *conditions* of *intelligibility*) and *many* in the *matter* (the *domains*, the *methods*). This chapter asks the *final* question that the *whole* *presupposes*: *why* *does* the *philosophy* *matter*?

The *transition* is the *inversion* of the *unity* *question*. The *unity* asks *what* the *discipline* *is* (the *no* *mere* *family*). The *value* *question* asks *what* the *discipline* *is* *for* — and the *answer* *has* *two* *faces*. If the *philosophy* *yields* the *understanding* (the *no* *mere* *result*), then the *value* is the *intrinsic* (the *no* *mere* *instrument*). If the *philosophy* *yields* the *clarity* and the *criticism* (the *no* *mere* *ornament*), then the *value* is the *instrumental* (the *no* *mere* *luxury*). And the *two* *faces* *are* the *bookends* of the *whole* *book*: the *introductory* *thesis* (the *systematic* *analysis*; Ch. 1, §2) *promised* the *value*, and the *closing* *chapter* *must* *deliver* the *account*.

The *chapter* proceeds in *eight* *movements*. §1 states the *question* and *reconsiders* Russell’s *Chapter* *XV*. §2 examines the *intrinsic* *goods* of the *inquiry*. §3 examines the *instrumental* *goods*. §4 examines the *examined* *life*. §5 examines the *charge* of the *uselessness*. §6 examines the *charge* of the *danger*. §7 examines the *practice* (the *habits*, the *curriculum*, the *institution*). §8 gives the *verdict* and the *closing* *meditation*.

## 1. The Question and Russell’s Chapter XV

### 1.1 The structure of the question

**The *question***. The *value* *question* (*why* *does* the *philosophy* *matter*?) is *ambiguous* between the *four* *senses*, and the *failure* to *distinguish* them *produces* the *confusion* (the *no* *single* *question*).

**The *four* *senses***.

| The *sense* | The *content* | The *test* |
|---|---|---|
| **The *intrinsic*** | The *value* *for* its *own* *sake* (the *understanding*) | The *no* *mere* *instrument* |
| **The *instrumental*** | The *value* *for* the *other* *ends* (the *clarity*, the *criticism*) | The *transfer* |
| **The *constitutive*** | The *value* *in* the *practice* (the *virtues*, the *life*) | The *no* *mere* *product* |
| **The *exemplary*** | The *value* *in* the *model* (the *examined* *life*) | The *no* *mere* *example* |

**The *significance***. The *senses* *come* *apart*: the *philosophy* *may* have the *intrinsic* *value* and *lack* the *instrumental* (the *no* *mere* *utility*), and it *may* have the *constitutive* *value* and *lack* the *exemplary* (the *no* *mere* *model*), and the *verdict* *depends* on the *sense* (the *no* *single* *verdict*), and the *defence* *against* the *charges* *requires* the *distinct* *answers* (the *no* *mere* *rhetoric*).

**The *criteria***. The *value* is *assessed* by the *five* *markers*.

| The *marker* | The *content* | The *significance* |
|---|---|---|
| **The *genuineness*** | The *value* is the *real* (the *no* *mere* *projection*) | The *no* *mere* *self*-*congratulation* |
| **The *comparative*** | The *value* is the *competitive* (the *no* *mere* *vacuous*) | The *no* *mere* *apologetics* |
| **The *distribution*** | The *value* is the *accessible* (the *no* *mere* *elite*) | The *no* *mere* *privilege* |
| **The *transfer*** | The *value* is the *exportable* (the *no* *mere* *enclosure*) | The *no* *mere* *insulation* |
| **The *cost*** | The *value* is the *net* (the *no* *mere* *benefit*) | The *no* *mere* *sales* *talk* |

**The *problem***. The *markers* *pull* *apart*: the *genuineness* *favours* the *intrinsic* (the *no* *mere* *utility*), the *distribution* *favours* the *instrumental* (the *no* *mere* *privilege*), the *transfer* *favours* the *practice* (the *no* *mere* *enclosure*), and the *cost* *favours* the *honesty* (the *no* *mere* *sales* *talk*).

The *philosophy* *does* not *answer* the *question* for the *reader*; it *forms* the *reader* *who* *can* *ask* it.
