Chapter 45 examined the *content* of the moral judgement; Chapter 46 its *efficacy*; Chapter 47 its *architecture*; Chapter 48 its *point*; Chapter 49 its *splendour*; Chapter 50 its *horizon*. This chapter examines its *application* — the *case*, the *decision*, the *stakes*.

The *transition* is the *inversion* of the *whole* of Part VII. The *applied* *ethics* asks the *question* that *tests* the *whole* *apparatus*: *given* the *theory* — *and* the *facts* *and* the *uncertainty* *and* the *time*-*pressure* — *what* *do* we *do* *now*, *here*, *for* *this* *patient*, *this* *model*, *this* *farm*, *this* *atmosphere*?

The *chapter* proceeds in *eight* *movements*. §1 examines the *method* of *applied* *ethics*. §2 examines the *bioethics*. §3 examines the *ethics* of the *artificial* *intelligence*. §4 examines the *data*, the *privacy*, the *attention*. §5 examines the *animal* *ethics*. §6 examines the *environmental* *ethics*. §7 examines the *war*, the *punishment*, the *migration*. §8 examines the *moral* *risk* and *gives* the *verdict*.

## 1. The Method of Applied Ethics

### 1.1 The structure of the question

**The *question***. The *applied* *question* (*what* *ought* we *to* *do* *here*?) is *distinct* from the *theoretical* (the *what* *is* the *right*?, the *what* *is* the *good*?), and the *distinction* is *not* the *distinction* *between* the *theory* and the *practice* (the *no* *mere* *handbook*), but the *distinction* *between* the *general* and the *particular* (the *no* *mere* *derivation*), and the *particular* *raises* the *distinctive* *difficulties* (the *facts*, the *uncertainty*, the *time*, the *institutions*).

**The *stages***.

| The *stage* | The *content* | The *difficulty* |
|---|---|---|
| **The *identification*** | The *question* *stated* (the *no* *mere* *worry*) | The *framing* |
| **The *description*** | The *facts* *established* (the *empirical* *insight*) | The *no* *certainty* |
| **The *evaluation*** | The *principles* *applied* (the *normative* *insight*) | The *conflict* |
| **The *decision*** | The *judgement* *rendered* (the *action* *insight*) | The *responsibility* |
| **The *review*** | The *outcome* *assessed* (the *learning* *insight*) | The *no* *experiment* |

**The *significance***. The *stages* *reveal* the *gap* *between* the *theory* and the *case* (the *no* *algorithm*), and the *gap* *requires* the *distinctive* *method* (the *no* *mere* *deduction*), and the *method* *connects* the *applied* *ethics* to the *methodology* of Part I (the *reflective* *equilibrium*; Ch. 3, §4).

**The *constraints***.

| The *constraint* | The *content* | The *significance* |
|---|---|---|
| **The *action*-*guidance*** | The *theory* *must* *yield* the *verdict* (the *no* *mere* *description*) | The *practical* *pressure* |
| **The *sensitivity*** | The *verdict* *must* *track* the *case* (the *no* *mere* *abstraction*) | The *contextual* *pressure* |
| **The *coherence*** | The *verdict* *must* *fit* the *theory* (the *no* *mere* *intuition*) | The *theoretical* *pressure* |
| **The *accountability*** | The *verdict* *must* be *justifiable* *to* the *others* (the *no* *mere* *authority*) | The *public* *pressure* |

**The *problem***. The *constraints* *pull* *apart*: the *action*-*guidance* *favours* the *determinate* *theory* (the *no* *indeterminacy*), the *sensitivity* *favours* the *contextual* *judgement* (the *no* *deduction*), the *coherence* *favours* the *consistency* (the *no* *ad* *hoc* *exception*), and the *accountability* *favours* the *public* *reason* (the *no* *mere* *expertise*).

### 1.2 The top-down model

**The *thesis*** (the *classical* *deductive* *model*; the *contemporary* *literature*; the *critical* *literature*; Hare, *Moral Thinking*, 1981, ch. 1). The *applied* *ethics* is the *application* of the *theory* to the *case* (the *no* *mere* *intuition*), and the *theory* *yields* the *verdict* (the *no* *judgement* *beyond* the *theory*), and the *justification* is the *derivation* (the *no* *mere* *precedent*), and the *role* of the *applied* *ethics* is the *subsumption* (the *no* *mere* *casuistry*).

**The *structure***.

$$
\begin{array}{ll}
1. & \text{The *theory* } T \text{ *yields* the *verdict* } V \text{ in the *case* } C \ (\text{the *application*}). \\
2. & T \text{ is the *justified* *theory*} \ (\text{the *no* *mere* *intuition*}). \\
3. & \text{The *application* is the *deduction*} \ (\text{the *no* *judgement* *beyond* the *theory*}). \\
\hline
4. & \therefore V \text{ is the *justified* *verdict*} \ (\text{the *top*-*down* *insight*}), \ \text{and the *justification* is the *derivation*} \ (\text{the *no* *mere* *precedent*}).}
\end{array}
$$

**The *virtue***. The *model* *secures* the *determinacy* (the *no* *arbitrary* *verdict*), and it *secures* the *coherence* (the *no* *ad* *hoc*), and it *grounds* the *accountability* (the *public* *theory*), and it *fits* the *practice* of the *trolley* *literature* (the *theory* *yielding* the *verdict*).

**The *objections***.

**(i) The *no* *algorithm* *objection***. The *theory* *under*-*determines* the *verdict* (the *no* *unique* *application*, the *no* *mere* *subsumption*), and the *case* *requires* the *judgement* (the *no* *mere* *deduction*), and the *model* *fails*.

**The *reply*** (the *contemporary* *literature*). The *under*-*determination* *is* the *condition* (the *no* *mere* *defect*), and the *reply* *grounds* the *judgement* in the *theory* *plus* the *facts* *plus* the *specification* (§1.4). The *reply* is *defensible* (the *no* *mere* *algorithm*), and *contested* (the *no* *determinacy*).

**(ii) The *no* *theory* *objection*** (the *anti*-*theory*; Ch. 45, §5; the *particularism*; the *contemporary* *literature*). The *moral* *theory* *misrepresents* the *moral* *life* (the *no* *codification*), and the *applied* *ethics* *requires* the *perception* (the *no* *mere* *subsumption*), and the *model* *fails*.

**The *reply*** (the *contemporary* *literature*). The *theory* *guides* *without* *codifying* (the *no* *mere* *algorithm*), and the *reply* *grounds* the *applied* *ethics* in the *theory* *as* the *constraint* (the *no* *mere* *codification*). The *reply* is *defensible* (the *guidance* *insight*), and *contested* (the *no* *codification*).

### 1.3 Casuistry and the bottom-up model

**The *thesis*** (the *contemporary* *literature*; Jonsen & Toulmin, *The Abuse of Casuistry*, 1988, ch. 1; the *clinical* *ethics* *literature*; the *no* *mere* *theory*). The *applied* *ethics* *proceeds* by the *analogy* to the *paradigm* *cases* (the *no* *mere* *principle*), and the *paradigm* *cases* *anchor* the *judgement* (the *no* *mere* *intuition*), and the *analogy* *extends* the *judgement* to the *new* *case* (the *no* *mere* *deduction*), and the *theory* is the *summary* of the *practice* (the *no* *mere* *foundation*).

**The *structure***.

$$
\begin{array}{ll}
1. & \text{The *paradigm* *case* } C_0 \text{ *yields* the *verdict* } V_0 \ (\text{the *no* *mere* *theory*}). \\
2. & \text{The *new* *case* } C_1 \text{ *is* *analogous* to } C_0 \ (\text{the *similarity* *insight*}). \\
3. & \text{The *analogy* *extend* the *verdict*} \ (\text{the *no* *mere* *deduction*}). \\
\hline
4. & \therefore V_0 \text{ *applies* to } C_1 \ (\text{the *casuist* *insight*}), \ \text{and the *theory* is the *summary*} \ (\text{the *no* *mere* *foundation*}), \ \text{and the *practice* *grounds* the *principle*} \ (\text{the *no* *mere* *top*-*down*}).}
\end{array}
$$

**The *virtue***. The *model* *captures* the *contextual* *sensitivity* (the *no* *mere* *abstraction*), and it *fits* the *clinical* *practice* (the *case* *conference*, the *no* *mere* *algorithm*), and it *grounds* the *judgement* in the *precedent* (the *no* *mere* *authority*), and it *connects* the *applied* *ethics* to the *common* *law* (the *no* *mere* *code*).

**The *objections***.

**(i) The *no* *principle* *objection***. The *casuistry* *is* the *precedent* *without* the *principle* (the *no* *justification*), and the *analogy* *is* *arbitrary* (the *no* *criterion*), and the *model* *fails*.

**The *reply*** (Jonsen & Toulmin 1988, ch. 1). The *analogy* *has* the *taxonomy* (the *no* *arbitrary*), and the *reply* *grounds* the *analogy* in the *paradigm* (the *no* *mere* *precedent*). The *reply* is *defensible* (the *taxonomic* *insight*), and *contested* (the *no* *principle*).

**(ii) The *no* *critique* *objection***. The *casuistry* *preserves* the *tradition* (the *no* *critique*), and the *injustice* *can* be *the* *paradigm* (the *no* *mere* *neutrality*), and the *model* *fails*.

**The *reply*** (the *contemporary* *literature*). The *paradigm* *is* *revisable* (the *no* *mere* *conservatism*), and the *reply* *grounds* the *critique* in the *theory* (the *no* *mere* *tradition*). The *reply* is *defensible*.

### 1.4 Specification and the wide reflective equilibrium

**The *thesis*** (Richardson, “Specifying Norms as a Way to Resolve Concrete Ethical Problems,” 1990, *PPA* 19; Daniels, *Justice and Justification*, 1996, ch. 1; the *contemporary* *literature*; the *no* *mere* *derivation*). The *norms* are *specified* (the *no* *mere* *application*), and the *specification* *adapts* the *norm* to the *case* (the *no* *mere* *subsumption*), and the *equilibrium* *adjusts* the *norm* and the *judgement* (the *no* *mere* *foundation*), and the *method* is the *wide* *reflective* *equilibrium* (the *no* *mere* *narrow*).

**The *structure*** (the *equilibrium*).

$$
\text{The *verdict* in } C \ \leftrightarrows \ \text{the *principle* } P \ \leftrightarrows \ \text{the *background* *theory* } T \ \leftrightarrows \ \text{the *considered* *judgements* } J
$$

**The *specification*** (the *Richardson* *form*).

$$
\text{The *norm* } N \ \Rightarrow \ \text{the *specified* *norm* } N' \ \text{that *is* *applicable* to } C \ (\text{the *no* *mere* *subsumption*})
$$

**The *argument***.

$$
\begin{array}{ll}
1. & \text{The *general* *norm* *under*-*determines* the *case*} \ (\text{the *no* *mere* *deduction*}). \\
2. & \text{The *specification* *yields* the *applicable* *norm*} \ (\text{the *no* *mere* *exception*}). \\
3. & \text{The *equilibrium* *adjusts* the *whole*} \ (\text{the *no* *mere* *foundation*}). \\
\hline
4. & \therefore \text{The *equilibrium* *grounds* the *verdict*} \ (\text{the *Daniels* *insight*}), \ \text{and the *specification* *is* the *method*} \ (\text{the *Richardson* *insight*}), \ \text{and the *no* *mere} \ \text{derivation*} \ (\text{the *applied* *insight*}).}
\end{array}
$$

**The *virtue***. The *method* *captures* the *case* (the *no* *mere* *abstraction*), and it *captures* the *theory* (the *no* *mere* *intuition*), and it *captures* the *revision* (the *no* *mere* *conservatism*), and it *connects* the *applied* *ethics* to the *methodology* of Part I (Ch. 3, §4; Ch. 45, §5.3).

**The *objections***.

**(i) The *no* *stability* *objection***. The *equilibrium* *is* *unstable* (the *no* *unique* *convergence*), and the *different* *starting* *points* *yield* the *different* *verdicts* (the *no* *determinacy*), and the *reply* *fails*.

**The *reply*** (the *contemporary* *literature*). The *stability* *is* the *ideal* (the *no* *mere* *fact*), and the *reply* *grounds* the *method* in the *convergence* (the *no* *mere* *relativism*). The *reply* is *defensible* (the *no* *mere* *relativism*), and *contested* (the *no* *determinacy*).

**(ii) The *no* *constraint* *objection***. The *equilibrium* *licenses* the *wishful* *adjustment* (the *no* *mere* *coherence*), and the *intuition* *can* *override* the *theory* (the *no* *mere* *discipline*), and the *reply* *fails*.

**The *reply*** (Daniels 1996, ch. 1). The *constraint* is the *whole* of the *considered* *judgements* (the *no* *mere* *single* *intuition*), and the *reply* *grounds* the *discipline* in the *whole* (the *no* *mere* *coherence*). The *reply* is *defensible*.

### 1.5 Principlism

**The *thesis*** (Beauchamp & Childress, *Principles of Biomedical Ethics*, 1979/2019, ch. 1; the *contemporary* *literature*; the *no* *mere* *single* *theory*). The *applied* *ethics* *proceeds* by the *four* *principles* (the *autonomy*, the *beneficence*, the *non*-*maleficence*, the *justice*), and the *principles* are the *prima* *facie* (the *no* *absolute*, the *no* *lexical* *order*), and the *conflict* *is* resolved by the *specification* and the *balancing* (the *no* *mere* *derivation*), and the *theory* is the *background* (the *no* *mere* *foundation*).

**The *structure***.

$$
\text{Autonomy} \ \wedge \ \text{Beneficence} \ \wedge \ \text{Non-maleficence} \ \wedge \ \text{Justice} \ (\text{the *prima* *facie*, the *no* *lexical* *order*})
$$

**The *argument***.

$$
\begin{array}{ll}
1. & \text{The *single* *theory* *fails* the *practice*} \ (\text{the *no* *mere* *algorithm*}). \\
2. & \text{The *four* *principles* *capture* the *common* *morality*} \ (\text{the *no* *mere* *sect*}). \\
3. & \text{The *prima* *facie* *structure* *permits* the *conflict*} \ (\text{the *no* *mere* *monism*}). \\
\hline
4. & \therefore \text{The *principlism* *grounds* the *practice*} \ (\text{the *Beauchamp*-*Childress* *insight*}), \ \text{and the *specification* *resolves* the *conflict*} \ (\text{the *no* *mere} \ \text{derivation*}), \ \text{and the *theory* is the *background*} \ (\text{the *no* *mere} \ \text{foundation*}).}
\end{array}
$$

**The *virtue***. The *account* *captures* the *common* *morality* (the *no* *mere* *sect*), and it *fits* the *clinical* *practice* (the *no* *mere* *algorithm*), and it *grounds* the *pluralism* (the *no* *mere* *monism*), and it *connects* the *applied* *ethics* to the *normative* *ethics* (Ch. 45, §1–4).

**The *objections***.

**(i) The *no* *system* *objection***. The *principlism* *is* the *checklist* (the *no* *theory*), and the *balancing* *is* the *intuition* (the *no* *justification*), and the *reply* *fails*.

**The *reply*** (Beauchamp & Childress 2019, ch. 1). The *principles* *have* the *theory* *behind* them (the *no* *mere* *checklist*), and the *reply* *grounds* the *balancing* in the *specification* (the *no* *mere* *intuition*). The *reply* is *defensible* (the *no* *mere* *checklist*), and *contested* (the *no* *system*).

**(ii) The *no* *ordering* *objection***. The *no* *lexical* *order* *leaves* the *conflict* *unresolved* (the *no* *determinacy*), and the *autonomy* *trumps* the *others* *in* the *practice* (the *no* *mere* *prima* *facie*), and the *reply* *fails*.

**The *reply*** (the *contemporary* *literature*). The *no* *ordering* is the *condition* of the *pluralism* (the *no* *mere* *defect*), and the *reply* *grounds* the *resolution* in the *specification*. The *reply* is *defensible* (the *no* *mere* *hierarchy*), and *contested* (the *no* *determinacy*).

### 1.6 Public reason and the empirical turn

**The *thesis*** (Rawls, *Political Liberalism*, 1993, the *public* *reason*; the *contemporary* *literature*; the *contemporary* *literature*; the *evidence*-*based* *medicine*; the *no* *mere* *expertise*). The *applied* *ethics* *must* be *justifiable* *to* the *public* (the *no* *mere* *sectarian*), and the *justification* *requires* the *shared* *reason* (the *no* *mere* *authority*), and the *empirical* *evidence* *constrains* the *verdict* (the *no* *mere* *armchair*), and the *method* *connects* the *ethics* to the *politics* (Ch. 47, §6) and to the *epistemology* (Ch. 10, §7).

**The *structure***.

$$
\text{The *justification*} \ = \ \text{the *public* *reason*} \ \wedge \ \text{the *empirical* *evidence*} \ (\text{the *no* *mere* *authority*})
$$

**The *argument***.

$$
\begin{array}{ll}
1. & \text{The *coercion* *requires* the *justification*} \ (\text{the *no* *mere* *authority*}). \\
2. & \text{The *justification* *requires* the *public* *reason*} \ (\text{the *no* *mere* *sect*}). \\
3. & \text{The *reason* *requires* the *evidence*} \ (\text{the *no* *mere* *intuition*}). \\
\hline
4. & \therefore \text{The *applied* *ethics* *requires* the *public* *reason*} \ (\text{the *Rawls* *insight*}), \ \text{and the *evidence*} \ (\text{the *empirical* *insight*}), \ \text{and the *no* *mere} \ \text{expertise*} \ (\text{the *democratic* *insight*}).}
\end{array}
$$

**The *virtue***. The *account* *captures* the *legitimacy* (the *no* *mere* *authority*), and it *fits* the *pluralism* (the *no* *mere* *sect*), and it *grounds* the *verdict* in the *evidence* (the *no* *mere* *intuition*), and it *connects* the *applied* *ethics* to the *politics* (Ch. 47) and to the *science* (Ch. 38–40).

**The *objections***.

**(i) The *no* *consensus* *objection***. The *public* *reason* *presupposes* the *consensus* (the *no* *mere* *fact*), and the *deep* *disagreement* *(the abortion, the euthanasia)* *blocks* the *justification* (the *no* *mere* *reason*), and the *reply* *fails*.

**The *reply*** (the *contemporary* *literature*). The *consensus* *is* the *ideal* (the *no* *mere* *fact*), and the *reply* *grounds* the *justification* in the *process* (the *deliberation*). The *reply* is *defensible* (the *no* *mere* *agreement*), and *contested* (the *no* *substantive* *reason*).

**(ii) The *no* *neutrality* *objection***. The *public* *reason* *is* the *secular* (the *no* *religious* *reason*), and the *neutrality* *is* the *contested* (the *no* *mere* *form*), and the *reply* *fails*.

**The *reply*** (the *contemporary* *literature*; the *Rawls* *proviso*). The *religious* *reason* *can* *enter* the *public* *reason* (the *proviso*, the *no* *mere* *exclusion*), and the *reply* *grounds* the *inclusion* in the *translation*. The *reply* is *defensible* (the *proviso* *insight*).

### 1.7 The state of the method debate

**The *assessment***. The *top*-*down* *captures* the *coherence* (the *no* *ad* *hoc*), the *casuistry* *captures* the *context* (the *no* *mere* *abstraction*), the *specification* *integrates* the *two* (the *no* *mere* *derivation*), the *principlism* *captures* the *pluralism* (the *no* *mere* *monism*), and the *public* *reason* *captures* the *legitimacy* (the *no* *mere* *authority*).

| The *model* | The *strength* | The *weakness* |
|---|---|---|
| **Top-down** | The *coherence* | The *no* *algorithm* |
| **Casuistry** | The *context* | The *no* *principle* |
| **Specification** | The *integration* | The *no* *stability* |
| **Principlism** | The *pluralism* | The *no* *system* |
| **Public reason** | The *legitimacy* | The *no* *consensus* |

**The *most* *defensible* *position***. A *qualified* *wide*-*equilibrium* *method*: the *verdict* *is* the *outcome* of the *specification* (the *Richardson* *insight*), *constrained* by the *principles* (the *Beauchamp*-*Childress* *insight*), *informed* by the *paradigm* *cases* (the *casuist* *insight*), *justified* by the *public* *reason* (the *Rawls* *insight*), and *tested* by the *evidence* (the *empirical* *insight*), *qualified* by the *admission* that the *method* *guarantees* *no* *determinacy* (the *no* *algorithm*), and that the *disagreement* *remains* (the *no* *consensus*).

## 2. Bioethics

### 2.1 Autonomy and informed consent

**The *thesis*** (Beauchamp & Childress 2019, ch. 4; Faden & Beauchamp, *A History and Theory of Informed Consent*, 1986, ch. 1; the *contemporary* *literature*; the *no* *mere* *permission*). The *autonomy* *requires* the *informed* *consent* (the *no* *mere* *assent*), and the *consent* *requires* the *competence*, the *disclosure*, the *understanding*, the *voluntariness*, and the *authorization* (the *no* *mere* *signature*), and the *consent* *protects* the *bodily* *integrity* (the *no* *mere* *formality*), and the *failure* is the *battery* (the *no* *mere* *negligence*).

**The *structure***.

$$
\text{Valid consent} = \text{competence} \wedge \text{disclosure} \wedge \text{understanding} \wedge \text{voluntariness} \wedge \text{authorization}
$$

**The *argument***.

$$
\begin{array}{ll}
1. & \text{The *person* *is* the *sovereign* over the *body*} \ (\text{the *no* *mere* *paternalism*}). \\
2. & \text{The *sovereignty* *requires* the *consent*} \ (\text{the *no* *mere* *assent*}). \\
3. & \text{The *consent* *requires* the *conditions*} \ (\text{the *no* *mere* *signature*}). \\
\hline
4. & \therefore \text{The *informed* *consent* *grounds* the *intervention*} \ (\text{the *Beauchamp* *insight*}), \ \text{and the *failure* is the *wrong*} \ (\text{the *no* *mere} \ \text{formality*}), \ \text{and the *paternalism* is the *presumption*} \ (\text{the *no* *mere} \ \text{care*}).}
\end{array}
$$

**The *virtue***. The *account* *captures* the *dignity* (the *no* *mere* *compliance*), and it *fits* the *clinical* *practice* (the *no* *mere* *ritual*), and it *grounds* the *trust* (the *no* *mere* *contract*), and it *connects* the *bioethics* to the *autonomy* (Ch. 47, §4.3) and to the *agency* (Ch. 27).

**The *objections***.
